# Derived Source Manifest

**171 files inside `01 Sources/Course Videos/` are AI-generated summaries, not evidence.**

They sit next to the raw transcripts they were generated from. Nothing in their text says
so, which is the problem this file solves.

> [!warning] What this means in practice
> **A factual claim — a number, a quotation, a student result, a price — must be verified
> against the raw `.txt`, never against the `.md` summary.** The summary is a navigation
> aid. It paraphrases, compresses, and occasionally structures things the speaker didn't.
>
> The summaries are useful. They are just not proof.

> [!info] Why a manifest instead of frontmatter on each file
> Constitution Rule 3: `01 Sources/` is immutable. Adding a `status: Derived` line to 171
> source files would be 171 edits to the evidentiary record — a worse violation than the
> problem it fixes. This file lets lint and the claim register tell derived from raw
> without touching a single source. **The physical move to `09 Derived/` happens after
> the Legacy Content Crosswalk is built, since the crosswalk is built from these files.**
