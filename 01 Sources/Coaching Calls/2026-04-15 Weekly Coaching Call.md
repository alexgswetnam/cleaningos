## Troy's Frames — attributed, not adopted

Named here so they are findable. **These are Troy's material, not CleaningOS IP.** None
has been promoted to `03 Concepts/`; see the reasoning in Conflicts below.

- **Be → Do → Have**, inverting the usual do-have-be. Identity precedes action precedes
  results.
- **The Funnel of Specificity.** Moving language from vague to specific. "ER words" —
  more, less, better, faster, fewer — as the signal that you are being vague.
  *"A plan lives on your calendar. An idea, a dream, it's just out there."*
- **WOLF values** — Wonder, Ownership, Love, Freedom. Wonder as "hyper suspicious of
  certainty."
- **The two challenges.** Every leader vastly underestimates their own potential, and
  every leader vastly underestimates their team's potential.
- **The 10/10 exercise.** Rate yourself 1–10 as a leader; imagine waking up a 10; what do
  you start, what do you stop, what are the results. Then: *that 10 is actually a 2 —
  what's the new 10?*
- **Full participation.** The value of a session is produced by the participant, not the
  presenter.
- **Reality vs. fantasy land.** Deliberately operating "a little delusional" as generative
  space.
