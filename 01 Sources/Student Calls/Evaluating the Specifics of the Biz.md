# Evaluating the Specifics of the Biz

Two members walk a real acquisition target's CIM/PowerPoint slide by slide: reading
add-backs and SDE critically, spotting a financial anomaly (flat revenue, jumping SDE),
assessing the risk of a no-written-contract, relationship-only commercial book, planning
which operational systems to implement post-close, and choosing seller financing over an
SBA loan. Includes a detailed cautionary story about a non-compete failing in arbitration.
