1. Program and Investment
You are enrolling in the House Cleaning Skool 1:1 Coaching Package (the "Program"). The
investment is $6,000.00, paid through Klarna over 12 months, plus CRM access at the
discounted rate of $147.00 per month for as long as you keep the CRM active.
Klarna is a separate payment/financing provider. Your payment arrangement with Klarna does
not change the terms of this Agreement or the Program access terms below.
2. What Is Included
• Lifetime access to the highest tier of the House Cleaning Skool for as long as the Skool and
program exist. Translation: the plan is for Skool to live forever, but if the internet gets hit by a
meteor, we are not liable for the meteor.
• Access to all SOPs, templates, training materials, scripts, systems, and resources made
available inside the Program.
• At least 60 minutes of 1:1 time weekly during the active coaching period, plus direct access
to message Alex through your business Discord.
• Hands-on support to help set up and optimize the marketing system needed to profitably
acquire house cleaning clients, including the practical pieces required to get leads, book jobs,
follow up, and track performance.
• A VA sourcing and training process: we will personally help source and choose a VA
alongside the head trainer, then put that VA through an 8-week bootcamp at no additional
cost to you other than your responsibility to pay the VA.
3. Discord Access and Response Expectations
Alex will be inside your business Discord and you may message him there. Normal response
expectation is within 24 business hours, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays,
emergencies, travel weirdness, or the occasional human moment. That said, Alex is not going to

fall off the face of the planet; he has his phone on him like every other semi-functioning adult in
2026.
Discord access is for business coaching, execution questions, strategy, feedback, and support.
It is not unlimited emergency consulting, legal advice, tax advice, HR advice, or therapy.
4. VA Sourcing and 8-Week Bootcamp
We guarantee we will help source and place a strong, qualified VA candidate into the bootcamp
process. Internally we may call this a "killer VA," but for legal purposes that means a VA we
believe is capable, trainable, and a good fit based on the information available during sourcing
and screening.
The 8-week bootcamp is for your VA, although you are welcome to join. Bootcamp details may
be described on a separate page, onboarding document, schedule, SOP, or training outline. We
may update the format, curriculum, trainer, cadence, or delivery method as long as the purpose
remains training the VA for the relevant house cleaning business operations role.
You are responsible for paying the VA directly and for all hiring, firing, employment classification,
payroll, tax, legal, HR, and compliance obligations connected to that VA. The VA is not our
employee, agent, contractor, or representative unless separately agreed in writing.
5. Marketing Setup and Optimization
We will help you build, set up, and optimize the marketing system needed to acquire house
cleaning clients profitably. This may include, depending on your business needs, offer creation,
ads, landing pages, CRM setup, automations, lead follow-up, call scripts, booking flows,
tracking, review systems, and related client acquisition assets.
You are responsible for third-party costs, including but not limited to ad spend, domains,
websites, software, phone numbers, call/text/email usage, payment processing, contractors,
designers, virtual assistants, or other tools needed to run the business.
6. The Guarantee
Our guarantee is simple: we will help you add $10,000 per month in revenue to your house
cleaning business and build a system that allows you to maintain that added revenue and
continue growing while working less than 10 hours per week within 3 months. If that does not
happen, we will keep working with you for free until it does, subject to the requirements below.
This is a revenue and systems guarantee, not a promise of net profit, personal income,
valuation, exact margins, specific ad metrics, or a perfectly smooth ride. Business is still
business. We bring the system, coaching, marketing setup, VA training, and pressure. You bring
implementation, ad spend, and not ghosting the process.
7. Requirements to Claim the Guarantee
To qualify for the guarantee, you must act in good faith and materially complete the
implementation requirements we assign. This includes, at minimum:
• Be willing and able to spend money on advertising and other reasonable client acquisition
costs. The exact amount may vary by market and strategy, but $0 in ad spend is not a serious
attempt at a paid marketing system.
• Attend scheduled coaching calls or provide reasonable notice if rescheduling is needed.

• Implement assigned actions, systems, offers, follow-up, hiring steps, and marketing changes
in a timely manner.
• Provide access to the business information needed for us to diagnose and optimize the
system, including lead data, ad data, booking data, revenue data, and relevant CRM
information.
• Track revenue and lead sources consistently so the added revenue can be measured. Alex
will help with this.
• Maintain professional communication with us, your VA, your clients, and any relevant team
members.
• Not materially change the business, pause execution, stop taking clients, shut down ads,
fire the VA without replacement, ignore the CRM, or disappear and then ask why the business
isn’t growing as fast as expected..
If you do not meet these requirements, the guarantee may be paused, extended, or voided at
our reasonable discretion.
8. CRM Access
CRM access is billed at the discounted rate of $147.00 per month. You may cancel the CRM at
any time. Canceling the machine we are using to help make you money would be flat out stupid.
Canceling CRM access may limit our ability to support, track, optimize, automate, or fulfill parts
of the Program and may affect your eligibility for the guarantee if the CRM is materially needed
for implementation.
9. No Refunds or Cancellation of Program Investment
All Program payments are final and non-refundable. There are no cancellations, chargeback
games, "I changed my mind" refunds, or partial refunds after enrollment. The remedy for
dissatisfaction is the guarantee described above: if you qualify and the guaranteed outcome has
not been reached, we keep working with you for free until it is reached.
10. Student Responsibilities
You remain the owner and operator of your business. You are responsible for business
decisions, client work, pricing, hiring, insurance, taxes, licensing, employment practices,
subcontractor relationships, customer service, operations, bookkeeping, legal compliance, and
all other obligations connected to running your company.
We may provide coaching, templates, SOPs, suggestions, examples, and systems, but you are
responsible for deciding what to implement and for confirming that your implementation is lawful
and appropriate for your specific business, state, market, team, and customers.
11. Legal, Tax, HR, Employment, and Compliance Disclaimer
We are not your lawyers, accountants, tax advisors, payroll providers, HR department,
insurance agents, or compliance officers. Nothing in the Program is legal, tax, accounting, HR,
payroll, employment, insurance, or regulatory advice. You agree to consult qualified
professionals for those matters.
You agree that we are not liable for business losses, employee or contractor issues, client
disputes, tax problems, ad account restrictions, platform changes, customer complaints,

chargebacks, wage claims, employment classification issues, regulatory matters, injuries,
property damage, or any other issue arising from your operation of the business.
12. Intellectual Property and Confidentiality
All SOPs, templates, scripts, trainings, systems, automations, documents, frameworks, videos,
and Program materials are owned by us or licensed to us. You receive a limited,
non-transferable license to use them inside your own house cleaning business.
You may not copy, resell, publish, share, distribute, teach, package, upload, sublicense, or give
away the materials to anyone outside your business. You also may not use them to create a
competing coaching program, course, community, agency, VA training company, CRM product,
or consulting offer within 2 years of signing this agreement.
13. Case Study, Testimonial, and Marketing Permission
You give us permission to use your name, business name, likeness, screenshots, messages,
calls, revenue numbers, wins, before-and-after metrics, testimonials, and results as case
studies, ads, social posts, sales materials, Skool content, emails, webinars, videos, and other
marketing. Yes, we want you to win. Also yes, we are in it for us too. That is how capitalism
works.
We may edit for length or clarity, but we will not intentionally misrepresent your results. Upon
request, we may anonymize sensitive personal information when reasonable, but the default
permission is broad because this Program is designed to create public proof.
14. Non-Solicit and Team Protection
During the Program and for 12 months after your last active participation, you agree not to
directly hire, solicit, contract with, bypass, or attempt to work around our trainers, team
members, contractors, VAs, candidates, partners, vendors, or internal resources except through
the Program or with our written permission.
15. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent allowed by law, our total liability under this Agreement is limited to the
amount actually paid by you for the Program, excluding ad spend, software, VA pay, payroll,
third-party services, or other business expenses. We are not liable for indirect, incidental,
special, consequential, punitive, or lost-profit damages.
16. Arbitration, Texas Law, and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute, claim, or
controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement will be resolved by binding arbitration in
Texas, rather than court, except for claims related to intellectual property, confidentiality,
non-solicit obligations, payment collection, or injunctive relief.
In normal English: if there is a serious dispute, we are not turning it into a dramatic courthouse
miniseries. We handle it through arbitration in Texas like adults who would rather spend their
energy building businesses. Honestly, if it comes to this, you’ve royally screwed the pooch
somehow and are not willing to put the same effort we are into making you successful.
17. Entire Agreement and Updates

This Agreement, together with any referenced onboarding page or bootcamp outline, is the
entire agreement between the parties regarding the Program and replaces prior messages,
calls, sales conversations, texts, DMs, or informal descriptions about the Program. Any material
changes must be in writing and agreed to by the parties, except that we may improve or update
delivery methods, training materials, SOPs, and Program processes over time.
