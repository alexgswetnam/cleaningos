## Known limits of the instrument

Recorded here because they bound every conclusion drawn from this data:

1. **No customer language exists in this file.** Every answer was chosen from options Alex
   wrote. Response counts show which of *his* phrasings resonated — they are not the
   customer's own words.
2. **Several options are compound.** The most-selected profit-killer option bundles three
   distinct problems (lead volume, close rate, follow-up). Its count cannot be decomposed.
3. **Opt-in sample.** People who took a growth quiz and asked for a free resource. Not a
   random sample of cleaning business owners.
4. **`Season` and `Role` columns are numeric scores**, not Season or Role names — range
   11–33 and 12–41. The scoring key is not in the export. **NEEDS ALEX.**
5. **n = 30.** Directional. A 3-response difference is noise.
