## Quick Summary
This lesson explains the basic setup for getting a local phone number and submitting an A2P approval campaign inside Harvest CRM. For DIY setup, the student should go to Settings, then Phone System, and buy a local number. For DFY setup, this is handled on the first onboarding call. The recommended A2P campaign use case is Low Volume Mixed. The approval language should explain that the business sends booking confirmations, arrival notices, service reminders, and occasional promotional offers to residential cleaning customers who opt in. The lesson also provides sample messages, an opt-in confirmation message, and consent checkbox wording for a Facebook Lead Form. The core point is that this exact structure is the best way found to reliably get A2P verified.

## Core Teaching
The setup starts by buying a local number inside Harvest CRM. From there, the student needs to submit an A2P campaign that matches the way the cleaning business actually texts customers: appointment confirmations, cleaner arrival notices, service reminders, lead follow-up, booking links, and occasional promotions.

For the campaign, use **Low Volume Mixed** as the campaign use case. The use case description should say that the business is sending booking confirmations, on-the-way arrival notices, service reminders, and occasional promotional offers to residential house-cleaning customers who opted in through a Facebook Lead Form with a required SMS opt-in checkbox.

The opt-in language matters. The form should clearly state that users agree to receive SMS updates and promotional messages, that message and data rates may apply, that consent is not a condition of purchase, and that they can reply STOP to opt out. The form also needs links to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the screenshot of the consent checkbox should be created inside Facebook Lead Forms.

## Key Principles
- Buy the local phone number inside Harvest CRM before submitting the A2P campaign.
- Use Low Volume Mixed for the campaign use case.
- Make the campaign language match what the cleaning business actually sends.
- Include HELP and STOP language in sample messages.
- Use a required SMS opt-in checkbox on the lead form.
- Make the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links full naked URLs in the opt-in text.
- Use a real screenshot of the Facebook Lead Form consent checkbox.
- For DFY setup, this can be handled during onboarding.

## Step-by-Step Process
- Go to Settings inside Harvest CRM.
- Open the Phone System area.
- Buy a local phone number.
- Start the A2P campaign approval process.
- Use Low Volume Mixed as the campaign use case.
- Copy, paste, and customize the provided A2P language to the student's business name, website, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy.
- Use sample messages that include booking confirmations, on-the-way notices, lead follow-up, booking links, and promotional messages.
- Create the SMS opt-in checkbox inside Facebook Lead Forms.
- Screenshot the consent checkbox and use that screenshot for approval.
- Submit the campaign for approval.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Skipping A2P setup and then wondering why SMS cannot work properly.
- Using vague campaign language instead of explaining the real cleaning-business SMS use case.
- Forgetting HELP and STOP language in sample messages.
- Using an opt-in form that does not require the checkbox.
- Leaving out full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links.
- Trying to use a screenshot that does not clearly show the consent checkbox.
- Not replacing placeholders like YOUR BUSINESS NAME, YOUR WEBSITE, and policy links before submitting.

## Cleaned Lesson Notes
For DIY setup, go to **Settings → Phone System** in Harvest CRM and buy a local number. If the setup is done-for-you, this will be handled on the first onboarding call.

For A2P approval, use **Low Volume Mixed** as the campaign use case. Use sample messages that match the business:

- Booking confirmation messages.
- Cleaner on-the-way messages.
- Facebook inquiry follow-up messages.
- Booking link messages.
- Occasional promotional discount messages.

Every message should include the proper opt-out language when needed, especially STOP and HELP language.

The opt-in text should include full naked URL links to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

The best screenshot for approval should be made inside Facebook Lead Forms. Create the lead form, add the required SMS opt-in checkbox, then screenshot it as it appears to users. This has been the best way found to reliably get A2P verified.
