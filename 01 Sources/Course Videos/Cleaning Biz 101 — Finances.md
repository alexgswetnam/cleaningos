# Cleaning Biz 101 — Finances

## 1. Finances: Profit First

This part of the business is the money system — the most boring part, but extremely
important. A business can be "busy" and still be broke. The "Profit First" system is used
to stay consistently profitable; the book by the same name is recommended directly as
having transformed how the business looks at finances, making sure the owner gets paid
first.

## 2. Paying Cleaners: Speed + Simplicity Wins

Paying cleaners used to be a weekly nightmare until it was kept simple: a bank that
supports Zelle (PNC Bank, whose Zelle limit was raised after a month of use) makes payouts
fast and simple. The goals: pay accurately, pay on time, keep it easy to track, reduce
back-and-forth. The Free Tracking Sheet (Free Resources module) doubles as a weekly pay
stub, showing hours worked and amount owed per cleaner.

## 3. The Money Connects to Everything

Finances aren't "separate" from the business — they connect to everything: Acquire tells
you if marketing is profitable, Convert affects cost per booking, Fulfill affects
refunds/redos and retention, Operate affects cleaner efficiency and quality. Money is just
the scoreboard for the whole system. Track it and you can improve it; ignore it and the
business stays stressed no matter how busy it is. Knowing exact numbers isn't optional.

## 4. Wave: Your Money Scoreboard (Keep It Simple)

Wave (waveapps.com) is the finance tracking tool used — no fancy reports needed. Its job
is making sure every transaction lands in a category, so at a glance the business can see
how much came in, where it went, and what was actually kept. High-level categories that
matter: labor spend, marketing spend, software spend, and what's left. Described as
super easy to use and recommended to everyone.
