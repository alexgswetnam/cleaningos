## Quick Summary
This lesson defines a leads bottleneck and places it alongside other bottlenecks like labor, logistics, and leadership. Most cleaning businesses are stuck because they have a leads bottleneck. A leads bottleneck is broken into two parts: marketing, which gets leads, and sales, which converts leads into customers or bookings. The lesson also uses different language from Business Systems 101: acquiring leads and converting leads. Not every business has a leads bottleneck, because some may have labor or logistics problems instead. The key is to identify the real bottleneck and then fix that specific problem. This Leads Fundamentals course is meant to solve the marketing or acquiring leads side of the leads problem.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that growth problems need to be diagnosed by bottleneck. The lesson mentions four bottlenecks: leads, labor, logistics, and leadership. Most cleaning businesses are stuck because of a leads bottleneck, but not all of them.

A leads bottleneck means the main reason the business is not growing is that not enough people are reaching out and saying they want a cleaning, or the leads are not being converted well enough. The leads bottleneck is split into two parts. Marketing gets the leads. Sales converts the leads into customers, clients, or bookings. Another way to say the same thing is acquiring leads and converting leads.

The lesson gives examples of other bottlenecks. Nicole has a labor bottleneck because she cannot take on more cleans without more cleaners. Jack has a logistics issue handling the leads coming in, and also a leads bottleneck because his close rate is bad.

The point is to identify the bottleneck first. Once the bottleneck is clear, the business can focus on fixing the correct problem. This Leads Fundamentals course focuses specifically on the marketing side, or the acquiring leads problem. The way the leads problem is solved may look different depending on whether the business is in survival, stability, scale, or sustain and sell, but the philosophy of marketing and sales stays the same.
## Key Principles
- There are different bottlenecks: leads, labor, logistics, and leadership.
- Most cleaning businesses are stuck because of a leads bottleneck.
- A leads bottleneck includes both marketing and sales.
- Marketing is getting or acquiring leads.
- Sales is converting leads into bookings.
- Not every business has the same bottleneck.
- Identify the bottleneck before trying to fix the business.
- The solution depends on the business season, but the philosophy stays the same.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Look at the business and identify the main reason growth is stuck.
2. Decide whether the bottleneck is leads, labor, logistics, or leadership.
3. If it is leads, separate the issue into marketing and sales.
4. Ask whether the business is not acquiring enough leads or not converting enough leads.
5. Fix the specific part of the leads bottleneck instead of treating everything as one problem.
6. Consider the current season of the business, such as survival, stability, scale, or sustain and sell, when deciding how to solve it.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Assuming every growth problem is a leads problem.
- Ignoring labor, logistics, or leadership bottlenecks.
- Combining marketing and sales instead of diagnosing which side is broken.
- Trying to solve a close-rate problem with more marketing only.
- Trying to take on more cleans when the real bottleneck is labor.
