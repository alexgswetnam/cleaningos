Core Teaching
Most cleaning businesses do not have a true lead engine. If you want to operate like a real business instead of a side hustle, you need inbound leads showing up consistently even when you are busy, tired, or not in the mood to go hunt.
There are two buckets: free marketing and paid marketing.
Free marketing includes posting in Facebook groups, asking friends for referrals, commenting, networking, word of mouth, and organic posts. Free is fine, but it is unpredictable. It depends on your time, energy, and motivation. If you miss a week or get busy, leads slow down.
Paid marketing gives control. It lets you decide that you want leads every day and that you are willing to pay for attention. Paid gives control over how many people see you, how consistent lead flow is, and how much you can scale.
The lesson’s hard truth is that if you do not have paid marketing, you do not really have a lead engine. You have a social strategy, which can help, but it cannot carry the business.
