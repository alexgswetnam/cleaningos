Okay, will a website get you leads? A website is not going to get you leads by just existing, unless you have good SEO. I mean buying backlinks and good technical SEO and people finding your website through your Google business profile and those things. So no, a website alone is not going to get you leads, but you can funnel leads to your website, build trust, and it's going to make the sale much easier and people can reach out to you from there.
