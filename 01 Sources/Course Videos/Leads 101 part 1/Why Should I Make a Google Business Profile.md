# Why Should I Make a Google Business Profile (why a cleaning business should create and build a Google Business Profile)
## Module
Why should I make a Google profile
## Course

## Best For
Cleaning business owners who have not created a Google Business Profile yet or are not prioritizing reviews and map-pack visibility.
## Problems This Solves
- Not using a free source of cleaning leads.
- Putting reviews on platforms that matter less long term.
- Waiting too long to build Google visibility.
- Not understanding how Google Maps and the Map Pack can produce leads.
- Depending only on paid lead sources.
## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that a Google Business Profile should be created because it is free and can generate free leads. People can find the business through Google Maps, reviews, and the Map Pack. The lesson says there is nothing to lose by making one. In the example given, the Google Business Profile was bringing in at least $6,000 a month in new business. The recommendation is to create it sooner rather than later so reviews can start stacking up. If there was one thing to do differently, reviews would have been collected on Google Business Profile instead of Thumbtack, Facebook, and other places.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a Google Business Profile is one of the first and most important free marketing assets for a cleaning business. It costs nothing to create and can bring in leads when people find the business on Google Maps or in the Map Pack.

The lesson emphasizes that the value compounds through reviews. The sooner the profile is created, the sooner the business can start stacking reviews. Those reviews help the profile become stronger over time.

The example given is that the Google Business Profile was producing at least $6,000 per month in new business. That makes it a major lead source, not just a nice online listing.

The hindsight lesson is important: instead of collecting reviews on Thumbtack, Facebook, and other platforms, the reviews should have been pushed to Google Business Profile earlier.
## Key Principles
- A Google Business Profile is free.
- It can generate free leads through Google Maps and the Map Pack.
- There is nothing to lose by creating one.
- Create it sooner so reviews can start compounding.
- Google reviews may be more valuable long term than reviews on other platforms.
- Google Business Profile can become a major source of new business.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Create a Google Business Profile as soon as possible.
2. Set it up so people can find the business through Google Maps and the Map Pack.
3. Start asking customers for reviews on Google.
4. Prioritize Google reviews over collecting reviews on less valuable platforms.
5. Keep building the profile so it can become a free lead source over time.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- One, it’s free.
- You’re going to get free leads.
- You have nothing to lose.
- Make it sooner rather than later so that way you can be stacking up reviews.
- If there’s one thing we could do different, we would go back and get reviews on our Google Business Profile.
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Waiting too long to make a Google Business Profile.
- Collecting reviews on Thumbtack or Facebook instead of prioritizing Google.
- Ignoring the Map Pack as a free lead source.
- Assuming free marketing assets are less important than paid advertising.
