## Quick Summary
This lesson defines USP as unique selling proposition, sometimes called a unique selling point. A USP is what makes the cleaning company unique and different from every other company. Examples include being eco-friendly, using green self-made products, having a larger team that cleans quickly, offering a satisfaction guarantee, being a Christian cleaning company, and having cleaners who are legal, background checked, insured, experienced, and English-speaking. The lesson emphasizes that a USP is not just a sales line; it should reflect what is genuinely different about the business. Without a USP, customers compare only price and may think the business is simply more expensive. The owner should know the USP, be confident in it, and communicate it in marketing, on the website, and on the phone.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a cleaning business needs a clear reason why it is different. USP means unique selling proposition, and some people also call it a unique selling point. It is the thing that makes the business stand out and helps answer why someone should choose it over another cleaning company.

A USP can take different forms. Some companies make eco-friendly, green-only, self-made cleaning products their USP. Others use a team of four or five people and clean the home in 45 minutes to an hour as their USP. Many companies do not have one and end up looking like every other cleaning company.

For this kind of scalable cleaning business, one major USP is a satisfaction guarantee. The company makes it clear that the home will be cleaned up to standard, and if something is missed or messed up, the business will fix it and make sure the clean is done right.

Another USP mentioned is being a Christian cleaning company. This is presented as a real difference that changes how decisions are made, how customers and cleaners are handled, what kind of cleaners are attracted, and what the company represents.

Other differentiators include cleaners being legal, background checked, insured, experienced, and English-speaking. These things should be made clear in marketing, on the website, and during phone calls.

Without a USP, the customer compares one price to another and concludes that one company is simply more expensive. With a strong USP, the business can explain that it is offering a different level of service.
## Key Principles
- USP means unique selling proposition.
- A USP explains what makes the company different.
- The USP should be genuine, not just a marketing line.
- A satisfaction guarantee can be a strong USP.
- Values and identity can be part of a USP when they genuinely shape the business.
- Cleaner standards can be part of the USP.
- The USP should be clear in marketing, the website, and phone sales.
- Without a USP, customers compare only price.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Define what genuinely makes the cleaning company different.
2. Identify whether the USP is based on service standards, speed, products, guarantee, values, cleaner quality, or another real difference.
3. Make the USP clear in marketing.
4. Put the USP on the website.
5. Bring up the USP on phone calls when explaining why the company is different.
6. Use the USP to show why the service is not the same as a cheaper competitor.
7. Become confident in why someone should pick you over somebody else.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- USP is unique selling proposition.
- What is different about you? What’s your X factor?
- We are going to make sure that your home is cleaned up to our standards.
- If we mess up, we’re going to solve that problem and fix it.
- We’re giving you a completely different service, a different level of service.
- You want to know your USP. You want to be confident in your USP.
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Having no USP and looking like every other cleaning company.
- Only competing on price.
- Not making the satisfaction guarantee clear.
- Treating the USP as a fake marketing point instead of a real difference.
- Failing to mention differentiators on the website or phone.
- Letting customers think the only difference is that your price is higher.
