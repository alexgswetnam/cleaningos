Quick Summary
This lesson walks through the actual order of lead sources used in the business. They started with Thumbtack and spent heavily in the first two months, around $4,000 in month one and $2,000–$3,000 in month two. Thumbtack continued for about four to six months and became a major early lead source, with total spend eventually well over $10,000 and probably closer to $20,000. After that, they worked on Google Business Profile, which required about two months of heavy research and implementation and produced strong results. Facebook Ads were tested and did not work well at the time, producing only two bookings after spending a lot. Google Ads later worked very well. They did not prioritize LSA because calls were $70–$90 in their area. The lesson ends with the reminder that Thumbtack, LSA, and other channels can perform differently depending on the business and area, so you have to test what works for you.
Core Teaching
The business started getting leads through Thumbtack. In the first month, they spent around $4,000. In the second month, they spent around $2,000–$3,000. They spent a lot early because they did not have the close and were still struggling on the labor side.
They kept using Thumbtack for about four or five months, and also continued using it for roughly the first six months. Total Thumbtack spend was well over $10,000 and probably closer to $20,000.
After Thumbtack, they focused on Google Business Profile. The speaker spent about two months heavily researching and implementing GBP. It produced great results, and the lesson says every person in the group is able to do that.
Facebook Ads were then tested and were a bust for them. They spent a bunch of money and only got two bookings. That does not mean Facebook will fail for everyone, but it did not make sense for them at that time.
Google Ads came next and worked wonderfully. LSA was not used because in their area it was $70–$90 per call. The lesson closes with the point that what works depends on the business and area, so you have to test everything and see what works best for you.

Step-by-Step Process
Start with an available lead channel, like Thumbtack, if it works in your area.
Spend enough to get real data instead of judging too quickly.
Keep using what works while you learn and test the next source.
Research and implement Google Business Profile.
Test additional channels like Facebook Ads and Google Ads.
Avoid or delay channels like LSA if the economics do not make sense in your area.
Keep testing because channel performance depends on your business and local market.
