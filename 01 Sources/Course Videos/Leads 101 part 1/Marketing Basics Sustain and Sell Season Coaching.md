Core Teaching
In the Sustain and Sell season, the owner needs to zoom out and look at trends over time. You should know when business is expected to increase and when marketing may not be as profitable.
January, February, and March are examples of tougher months. People may cancel, and marketing may not perform as well. This does not necessarily mean everything is broken; it may be part of the seasonal trend.
Marketing should be adjusted based on the season and holidays. That can include special offers and campaign changes.
Because the business is getting many leads, you also need referral programs, email marketing, and follow-up dialed in. The goal is to keep capitalizing on the leads you already have.
You need accurate numbers and a regular pulse check on whether the business is increasing or decreasing. At this stage, you should always be testing new creatives, copy, and landing pages.
