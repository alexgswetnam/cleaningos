Core Teaching
In the Scale season, you know your numbers. Marketing is an equation. You know that if you put in X amount, you get leads at a certain cost per lead, and a certain percentage of those leads turn into bookings.
The goal in this season is to optimize. You want the best cost per lead you can get while also making sure best practices are in place. That includes organic/free traffic through Google Business Profile and using channels like Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Thumbtack if they work for your business.
In Scale, you should already have tested multiple lead sources. You should know which ones work best for your business, your area, and your offer.
Moving from Scale to Sustain and Sell usually means spending more money on marketing because you already know the numbers work. It is time to bet on yourself and increase lead flow.
The warning is that spending more is easy, but handling more leads is harder. If you add another three to five leads per day, your team must follow up correctly from both the sales and systems side. Another issue is trying to force a channel to work when another channel would be better, such as trying to squeeze results out of LSA when PPC would make more sense.
Key Principles
In Scale, marketing should be an equation.
Know cost per lead by source.
Know close rate by source.
Optimize before increasing spend.
Use the channels that work for your area and offer.
Scaling spend requires sales and systems readiness.
Do not force the wrong channel to work.
More leads require better follow-up capacity.
Step-by-Step Process
Confirm you know your cost per lead from each source.
Confirm you know your close rate from each source.
Identify the lead sources that work best for your business, area, and offer.
Optimize cost per lead and best practices across those sources.
Make sure your team can handle more daily leads.
Increase marketing spend once the numbers are reliable.
Watch for bottlenecks in sales follow-up and systems.
Stop trying to force weaker channels when a stronger channel is available.
