## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a cleaning company website should not be treated like a full self-checkout lane. The website should build trust and capture the lead, while most of the real sale still happens on the phone.

Instant quotes are not preferred because they can attract price shoppers. When the first thing a prospect gets is a number, they start comparing the company like a commodity. Instant quotes also remove context. A phone call allows the company to ask the right questions, set expectations, build trust, and match the prospect to the right service. That is how higher-quality recurring clients are won.

Long forms are also discouraged. They may feel professional, but they create friction. People are busy, and if reaching out feels like homework, they may leave. The preferred form is short: name, phone, and a quick note if they want to add one. The goal of the website is not to perfectly qualify the lead. The goal is to capture the lead so follow-up and the phone call can do the real work.

The website has three jobs: build trust quickly with reviews, photos, a clear offer, and policies; make it easy to reach the business through phone and a simple form; and route leads into the CRM so follow-up is automatic.

WordPress is preferred because it gives flexibility and control for SEO, including page speed, on-page SEO, service area pages, schema, internal linking, and technical fixes. The website philosophy is simple: trust and capture happen on the website, sales happen on the phone.

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Use the website to build trust quickly with reviews, photos, a clear offer, and policies.
2. Make contacting the business easy with a phone number and simple form.
3. Keep the form short: name, phone, and optional quick note.
4. Avoid making instant quotes the main sales mechanism.
5. Avoid long forms that make the prospect work too hard.
6. Route website leads into the CRM so follow-up happens automatically.
7. Use the phone call to ask questions, build trust, set expectations, and close the sale.
8. Use WordPress if long-term SEO control is a serious priority.
