Quick Summary
This lesson explains that creating a Google Business Profile is simple and free. Google provides an easy setup process, and there are many YouTube videos showing how to walk through it. In the beginning, a student can create a service-area business and verify it by showing things like equipment, car, transportation, and nearby street. The lesson says it is ideal to get a virtual address or location where the business is actually present because having an address can help the business show up higher locally. The speaker notes that they have an office, but students do not need an actual office if there are other places in the area that enable this setup. The deeper optimization is covered in the Google Business setup and growth material.

Step-by-Step Process
Create a Google Business Profile through Google’s setup process.
Choose service-area business if you are starting without an address.
Complete verification with requested proof, such as equipment, vehicle, transportation, or nearby street.
Look for a valid address or location option in your area.
Use the Google Business setup and growth material for deeper optimization.
