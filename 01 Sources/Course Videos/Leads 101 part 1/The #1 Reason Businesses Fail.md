Quick Summary
This lesson explains that businesses usually fail because they run out of money, but the deeper reason is often that they never made their marketing profitable. A business can have startup capital, systems, cleaners, a CRM, scheduling software, and even a VA, but still fail if it cannot get and close leads profitably. Core Teaching
The surface-level reason businesses fail is that they run out of money or operating capital. They start with $20,000, a credit card, or whatever amount they have, and eventually they spend it all and cannot keep going.
But the deeper question is: why did they run out of money? In this lesson, the answer is usually that they never got profitable marketing working.
Profitable marketing means you can explain the numbers. For example, if you spend $50 for a lead and close one out of two leads, then it costs $100 to get a booking. If the average booking is $350 and half of that is profit, then the profit is $175. Subtract the $100 marketing cost and you still have $75 profit on the first clean. If 20% of those cleans turn into recurring clients, there is even more long-term profit.
If you cannot clearly lay out how and why your marketing is profitable, you are going to have an issue. This is why many cleaning business owners struggle even when they already have cleaners, systems, a CRM, a scheduling platform, and maybe a VA. They still cannot get leads profitably or close those leads profitably.
Marketing is make-or-break. If you can get leads profitably and convert them into cleans, the other problems become easier to solve. You can improve cleaners, systems, VA support, and leadership. But if the business cannot get and convert leads profitably, the business will fail.

Step-by-Step Process
Know your cost per lead.
Know your close rate.
Calculate your cost per booking.
Know your average booking value.
Estimate profit per booking.
Subtract your marketing cost from the profit.
Account for the percentage of first cleans that become recurring clients.
Decide whether the marketing is actually profitable.
