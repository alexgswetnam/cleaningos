Should someone else do your marketing for you? I vote no. The reason I vote no is because you are a business owner, you're an entrepreneur, and marketing is one of the most valuable skills you can have. And so if you're always relying on someone else to do your marketing for you, even if we did it for you, you wouldn't be learning.

You wouldn't be understanding the fundamentals and the philosophy of marketing that you need to be able to continue to grow your business and not rely on someone else and also take it into your future business things and also just your life in general, because marketing is just a great personal skill to have. So I would not recommend for someone else to do your marketing for you. Now, if someone's setting it up for you and then you're taking it over, that's a different deal.

Or if you already have the skill of marketing and you just want someone else to do it for you and you know what you're looking at, you just don't have the time or you have just the money to be able to throw at it, cool, have someone else do it for you. But if you're that person, you wouldn't even be watching this.
