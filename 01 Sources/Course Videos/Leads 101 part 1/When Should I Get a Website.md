## Problems This Solves
- Waiting too long to create a basic online presence.
- Thinking a website has to be complicated before launching.
- Not knowing whether to start simple or build for SEO from day one.
- Confusing a basic trust-building website with a full SEO-focused website.
- Feeling overwhelmed by platform choices when starting out.
## Quick Summary
This lesson recommends getting a website as soon as you are starting your cleaning business. The website does not need to be complicated at first. A basic one-page website can be enough to give people information about the business and build trust. Platforms like Booking Koala can help you get something simple up quickly. Later, when the business is growing and ready to focus on SEO and Google Business Profile optimization, it makes sense to improve the website. WordPress is recommended long term because it gives more control over SEO than platforms like Wix or Squarespace, though those platforms can still work.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a website should not be delayed just because the owner thinks it has to be perfect. A basic website is useful as soon as the business starts because it gives prospects somewhere to learn about the company and builds trust before they contact you.

At the beginning, the website can be simple. It can be a one-page website with information about the company, trust-building content, and a clear way to reach out. The goal at this stage is not to build a massive website. The goal is to look legitimate and make it easier for leads to trust the business.

As the business grows, the website can become more strategic. Once the business is ready to focus more heavily on SEO and Google Business Profile optimization, it makes sense to invest in a better website. WordPress is recommended for that stage because it gives more control over SEO. Other platforms like Wix and Squarespace can work, but they can be harder to optimize correctly.
## Key Principles
- Get a website as soon as you start the business.
- The first website does not need to be complicated.
- A simple one-page site can build enough trust early on.
- Improve the website later when SEO becomes a bigger focus.
- WordPress gives the most control for SEO long term.
- Wix and Squarespace can work, but may be harder to optimize.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Create a basic website when starting the business.
2. Keep the first version simple, even if it is only one page.
3. Include information about the company and trust-building elements.
4. Use a simple platform if you need to get online quickly.
5. When the business is growing, upgrade the website with SEO and Google Business Profile optimization in mind.
6. Consider WordPress when you are ready for more control over SEO.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Waiting until the business is bigger before creating any website.
- Overbuilding the first website instead of getting a basic trust-building page up.
- Assuming Wix, Squarespace, or similar platforms are impossible for SEO.
- Ignoring the long-term SEO advantages of WordPress.
