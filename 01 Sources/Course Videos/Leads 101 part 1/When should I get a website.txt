When should you get a website? I would recommend getting a website as soon as you are starting your business. It is not as hard as you think to get a basic website up and you can use a platform like Booking Koala and those in order to have a basic website, you don't need to build out like crazy. It can be similar to us where we have a very simple one-page website, information about us, build trust with people and then eventually once you're growing and you're ready to really focus on your SEO for Google Business Profile Optimization and those kind of things, then you can have a better website.

I recommend WordPress because WordPress you're very much able to take control over every aspect of your SEO. There are other websites though, Wix and all these other ones, Squarespace, where it's a bit more difficult to optimize correctly for your SEO, but it's very possible.
