Core Teaching
First, define “working.” In this lesson, working means profitable. Ideally, marketing is profitable on the first clean, but it may be profitable across three months, six months, or a year.
You cannot know whether marketing is working without tracking your numbers. Tracking lets you ask whether a channel is profitable. If it is not profitable, tracking also lets you diagnose whether the issue is acquiring leads or converting leads.
If the problem is leads, you need better marketing. If the problem is close rate, you need better sales.
The lesson does not give universal KPI targets because results depend on your area. It does point out that Meta leads are usually lower quality than Google Ads leads, though they may be cheaper. LSA should theoretically be higher quality, but that is not always the reality.

Decide whether the next step is better marketing or better sales.
Keep comparing channels based on both cost and quality.

Cleaned Lesson Notes
How do I know if marketing is working? First, we have to define working.
I would define working as profitable. It is ideal if marketing is profitable on the first clean, but it can also be judged across three months, six months, or a year.
The only way you know if marketing is working is by tracking your numbers. When you track, you can run the numbers and ask: is this profitable?
If it is not profitable, you need to know why. Is it not profitable because of the leads? Is it an acquiring issue? Or is it not profitable because of converting or sales?
That is extremely important because it determines your next step. Do you need better marketing, or do you need better sales?
As far as specific KPIs, it depends on your area. I cannot give one number for everyone. Meta lead quality will often be worse than Google Ads lead quality, but Meta may have a cheaper cost per lead. LSA should theoretically have higher lead quality than all of them, but that is not always the reality.
So the point is simple: track your numbers, run the numbers, and identify what parts are working and what parts are not working.
