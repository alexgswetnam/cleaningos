As far as which website platform to use, I kind of mentioned that in a past video, but I would recommend WordPress. It's possible to do Squarespace, Wix, Book&Qual, all these different ones, but WordPress gives you the most control over your SEO, which is important long-term. Now, if you're just trying to get something up and throw it up easy, then something like Squarespace and those are easier to do right from the jump, and you can improve on your website later.
