Core Teaching
Your ideal customer profile gives you a starting point for who you are speaking to in your marketing and sales.
The recommended starting archetypes are pet owners, busy professionals, and elderly or retired people who travel, have money, and do not want to clean themselves. Courtney laid these out clearly, and the speaker agrees that they are strong targets.
Each group needs different messaging. The way you speak to a pet owner is not the same as how you speak to a busy professional or retired person. The ads, marketing copy, and sales call language should change based on what that person cares about.
A key part of the sales call is asking why they are looking for a cleaning service. Are they dealing with pets? Kids? A busy schedule? Is the house a wreck or a hazard situation? You need to understand their desire so you can tailor what you say and what you sell.

Step-by-Step Process
Start with the three recommended archetypes: pet owners, busy professionals, and retired/elderly people.
Think through what each group cares about.
On calls, ask why the prospect is looking for cleaning service.
Identify whether the reason is pets, kids, busyness, a messy/hazard situation, or something else.
Tailor your words, sales angle, and marketing message to that desire.
Use the best client types as your starting place instead of trying to target everyone.
