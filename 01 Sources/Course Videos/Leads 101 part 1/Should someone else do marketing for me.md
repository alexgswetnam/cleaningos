Quick Summary
The lesson’s point of view is that someone else should not fully do your marketing for you if you do not understand marketing yet. Marketing is one of the most valuable skills a business owner can develop. If you always rely on another person, even if that person does a good job, you are not learning the fundamentals or the philosophy behind growth. The lesson makes an exception for cases where someone sets it up and then you take over. It also makes an exception for owners who already understand marketing and simply do not have the time or would rather pay for execution. But for the student watching this lesson, the recommendation is to learn marketing instead of immediately handing it off.
Core Teaching
The answer is mostly no: do not have someone else fully do your marketing for you if you have not learned the skill yourself.
You are a business owner and entrepreneur, and marketing is one of the most valuable skills you can have. If you rely on someone else for it all the time, you do not learn how marketing works. You also do not build the fundamentals and philosophy you need to keep growing this business, use the skill in future businesses, and even apply it in life in general.
There are two exceptions. First, it is different if someone sets it up for you and then you take it over. Second, it is fine if you already have marketing skill and know what you are looking at, but you do not have time or you have the money to pay someone to execute. But if that is you, the lesson points out that you probably would not be watching this video.
