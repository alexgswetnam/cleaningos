Quick Summary
This lesson defines Acquire as the lead engine of the business. It is the part that gets strangers interested enough to raise their hand. The goal is to get in front of the right people, build trust quickly, and give them a clear next step, such as calling, requesting a quote, or booking. When Acquire is strong, you know where leads come from, what a good lead looks like, and how attention turns into contact information. The core goal is predictable lead flow.
Core Teaching
Acquire is the part of the business that makes strangers become interested enough to raise their hand. It is your lead engine.
The job of Acquire is to get in front of the right people, build trust quickly, and give them a clear next step. That next step could be a call, quote request, booking link, or another way for them to contact you.
When Acquire is weak, everything else feels stressful. You are constantly trying to “find business” instead of working a predictable system.
The goal is predictable lead flow. You should know where leads come from, what a good lead looks like, and how attention turns into contact information.
