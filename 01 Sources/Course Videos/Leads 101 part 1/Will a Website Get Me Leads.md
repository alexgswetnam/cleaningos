## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that a website will not get leads just by existing. Leads come from people finding the site through SEO, backlinks, Google Business Profile, or other traffic sources. A website can help convert leads by building trust and making the sale easier. It can also be used as a place to funnel leads from other channels. The website is valuable, but it is not a magic lead engine on its own. The key is to drive traffic to it and use it to make people more comfortable reaching out.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a website is not automatically a lead source. A website sitting online with no SEO and no traffic is unlikely to produce leads by itself.

For a website to generate leads organically, people need to find it. That can happen through strong SEO, backlinks, technical SEO, Google Business Profile, or other channels that send traffic to the site.

The website still matters because it builds trust. Even if the lead comes from somewhere else, the website can make the sale easier by helping the person understand the company, trust the service, and reach out.

The right way to think about the website is that it supports lead generation and sales. It can receive leads, build trust, and help people contact the business, but it does not create leads just by existing.
## Key Principles
- A website alone does not guarantee leads.
- SEO and traffic are what allow a website to generate leads organically.
- Backlinks and technical SEO matter for people finding the site.
- Google Business Profile can help people find and visit the website.
- A website is still valuable because it builds trust.
- Leads can be funneled to the website from other channels.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Do not assume the website will generate leads just because it is live.
2. Build or improve SEO so people can find the website.
3. Use Google Business Profile and other lead channels to send traffic to the website.
4. Use the website to build trust and make reaching out easy.
5. Treat the website as part of the sales process, not the entire lead-generation strategy.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

- Ignoring technical SEO and backlinks.
- Not connecting the website to Google Business Profile.
- Treating the website as the whole marketing strategy instead of a trust-building asset.
