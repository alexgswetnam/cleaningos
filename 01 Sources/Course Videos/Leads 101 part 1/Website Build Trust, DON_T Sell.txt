Your website isn’t where sales happen... It’s where trust happens.

A lot of cleaning companies try to turn their website into a full-blown “self-checkout lane” with instant quotes, huge forms, and 20 questions before someone can even talk to you. I don’t agree with that approach for one simple reason:

Most sales still happen on the phone.

Why I don’t prefer instant quotes

Instant quotes feel efficient, but they often create two problems:





They attract price shoppers.
When the first thing you give someone is a number, you train them to compare you like a commodity.



They remove context.
A phone call lets you ask the right questions, set expectations, build trust, and match them to the right service. That’s how you win higher-quality recurring clients — not by racing to the cheapest quote.

If someone wants a number without talking, they’re usually not your best client anyway.

Why I don’t like long forms

Long forms feel “professional,” but they kill conversions. People are busy. If reaching out feels like homework, they bounce.

We prefer a short, frictionless form:





name



phone



a quick note (optional)

That’s it.

Because the goal of the website is not to perfectly qualify them!!! The goal is to capture the lead and then let your follow-up + phone call do the real work.

What the website is actually for

Your website has 3 jobs:





Build trust quickly (reviews, photos, clear offer, policies)



Make it easy to reach you (call + simple form)



Route leads into your CRM so follow-up is automatic

Why we prefer WordPress

We use WordPress because it’s the most flexible platform for SEO. If you’re serious about long-term, compounding lead flow (especially from the local map pack + organic search), you need a site you can fully optimize:





page speed



on-page SEO



service area pages



schema



internal linking



technical fixes when needed

A lot of “all-in-one” website builders are fine for a basic online presence, but they can be capped in what you can control SEO-wise. WordPress gives you control... In local services, control over your SEO turns into leads.

So our philosophy is simple:
Your website is a trust + capture machine. Sales happen on the phone.
