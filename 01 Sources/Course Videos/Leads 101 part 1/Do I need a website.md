Quick Summary
The answer in this lesson is yes, you should have a website. There are no downsides to having one, but the website by itself will not solve your marketing problem. Its main job is to create a foundation for marketing, especially Google PPC, and to help skeptical prospects learn more about the company. A website can also pre-sell people before they reach out, which makes them easier to close. The lesson says that people who find the business through Google Business Profile or organic SEO had a close rate around 70% in their example. A website is especially useful for customers who are researching a long-term cleaner and want to feel confident before choosing. So yes, you need one, but it should be understood as support for your marketing engine, not the whole engine by itself.
Core Teaching
Yes, you should have a website. There are no real downsides to having one.
But a website is not going to solve your marketing problem by itself. It lays the foundation for you to do marketing, especially Google PPC. You can technically do LSA and Meta Ads without a website, but a website is still valuable.
A website helps when people are skeptical on the phone and want to know more about your company. You can send them to the website. It also helps pre-sell prospects. When people find you through your website, Google Business Profile, or organic SEO, they have already done some research and are often easier to close.
The lesson gives an example that the close rate for people who found the business through Google Business Profile or organic SEO was around 70%. It is especially useful for people who are looking for a long-term cleaner and want to research before choosing.
