In the stability season, you likely already have one or two marketing channels that are bringing you leads. Now, how profitable those channels are is likely the area that you need to improve. But the point of moving from the stability season to the scale season means having a reliable marketing channel, a reliable process to get leads at a cost per lead that makes sense, and make sure that they're good quality leads and that you aren't capped on how much you can spend. There's some platforms, for instance, Thumbtack, that depending on your area, you might not be able to spend even $50 a day because there's just not enough lead flow in the area. So you want to make sure that you have, in the stability season, you already have a lead channel or two lead channels that are working, but you want to optimize those lead channels, get the cost per lead lower, get the quality of the lead higher, and then to move to the scale season, you are going to be tracking your numbers, tracking your cost per lead, tracking your close rate, making sure that the leads are coming in profitably, optimize it so that they come in more, you just have more profit because it's working better, and then at scale, to get to scale, you want to know that marketing is an equation. 

Know that when I spend X amount, I get this many leads, and of those many leads, this amount turns into bookings. It should feel like an equation. Marketing should not feel like a guess game. 

If you're in the stability season, it probably still feels like a guessing game. In the scale season, to get to the scale season, it cannot feel like a guessing game. You need to know your marketing equation.
