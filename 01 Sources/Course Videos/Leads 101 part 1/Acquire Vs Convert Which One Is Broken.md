Quick Summary
This lesson explains that you only know whether Acquire or Convert is broken by looking at your numbers. The key numbers are cost per lead, close rate, and cost per acquisition. The example used is Jack, who had $40 cost per lead from Google Ads, which was fine, but he was only closing 15–20% of those leads. He thought the leads were the problem and considered testing Facebook or Meta Ads, but the numbers showed that he had a converting problem, not a getting-leads problem. If you have no leads coming in, then you likely have an Acquire problem. If leads are coming in at a reasonable cost but are not turning into bookings, you likely have a Convert problem.
Core Teaching
You cannot diagnose your bottleneck by guessing. You diagnose it by tracking your numbers.
The numbers that matter are cost per lead, close rate, and cost per acquisition. Once you know those, you can tell whether the issue is getting leads or converting leads into bookings.
The lesson uses Jack as the example. Jack was getting $40 cost per lead from Google Ads, which was good. But he was only closing about 15–20% of those leads. He thought he had a lead problem and wanted to maybe try Facebook or Meta Ads. But because the numbers were visible, the diagnosis was clear: he did not have a lead problem. He had a conversion problem.
If you are getting leads at a reasonable cost but the close rate is low, focus on Convert. If you do not have leads coming in at all, then focus on Acquire.
Key Principles
You only know what is broken by tracking numbers.
Cost per lead tells you about Acquire.
Close rate tells you about Convert.
Cost per acquisition shows the total outcome.
A low close rate can make good leads look bad.
Do not switch lead sources before diagnosing the bottleneck.
More leads will not fix a broken sales process.
Step-by-Step Process
Track cost per lead.
Track close rate.
Track cost per acquisition or cost per booking.
If leads are not coming in, diagnose an Acquire problem.
If leads are coming in at a good cost but not booking, diagnose a Convert problem.
Fix the actual bottleneck instead of jumping to a new channel.
