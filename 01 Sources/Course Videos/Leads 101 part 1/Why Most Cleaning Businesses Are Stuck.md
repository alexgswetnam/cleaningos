## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that most cleaning business owners are not confident or happy with their marketing or sales. The main reason many businesses are stuck is a leads bottleneck. That can mean they are not getting enough leads, the leads do not make sense money-wise, or they are not closing enough of them. The lesson warns against focusing on a bunch of smaller problems while a major leads problem is staring the owner in the face. The advice is to fix the leads and sales problem first because it is make-or-break. Other smaller problems can be sorted after that.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that most cleaning businesses are stuck because of marketing and sales, not because of a collection of tiny problems. After many conversations with cleaning business owners, the repeated pattern is that they are not happy with their marketing, not confident in their marketing, not happy with sales, or not confident in sales.

When a business has a leads bottleneck, the owner should not distract themselves with a bunch of smaller issues. The big problem is usually obvious: not enough leads, leads that do not make financial sense, or not enough closes. That problem needs to be fixed first.

The lesson frames the leads problem as make-or-break. Everything else can be sorted later, but if the business cannot bring in and close leads at a number that makes sense, it will stay stuck.
## Key Principles
- Most stuck cleaning businesses have a leads bottleneck.
- Marketing and sales confidence matter for growth.
- Do not solve small problems while ignoring the big lead problem.
- The issue may be lead volume, lead economics, or close rate.
- Fixing leads and sales comes before many other operational improvements.
- The leads bottleneck is make-or-break.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Look honestly at whether the business is getting enough leads.
2. Check whether the leads make sense financially.
3. Check whether enough leads are being closed into bookings.
4. If any of those are weak, treat the leads bottleneck as the main problem.
5. Do not get distracted by smaller issues until the main leads and sales problem is being fixed.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- Every single one is not happy with, not confident with their marketing, not confident or happy with their sales.
- Don’t go and try to solve a bunch of other small problems when you have a big, fat problem staring you in the face.
- I’m not getting enough leads, or the leads aren’t making sense money-wise, and I’m not closing enough of these.
- Fix this problem first.
- This one, it’s make or break it.
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Working on small problems while ignoring the lack of leads.
- Assuming the business is stuck for complicated reasons when the leads problem is obvious.
- Not separating marketing problems from sales problems.
- Accepting poor lead quality or poor close rate without treating it as urgent.
