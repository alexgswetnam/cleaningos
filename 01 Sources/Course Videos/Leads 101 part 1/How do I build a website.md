Quick Summary
This lesson recommends building a website by finding a template and adapting it to your business. The speaker does not recommend coding from scratch or starting with a blank template. Students can use the speaker’s website as a reference and change the colors, logo, name, locations, and business-specific details. The exact build process depends on the platform, but the overall strategy is to start from something that already works. If the student is technically challenged, the recommendation is to hire someone on Fiverr and give them a reference website to model. The lesson also warns that technical SEO needs to be done well and checked using the videos in the course.
Core Teaching
Do not code your website from scratch. Do not start with a blank template.
Find a template that already works and appropriate it for your business. That means adapting the structure, copy, colors, logo, name, locations, and relevant details so it fits your cleaning business.
The speaker says students can use their site as a reference and take many things from it. There may also be a service or resource where students can copy and paste the website and use a how-to guide for SEO and optimization.
If building the site yourself is too technical, hire someone on Fiverr. But do not just ask them to build something from scratch. Give them a reference website and make sure they build based on that. Also make sure the technical SEO is done well.
