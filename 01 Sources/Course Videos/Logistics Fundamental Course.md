# Logistics Fundamental Course

21 lessons on running the Logistics Engine day to day: what a logistics bottleneck is,
why systems and SOPs matter, what good logistics accomplishes, the CRM adoption decision
(what a CRM is, when to get one), the systems stack and its cost (what to use, starting
vs. scaling, how much to pay), SOP craft (what an SOP is, sample SOPs, keeping them
short), one BookingKoala setup tip (teams vs. individuals), and a six-lesson block on
hiring, paying, scheduling, and firing a VA.

This is one of the four courses `00 Inbox/ingestion-queue.md` flagged as never confirmed
ingested. Much of it sits directly on top of existing Logistics Engine material —
[[SOPs]], [[BookingKoala]], [[Harvest CRM]], [[When To Hire A VA]], [[Managing A VA]] —
and updates those pages rather than duplicating them. Three lessons were genuinely new
decisions with no existing page: how to *pick* a VA (vetting/testing, distinct from
*when* to hire one), when to *fire* a VA (distinct from ongoing management), and the
recurring cost-benefit-analysis heuristic Alex names explicitly across three lessons
(CRM, VA, general systems spending).
