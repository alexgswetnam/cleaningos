# Leads 101 Part 2

22 lessons on Convert-stage sales mechanics: script use and training, objection handling
(a general agree-then-probe pattern, plus the specific "keep pushing" persistence
principle), call review and roleplay practice, confidence and tonality, the CRM sales
pipeline (two lessons restating the same six-stage structure), speed-to-lead, response-rate
cadence, close-rate diagnosis by channel, converting one-time clients to recurring,
reactivating cold leads, qualifying out bad-fit clients, and three Logistics-adjacent
payment lessons (processor choice, accepted methods, the 48-hour card hold).

**This source overlaps heavily with material already in the vault**, most of it via two
other course sources already ingested: `Cleaning Biz 101 — Convert` and
`Objection Handling Course`. In several cases the overlap isn't just thematic — it's the
same lesson under the same or a near-identical title, with matching verbatim phrases
(`Cleaning Biz 101 — Convert`'s "Speed-To-Lead: The First 5 Minutes" lesson and this
course's identically-named lesson both contain "Call them ASAP!!!" and "WITHIN SECONDS";
`Objection Handling Course`'s "Securing Recurring Cleanings" and this course's lesson of
the same name are the same worked call, same June 1 reschedule date, same reviewer
quotes). This reads as Alex's content being repackaged into more than one course bundle
on Skool rather than independently recorded material — most updates below are
corroboration (adding this source to an existing page's `sources:` list) rather than new
claims.
