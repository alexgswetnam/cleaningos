## Quick Summary

This lesson explains how the speaker determines cleaner pay using a variable percentage model. Instead of guaranteeing a fixed percentage, compensation varies by job type and margin. The speaker focuses on what cleaners effectively earn over time rather than a single percentage number. The lesson also explains why owners should avoid getting stuck on percentages and instead ensure cleaners earn a reasonable amount while keeping the business healthy.

## Core Teaching

Cleaner compensation should support both the cleaner and the business. Pay percentages vary based on job type, pricing, and margin. The focus should be on overall earnings rather than a fixed percentage.

## Key Principles

* Do not guarantee hourly pay for 1099 cleaners.
* Keep business margins healthy.
* Use variable percentages.
* Focus on overall earnings.
* Charge clients appropriately.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Price the cleaning job.
2. Determine an appropriate cleaner percentage.
3. Evaluate effective earnings over time.
4. Communicate expected earning ranges.
5. Monitor business profitability.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* “Don't get hung up on a percentage.”
* “My goal is to make sure you're making at least X amount.”
* “Our cleaners are making about this much.”

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Overpaying percentages while undercharging clients.
* Promising fixed percentages.
* Focusing only on percentages instead of earnings.
* Ignoring business margins.
