## Quick Summary

This lesson explains the importance of tracking cleaner performance at scale. The speaker records complaints, re-cleans, cleaner assignments, and patterns of mistakes. This data helps identify strengths, weaknesses, and whether a cleaner is a good fit for certain job types.

## Core Teaching

Track performance data so decisions are based on patterns rather than memory.

## Key Principles

* Track complaints.
* Track re-cleans.
* Identify patterns.
* Match cleaners to strengths.
* Remove poor performers quickly.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Log complaints and re-cleans.
2. Record who completed the job.
3. Identify patterns.
4. Update cleaner records.
5. Adjust assignments or remove cleaners when necessary.
