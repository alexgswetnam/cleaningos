## Problems This Solves
- Not knowing whether to use Zelle, ACH, Gusto, or another method.
- Paying cleaners slowly and frustrating them.
- Not having a weekly pay rhythm.
- Running into Zelle limits with a new bank account.
- Not accounting for delayed Stripe/payment processor deposits.
## Quick Summary
This lesson explains the business’s cleaner payment logistics. The company uses Zelle because it is easy for the business and cleaner, and cleaners like getting paid quickly instead of waiting for ACH. The business pays weekly on Friday for the work from that Friday back to the previous Saturday. The speaker notes that this can create some difficulty because money from Stripe or the payment platform may be delayed by a couple of days. Paying twice a month or once a month is possible, but weekly is what they do because cleaners like it. The lesson recommends using a bank that allows Zelle, noting that initial limits may be lower but can increase with use. PNC is the bank they use, and Chase and American Express are also mentioned as possible options.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that cleaner pay should be simple, reliable, and attractive to cleaners. The business uses Zelle because it is fast and easy. Cleaners like quick payment, and weekly pay helps maintain a good working relationship.

The payment rhythm is Friday payroll covering that Friday back to the previous Saturday. The owner should be aware that payment processor deposits, such as Stripe, may lag behind the cleaning revenue, so cash flow has to be managed.

Other systems like Gusto or ACH can work, and paying once or twice monthly is possible. But in this business, Zelle and weekly pay are the preferred logistics. The owner should choose a bank that supports Zelle and be aware that sending limits may start lower before increasing over time.
## Key Principles
- Pay should be predictable.
- Cleaners like getting paid quickly.
- Zelle is used because it is easy for both sides.
- Weekly pay can create cash-flow timing issues if processor deposits are delayed.
- Different pay frequencies are possible, but this business prefers weekly.
- Choose a bank that supports the payment method you want.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Choose the payment method, such as Zelle.
2. Use a bank that supports the method and has workable limits.
3. Set a clear pay schedule.
4. Pay weekly on Friday for the prior Saturday through Friday period if using this model.
5. Plan for delayed payment processor deposits.
6. Track what each cleaner is owed before sending payments.
7. Adjust only if another pay rhythm makes more sense for your business.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Ignoring cash flow delays from Stripe or the payment platform.
- Using a bank without enough Zelle capacity.
- Not having a clear pay schedule.
- Assuming ACH or monthly pay is always best for cleaner relationships.
- Failing to track cleaner pay before sending money.
