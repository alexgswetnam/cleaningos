## Quick Summary
This short lesson is a simple checklist of what the business needs from cleaners. The items listed are W-9, subcontractor agreement, background check approval, photo ID front and back, and insurance. It does not go into a long process, but it gives the basic documents and approvals that should be collected. The purpose is to keep cleaner onboarding organized and avoid starting work without the core paperwork. This lesson pairs well with the longer lesson on what documents to give cleaners. It is best used as a quick reference checklist.
## Core Teaching
The teaching is straightforward: before working with a cleaner, collect the basic documents and approvals. The list is W-9, subcontractor agreement, background check approval, photo ID front and back, and insurance.

This is not a full onboarding lesson. It is the quick minimum checklist the bot should point people to when they need to know what to request from a cleaner.
## Key Principles
- Do not onboard cleaners without basic paperwork.
- Keep the cleaner document checklist simple.
- Collect both tax/contract documents and identity/insurance documents.
- Background check approval should be part of the process.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Request a W-9.
2. Send and collect the subcontractor agreement.
3. Collect background check approval.
4. Collect photo ID, front and back.
5. Collect proof of insurance.
6. Store everything in the cleaner’s file before assigning work.
