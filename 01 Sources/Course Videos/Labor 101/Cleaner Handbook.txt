Type of Clean Guide
Reference cleaning checklist for detailed view of what we do/donʼt clean in each 
type.
Standard Clean
This type of cleaning is ideal for regular upkeep and is recommended weekly, bi
weekly, or monthly to prevent the buildup of dirt and maintain a fresh home. 
We can do standard cleans on homes up to a 5/10 on the dirtiness scale.
10
Cleaner HandBook
Ideally, their home has to be cleaned within the last 6 months.
Upkeep Clean
This is a ‘specialtyʼ clean. This is for clients who really only want a small amount 
of work done in their home. For example, “only the kitchen, living room, and one 
bathroomˮ, or “I want them to come and clean what they can within 2 hoursˮ.
These cleanings should be a 13/10 on the dirtiness scale.
Deep Clean
This is ideal for when your home needs a more intensive cleaning, such as before 
hosting guests, during seasonal changes, or simply if itʼs been a long time since 
the last cleaning.
Deep cleans are required on homes that are a 6/10 or greater on the dirtiness 
scale.
Recommended if itʼs been more than 6 months since their last cleaning, even if 
they rate it lower on the scale.
Move In Clean
This is for homes that have already been cleaned previously, and the client would 
like a once over and sanitization of everything before they move in. The home 
should be nearly empty. Typically, itʼll be a home that was on the market, cleaned 
for photos and showings, but the owner just wants one final cleaning before they 
move in. 
These cleanings should be a 13/10 on the dirtiness scale.
Move Out Clean
This is for homes that havenʼt been cleaned previously, and the client needs a 
deep clean to get it ready for showings or get their deposit back. The home must 
be empty. If the home isnʼt empty, it should be a deep clean.
These cleanings can be anywhere on the dirtiness scale.

Fridge Cleaning
Fridge interior cleaning involves wiping down and sanitizing the inside surfaces of 
the refrigerator after the client has (mostly) emptied its contents. The cleaner 
should:
Remove any remaining small items or easily movable containers.
Wipe all interior shelves, drawers, and side compartments with an all-purpose 
cleaner or mild disinfectant.
Dry surfaces to prevent streaks or residue.
Return any items moved during the cleaning to their original place.
Note: Cleaners are not responsible for disposing of expired food or organizing 
contents; clients should ensure the fridge is mostly emptied before service.
Oven Cleaning
Oven cleaning focuses on removing baked-on grease, food residue, and buildup 
from the interior of the oven. The cleaner should:
Upon arrival, spray the oven interior thoroughly with a recommended oven 
cleaner (e.g., Easy-Off), unless the client has pre-sprayed the night before.
Allow the cleaner to sit and break down grime for the duration of the service, 
leaving the oven as one of the final tasks completed.
At the end of the clean, wipe out the interior surfaces using damp cloths or 
paper towels until residue is removed.
Rinse and wipe again to ensure no cleaning product remains.
Wipe down the oven door (interior glass and edges).
Note: Clients may choose to pre-spray the oven in advance for deeper results. 
Cleaners should confirm at the start of the service whether this has been done.
Window Cleaning
Interior window cleaning includes the full treatment of the window area inside the 
home. The cleaner should:

Cleaner HandBook
Clean the glass panes with appropriate glass cleaner, leaving them streak
free.
Wipe and clear the window tracks of dust, dirt, and buildup. You do not have 
to open all the windows to clean the tracks, just clean what you can see as the 
window is.
Dust and wipe down the blinds to remove surface dust and debris.
Note: This service covers interior surfaces only; we do not do exterior window 
cleaning.
Baseboard Cleaning
Baseboard cleaning involves hand-wiping all baseboards throughout the home to 
remove dust, dirt, and buildup. The cleaner should:
Use a damp cloth or mild cleaner to hand-wipe each section of baseboard.
Pay attention to corners, edges, and behind doors where dirt collects.
Dry or buff as needed to prevent streaks or moisture spots.
