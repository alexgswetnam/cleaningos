## Quick Summary

This lesson provides an overview of what it means to work as a 1099 contractor. It explains tax responsibilities, required forms, quarterly estimated taxes, self-employment taxes, and the importance of tracking expenses. The lesson also references tools such as Keeper and Bonsai Tax.

## Core Teaching

1099 contractors are responsible for handling their own taxes and business records.

## Key Principles

* Track income and expenses.
* Set aside money for taxes.
* Make estimated tax payments.
* Maintain records.
* Understand contractor responsibilities.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Complete a W-9.
2. Track income and expenses.
3. Set aside money for taxes.
4. Make quarterly payments.
5. Collect your 1099-NEC.
6. File required tax forms.
