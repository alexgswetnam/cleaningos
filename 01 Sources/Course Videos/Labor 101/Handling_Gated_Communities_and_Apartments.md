## Quick Summary

This lesson explains how to collect and document access instructions for gated communities and apartment complexes. The process starts by asking the client for entry details and then confirming the best route with the cleaner after the job. These notes become useful for future visits and future cleaners.

## Core Teaching

Collect detailed access information from clients and verify it with cleaners so future visits are easier.

## Key Principles

* Ask early.
* Document everything.
* Confirm details after the clean.
* Create reusable notes.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Ask if the property has a gate or access restrictions.
2. Gather detailed instructions.
3. Add notes to the job record.
4. Confirm the best route with the cleaner.
5. Save notes for future visits.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* “Is there a gate?”
* “Is this an apartment complex?”
* “What's the easiest way to get there?”

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Failing to document access instructions.
* Relying on memory.
* Not confirming access details with cleaners.
