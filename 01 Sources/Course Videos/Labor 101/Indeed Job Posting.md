## Key Principles
- The job post should clearly describe the role and area served.
- Experience matters; the post requires at least two years of professional cleaning experience.
- Professionalism and integrity are part of the job, not optional extras.
- Applicants should know the physical requirements.
- Insurance, background check, and driver’s license expectations should be upfront.
- The post should mention schedule, pay, tips, and travel expectations.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Use the job post as a starting template.
2. Update the company name and service area.
3. Keep the emphasis on experienced residential cleaning.
4. List duties clearly.
5. Include requirements like experience, English, insurance, driver’s license, background check, and physical ability.
6. Clarify job type, pay range, schedule, benefits, tips, commute, and travel expectations.
7. Post it on Indeed or a similar hiring platform.

Here at The Faithful Cleaners, we’re looking for a meticulous and experienced Cleaning Technician to join our team here in the McKinney, Prosper, Frisco areas. In this role, you will be responsible for professionally and efficiently cleaning our clients’ homes.. The ideal candidate will have a thorough knowledge of proper cleaning chemical usage and effective cleaning techniques. As a Cleaning Technician, your primary duties will include sanitizing, disinfecting, dusting, sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping all designated areas of the client's property.

To excel in this role, you must be able to work independently, follow instructions precisely, and conduct yourself with integrity and professionalism as a representative of our company. You must be capable of performing all assigned tasks and handling physical labor ranging from light to strenuous. You must be super responsible, take accountability, and take pride in your work.
