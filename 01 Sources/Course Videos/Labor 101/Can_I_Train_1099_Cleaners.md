## Quick Summary

This lesson explains that 1099 cleaners should not be trained like employees. The speaker emphasizes that subcontractors should already know how to perform the work. Owners can define expected outcomes, but they should not provide employee-style training that may create compliance issues.

## Core Teaching

Hire experienced cleaners who already know how to clean rather than trying to train 1099 contractors.

## Key Principles

* Do not train 1099 contractors.
* Define outcomes instead of methods.
* Hire experienced cleaners.
* Stay compliant.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Recruit experienced cleaners.
2. Explain expected outcomes.
3. Evaluate performance.
4. Continue using cleaners who meet standards.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
