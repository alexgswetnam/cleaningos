## Quick Summary

This lesson explains how to evaluate potential locations for a Google Business Profile. The speaker recommends starting with coworking spaces and office suites, talking directly with owners, and making sure the location can support verification requirements. Competition, review counts, SEO strength, population, and median income are all considered before making a decision. The goal is to select a location where the business can realistically compete and gain visibility.

## Core Teaching

Location selection should be based on available office options, competitor strength, market opportunity, and the ability to get properly verified.

## Key Principles

* Explore coworking spaces and office suites.
* Evaluate nearby competitors.
* Consider review counts.
* Consider population and median income.
* Strong SEO can help outperform competitors.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Research coworking spaces and office suites.
2. Contact or visit potential locations.
3. Evaluate local competitors.
4. Review competitor review counts.
5. Assess SEO opportunities.
6. Check population and median income.
7. Choose the most favorable location.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* "What's the median income?"
* "What's the population?"

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Choosing a location without checking competition.
* Ignoring population and income data.
* Assuming review count is the only ranking factor.
