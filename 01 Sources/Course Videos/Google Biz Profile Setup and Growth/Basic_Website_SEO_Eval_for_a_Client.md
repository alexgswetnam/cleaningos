## Quick Summary

This lesson reviews a cleaning company's website and identifies foundational SEO issues. The speaker focuses on title tags, meta descriptions, keyword targeting, and heading structure. The recommendation is to optimize pages around what customers search for rather than company names alone. Proper H1, H2, and H3 structure is also emphasized.

## Core Teaching

Website SEO should clearly communicate what the business does and where it operates by using search-focused titles, descriptions, and content structure.

## Key Principles

* Optimize for search intent.
* Use one H1 per page.
* Structure content logically.
* Focus on searched keywords.
* Support GBP rankings with website SEO.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Review the title tag.
2. Review the meta description.
3. Identify target keywords.
4. Fix heading structure.
5. Align content with search intent.
6. Reassess performance.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* "What are people actually searching for?"
* "There can only be one H1."

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Using multiple H1 tags.
* Optimizing for the company name instead of search terms.
* Overly long descriptions.
* Poor page structure.
