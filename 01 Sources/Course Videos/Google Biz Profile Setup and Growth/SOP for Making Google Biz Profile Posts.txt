🧾 Google Business Profile (GBP) Posting SOP

Unified & Updated Version

Effective Date: Nov. 2025

Version: 2.0

Prepared By: Elle

Approved By: Alex / Elijah

Department: Marketing



🎯 1. Purpose

To maintain active, high-quality, SEO-optimized Google Business Profile (GBP) posts for The Faithful Cleaners, ensuring:



Strong local ranking

Consistent branding

High conversion from "Call now"

Proper use of real job photos

Compliance with offer/discount and content rules

This SOP combines marketing standards (content creation, posting structure) with sales/fulfillment standards (photo sourcing, QA, and rules on offers, phone numbers, and cross-posting).



👥 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:



Marketing Assistants / Admin / VA-Sales:

– Copywriting, post creation, scheduling, design (if needed), compliance.

VA-Fulfillment / Team Lead:

– Providing real job photos, exporting from BookingKoala, quality checks.

Managers / Owners:

– Approvals (if required), performance review, corrections.

This applies to all GBP locations handled by The Faithful Cleaners.



📆 3. Posting Cadence



3 posts per GBP weekly → Mon / Wed / Fri

80% Updates (services, before/after, tips, testimonials, team)

20% Offers (must follow discount rules)

Additionally:



Photo uploads: 3–5 new images weekly

Event/Promo posts: As needed, following rules



🧪 4. Post Types Allowed

A. What's New / Update (default)

Highlight a service, benefit, recent job, cleaning transformation, local SEO angle, or educational tip.

B. Offer Posts

Allowed but limited. Rules:



Discount must not exceed 15%

Short validity dates

No price shown in text

No offer graphics in the image

CTA remains the same: Tap "Call now" to book



🚫 5. Non-Negotiable Rules

These apply to all posts:



No phone number in post text. Ever.



Rely only on Call now button.

Real job photos only.



Never use stock images.

Never reuse the same exact photo across different GBPs.

No graphic-designed offer images.



No text baked into images.

Photos must look naturally taken from a real cleaning job.

Brand-safe images only:



No faces, family photos, personal items, documents, or sensitive information.



📸 6. Photo Guidelines

Acceptable Photos



Before & after shots

Cleaned rooms (kitchen, bathroom, common areas)

Detail shots (fixtures, appliances)

Team in action (faces avoided)

Quantity / Quality



Use 1–3 images per post, preferably 2

Bright, sharp, clean composition

Avoid blurry, dark, or cluttered photos

Filename Format

<city>_<keyword>_<thefaithfulcleaners>_<####>.jpg



🔽 6a. BookingKoala → Photo Export Steps



Open BookingKoala → Jobs.

Filter by City/Zip of the target GBP location.

Select 3–5 jobs with strong visuals.

Download 4–6 total images.

Rename the following filename format.

Store here:

Google Drive > Marketing > GBP > <Location> > YYYY > MM > raw/selected



📖 6b. Photo Uniqueness Log

Maintain a shared sheet titled "GBP Photo Usage Log."

Columns:

Date | GBP Location | File Name | BookingKoala Job ID | Room/Area | VA | Notes



Rule: Before scheduling, search the Sheet.

If a photo is used by another GBP → choose another image.



✍️ 7. Copywriting Rules



Include City + one local Neighborhood/Landmark early in text

Highlight one service only (e.g., Deep Clean, Move-Out Clean)

Tone: friendly, confident, helpful

Avoid hype, long emojis, excessive punctuation

0–1 emoji max

CTA must be: Tap "Call now" to book.

Post Length: 650–900 characters (if following the prompt) or 100–200 words max for general posts

No shortened URLs unless approved

No phone number in body text

Brand name "The Faithful Cleaners" may appear once

Offer Copy Rules



Max 15% discount

Must include start & end dates

Include one simple condition (e.g., "new clients only")

No prices in text

No emojis (optional guideline but safer for offer posts)



🧱 8. Post Structure (All Posts)



Title / Hook



Short and engaging ("Breathe easier with a fresh home!")

Body Text (2–3 sentences)



Describe a benefit, service, or job

Add local keyword naturally ("house cleaning in {City} near {Landmark}")

CTA



Tap "Call now" to book.

Hashtags (optional, 3–5 max)

Examples:



#HouseCleaning #DeepCleaning #CleanHomeHappyHome #TheFaithfulCleaners

Image(s)



Real job photos

Branded if needed (small logo watermark only)



💬 9. ChatGPT Prompt (Unified Exact Version)

Use this exact prompt when generating GBP copy:

You are writing a Google Business Profile post for a home cleaning company. Follow these rules STRICTLY:
- Return exactly 3 options labeled Option 1/2/3.
- Each option must be 650–900 characters.
- Do NOT include a phone number, email, or any URL.
- CTA must be a single, short line: "Tap **Call now** to book."
- Work in local SEO naturally using these variables: {City}, {Neighborhood_or_Landmark}.
- Emphasize exactly one service: {ServiceName} (e.g., Deep Clean, Move-Out Clean, Standard Maintenance, Short-Term Rental Turnover).
- Use friendly, professional tone; avoid hype.
- For Offer posts only (if `{IsOffer}=true`): discount must be ≤ 15%, include start `{OfferStartDate}` and end `{OfferEndDate}` dates, and a one-line condition like "new clients only." Do NOT embed prices.
- If `{IsOffer}=false`, do NOT mention any discount.
- Never ask to text/call a number; rely on the Call now button only.
- Avoid emojis except 0–1 optional tasteful emoji.
- Output in plain text.

Inputs:
- City: {City}
- Neighborhood_or_Landmark: {Neighborhood_or_Landmark}
- ServiceName: {ServiceName}
- IsOffer: {true|false}
- OfferDiscount: {0–15}% (ignored unless IsOffer=true)
- OfferStartDate: {YYYY-MM-DD}
- OfferEndDate: {YYYY-MM-DD}

Now produce 3 options that follow every rule above. Make the options distinct in angle (benefit, problem-solution, social proof), but consistent in tone.



📋 10. Approval Process



Create draft in Google Docs or Canva Content Calendar.

Attach photos + filename list.

Manager reviews (if required).

Once approved:



Upload to GBP

Add used photo info to the GBP Photo Usage Log

Double-check post formatting before publishing.



🔎 11. Quality Control Checklist

Before posting, confirm:

☐ No grammar or spelling errors

☐ Correct location + local SEO used

☐ CTA correct

☐ Photo(s) follow uniqueness + quality rules

☐ Discount (if any) ≤ 15%

☐ No phone number in text

☐ Real photos only (no stock, no graphics)

☐ Filename format correct

☐ Posted to correct GBP location
