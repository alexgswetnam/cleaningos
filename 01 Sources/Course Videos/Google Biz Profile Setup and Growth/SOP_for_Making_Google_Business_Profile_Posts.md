## Quick Summary

This lesson is a complete SOP for publishing Google Business Profile posts. It covers posting frequency, approved content types, offer limitations, photo requirements, copywriting rules, approval workflows, quality control, and performance review. Particular emphasis is placed on using real job photos, avoiding phone numbers in post text, and maintaining consistency across locations.

## Core Teaching

GBP posting should be systemized, consistent, SEO-aware, and based on real job photos rather than stock content.

## Key Principles

* Post consistently.
* Use real job photos.
* Never include phone numbers in post text.
* Use local SEO naturally.
* Track photo usage.
* Maintain quality control.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Gather approved photos.
2. Verify photo uniqueness.
3. Create post copy.
4. Review compliance requirements.
5. Obtain approval if required.
6. Publish the post.
7. Log photo usage.
8. Review performance monthly.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* "Google will get mad at you."
* "Tap Call now to book."
* "Real job photos only."

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Using stock photos.
* Including phone numbers in post text.
* Reusing identical photos across GBPs.
* Using offer graphics that violate guidelines.
* Publishing without quality review.
