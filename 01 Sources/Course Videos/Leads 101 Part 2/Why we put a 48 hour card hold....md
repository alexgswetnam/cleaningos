## Problems This Solves
- Cleaning a home and then finding out the client’s card has no money on it.
- Confusing a card hold with an actual charge when explaining payment policies to clients.
- Getting ghosted by a client after a failed payment hold.
- Not having a way to catch card issues before the cleaner shows up.
- Creating unnecessary payment risk by waiting until after the cleaning to test the card.
## Quick Summary
This lesson explains why we put a 48-hour hold on the customer’s card before the clean. The hold is done through Booking Koala, and it helps us identify card issues before they become a bigger problem. If the hold fails, we can contact the client and fix the payment issue before sending cleaners to the home. A hold is not the same thing as a charge, so we are very clear with customers that they are not actually charged until after the cleaning is completed and everything goes well. This protects the business from cleaning first and then discovering there is no valid payment method. Legit clients will usually fix the card, transfer funds, or provide another card. The point is simple: verify that the funds are there before the clean happens.
## Core Teaching
We put a 48-hour card hold before the clean because it protects the business. The hold lets us identify payment problems before cleaners go out and do the work. We have had situations where a card hold did not go through because there was no money on the card. When that happens, we can reach out and say, “Hey, the hold did not go through, so we need to fix that before the clean.”

That is much better than cleaning the home first, trying to charge afterward, and then realizing there is nothing there. At that point, you have already done the work, the cleaner still needs to get paid, and the client may ghost you.

The important distinction is that a hold is not a charge. Some clients may say, “Hey, I got charged.” The response is to clearly explain that it is only a hold and the actual charge will not happen until after the cleaning, once everything goes well and they are satisfied. If needed, the hold can be canceled.

A legit client who has a real card issue will usually fix it. They might give you a different card or transfer funds into the account. The hold simply verifies that the payment method is real and that the funds are available before the service happens.
## Key Principles
- A card hold protects the business before the clean happens.
- A hold is not the same thing as a charge.
- Be very clear on the phone that the client will not be charged until after the cleaning.
- Failed holds reveal payment problems before cleaners waste time.
- Legit clients usually fix payment issues when they are real.
- Waiting until after the clean to test the card creates unnecessary risk.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Collect the client’s card through the booking or scheduling platform.
2. Put a hold on the card 48 hours before the cleaning.
3. If the hold goes through, keep the clean on schedule.
4. If the hold fails, contact the client before the clean.
5. Ask them to fix the issue, transfer funds, or provide another card.
6. Make it clear that the hold is not a charge.
7. Charge the client only after the cleaning is completed and everything goes well.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- “There’s a 48-hour hold before the clean.”
- “The hold is for us to identify any card issues before they come up.”
- “A hold isn’t a charge.”
- “We put a hold, but we will not charge you until after the cleaning and everything goes perfectly well and you’re satisfied.”
- “Hey, the hold didn’t go through, so we need to fix that before the clean.”
- “Let me give you a different card.”
- “Let me transfer funds to the account.”
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not clean first and only then find out the card is bad.
- Do not explain the hold in a way that makes the client think they were charged early.
- Do not skip the hold just because the card is already on file.
- Do not send cleaners out when the payment issue has not been fixed.
- Do not assume a client will pay after the clean if the hold already failed.
