## Core Teaching
Acquire is how people raise their hand. Convert is how those raised hands become booked jobs. This is where a lot of money is made or lost.

Most cleaning businesses do not lose deals because the cleaning is bad. They lose deals because the lead response is slow, follow-up does not happen, the sales process is unclear, or booking feels harder than it should. The goal is simple: no lead gets lost.

Every lead needs four things: a source, a status, a next action, and a follow-up plan. If those are missing, you cannot trust that the lead is actually being worked.

Close rate changes the real cost of your leads. If leads cost $40 and you close 10%, that is $400 per booking. At 30%, it is $133 per booking. At 60%, it is $66 per booking. So the same marketing can feel terrible or amazing depending on how well you convert.
## Key Principles
- Acquire creates leads; Convert creates bookings.
- No lead should get lost.
- Every lead needs a source, status, next action, and follow-up plan.
- Close rate changes the real cost per booking.
- Weak Convert makes marketing feel expensive.
- Sales training matters because most cleaning business owners are not naturally good at sales.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Make sure every lead enters the system with a source.
2. Assign every lead a clear status.
3. Define the next action for every lead.
4. Create a follow-up plan so nobody slips through.
5. Track close rate and cost per booking.
6. Train on sales calls and objections to increase conversion.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not blame marketing before checking whether leads are being converted properly.
- Do not let leads sit without a next action.
- Do not assume a lead is dead just because they did not book immediately.
- Do not ignore sales training if objections are killing your close rate.
