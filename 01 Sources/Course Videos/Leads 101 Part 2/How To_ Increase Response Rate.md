## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that increasing response rate is a numbers game. Many people do not answer unknown numbers after one call, so one attempt is not enough. You should call, double dial, text, leave voicemails, and follow up across multiple days. Harvest CRM helps by sending automatic texts immediately and creating tasks for follow-up. When working the pipeline, you should double dial people twice a day, especially right after they reach out. After quoting someone, you do not just let them go; you keep following up until you get a definitive yes or no. If the answer is not now, they can move into long-term nurturing instead of being left in limbo.
## Core Teaching
Response rate improves when you follow up more. It is a numbers game. One call is usually not enough because many people do not answer unknown numbers. If it is important, you call again, text, leave a voicemail, and try again later.

When a lead reaches out, call quickly and call more than once. In the pipeline, double dial people twice a day, especially right after they reach out. Use automatic texts and tasks through the CRM so the follow-up starts immediately and does not depend on memory.

After you quote someone, the process is not over. Keep following up with texts and calls the next day and two days later. You want a definitive yes or no. Do not leave people in the gray area of “maybe they will get back to me.” If they are not ready, move them into long-term nurture.
## Key Principles
- Response rate is a numbers game.
- One call is not enough.
- Double dialing increases the chance of contact.
- Follow-up should happen quickly and repeatedly.
- Quoted leads still need follow-up.
- You need a definitive yes or no.
- Long-term nurture is better than leaving leads in limbo.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Call the lead immediately when they reach out.
2. Double dial if they do not answer.
3. Send a text and leave a voicemail where appropriate.
4. Keep calling and texting over multiple days.
5. After quoting, follow up the next day and again after that.
6. Keep working the lead until you get a yes or no.
7. If they are not ready, move them into long-term follow-up.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not assume no answer means no interest.
- Do not stop after one call or one text.
- Do not let quoted leads sit without follow-up.
- Do not keep people in a vague maybe stage forever.
- Do not rely on memory instead of CRM tasks and automations.
