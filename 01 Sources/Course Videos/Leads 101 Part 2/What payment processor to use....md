## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that the payment processor is not something to overcomplicate early on. We use Stripe because it works well, we have used it in other businesses, and it connects with Booking Koala. Booking Koala keeps the card on file and the actual charging happens through Stripe. Square, Stripe, and other processors are mostly going to have similar fees, so compatibility with your scheduling platform matters most. When the business is larger and a lot more money is moving through the processor, you can negotiate lower fees. Even saving 1% becomes meaningful at scale, because at $100,000 in volume that is $1,000 kept in the business. The main point is to pick the processor that works with your system and do not make it a huge decision before it needs to be.
## Core Teaching
We use Stripe. It connects with Booking Koala, which is our scheduling platform, so when we charge someone in Booking Koala, that payment is processed through Stripe. Booking Koala keeps the card on file, and Stripe handles the actual payment side. There are other options like Square, and there are all sorts of processors, but this is not a decision that should slow you down.

The biggest practical question is: does your payment processor work with the scheduling platform you use? If it does, and the fees are normal, that is usually good enough.

At scale, the conversation changes a little. Stripe is around a standard percentage fee plus a small transaction fee, and once you have a lot of money moving through, you may be able to negotiate that rate down. A 1% difference does not sound like much, but at $100K in payments, that is $1,000. Early on, though, the processor itself is not a big deal. Use what works with your system.
## Key Principles
- The processor should fit the scheduling platform.
- Do not overcomplicate payment processing early.
- Stripe works well with Booking Koala in our setup.
- Most processors have similar fees when you are small.
- At higher volume, negotiate fees because small percentages become real money.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Choose the scheduling platform you are going to use.
2. Check which processors integrate with that platform.
3. Pick a processor that allows card-on-file and clean charging.
4. Use the processor consistently so payments are simple to manage.
5. When volume grows, talk to the processor about lowering your rate.
