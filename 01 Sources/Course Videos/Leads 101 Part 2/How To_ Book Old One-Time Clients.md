## Quick Summary
This lesson recommends using a credit and automatic follow-up to book old one-time clients. In Harvest CRM, one-time clients can be placed into an automatic text sequence if they do not become recurring within a set window. The sequence can remind them that their credit is about to expire, ask if they want available spots, and create urgency before they lose the credit. Without a CRM, this becomes hard because the owner has to track new leads, cleaners, cleans, old leads, and past one-time clients manually. A CRM keeps the sales pipeline organized and lets automations handle repetitive work. The point is not just convenience; it gives the business owner time back and keeps past clients from being forgotten.
## Core Teaching
The best way to book old one-time clients is to have an automated follow-up system. Offer a credit after their first cleaning, and if they do not become recurring within a certain amount of time, trigger a sequence that reminds them the credit is about to expire.

In Harvest CRM, this is built in. If someone only books one time, they can go into an automatic text sequence. After 60 days, if they have not become recurring, they get texts like: your credit is about to expire, we have spots available, and are you sure you do not want to book before you lose the credit.

Without a CRM, this gets tough. You are already tracking new leads, cleaners, scheduled cleans, old leads that never booked, and one-time clients who have not rebooked. Automations let repetitive follow-up happen without you carrying it all in your head.
## Key Principles
- Past one-time clients are not dead opportunities.
- Credits create a reason to rebook.
- Follow-up should happen automatically where possible.
- A CRM keeps one-time clients from slipping through the cracks.
- Automation gives the owner time back.
- Recurring conversion should not depend on memory.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Identify clients who booked only one time.
2. Create or assign a follow-up sequence for one-time clients.
3. Offer a credit that expires after a certain period.
4. Send reminder texts before the credit expires.
5. Mention available spots and invite them to book.
6. When they respond, move quickly to schedule the cleaning.
7. If they do not respond, keep them in long-term nurture.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- “Hey, your credit is about to expire in three days.”
- “Hey, we have spots available. Do you want to know what we have?”
- “You’re about to lose the credit. Are you sure you don’t want to book?”
- “Let AI, let automations do the work for you that is repetitive.”
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not rely on memory to follow up with old one-time clients.
- Do not treat one-time clients as finished after the first clean.
- Do not manually manage every repetitive follow-up if a CRM can do it.
- Without a CRM, tracking this becomes difficult as the business grows.
