## Quick Summary
This lesson teaches that sales should feel like a normal human conversation, not a weird salesperson performance. Many people get awkward because they are uncomfortable with sales and slip into a salesy voice. The acronym MAFHAF means Make A Friend, Help A Friend. First, connect with the person like a human: ask about the area, kids, rooms, or normal life details. Then help the friend by offering a real solution through the cleaning service, guarantee, insured cleaners, and adjusted options if price is an issue. The mindset is that you and the customer are on the same side. This is especially helpful for people with higher EQ who feel uncomfortable selling.
## Core Teaching
Stop making sales awkward. You do not need a weird sales voice. You do not need to become a fake salesperson. You want the call to feel like a normal human conversation.

The framework mentioned is MAFHAF: Make A Friend, Help A Friend. First, make a friend. Connect with the person. Ask normal questions. Be personable. Then help the friend. You are offering them a good solution, not trying to force something on them.

When price comes up, stay on their side. If it is too expensive, help them think through options. Maybe there are rooms they do not use. Maybe there is a way to customize. You are not against them. You are helping a friend find the right cleaning solution.
## Key Principles
- Sales should feel like a human conversation.
- A salesy voice makes calls weird.
- Make a friend, then help a friend.
- Connection comes before the offer.
- You and the prospect should feel like you are on the same side.
- Helping is a better mindset than pressuring.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Drop the salesy voice.
2. Start the call like a normal human conversation.
3. Make a friend by asking real, personable questions.
4. Transition into helping them with the cleaning service.
5. Explain the value clearly: guarantee, insured cleaners, background checks, and being taken care of.
6. If they object, stay helpful and look for a solution.
7. Keep the call natural instead of performative.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- “MAFHAF: Make a friend, help a friend.”
- “How long have you been in the area?”
- “Any kids that are growing up?”
- “We have a satisfaction guarantee, so you’re all taken care of.”
- “Is there any room that you don’t use?”
- “We’re on the same side here.”
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not use a fake sales voice.
- Do not make the conversation awkward for the buyer.
- Do not treat selling like pressure.
- Do not avoid the offer just because sales feels uncomfortable.
- Do not forget to help after you connect.
