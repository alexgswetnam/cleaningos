## Core Teaching
The fastest way to increase bookings without spending another dollar on ads is to respond faster. In home services, the first company to respond often wins.

When a lead hits your website or calls your phone and you do not respond quickly, they usually either book someone else or cool off. Once they cool off, they are harder to close.

This is why CRM automation matters. Even if you cannot personally answer instantly, your CRM can send an immediate text, send a missed call text, send an email confirmation, and create a task so someone follows up. But even without a CRM, call new leads ASAP. Respond within seconds.
## Key Principles
- Speed-to-lead increases bookings.
- The first company to respond often wins.
- Slow response makes leads book elsewhere or cool off.
- CRM automation protects speed when you cannot answer manually.
- Speed-to-lead increases close rate without increasing ad spend.
- Waiting too long to get a CRM can cost real money.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Respond to every new lead within seconds if possible.
2. Call the lead ASAP.
3. If they called and you missed it, send a missed call text.
4. Use CRM automation for immediate auto-texts and email confirmations.
5. Create tasks and reminders so follow-up actually happens.
6. Do not wait to improve speed-to-lead until after you spend more on ads.
