You need the 6 stages below in your CRM. 



It's the core of what makes Harvest CRM our favorite choice, and why it's better than anything else we've personally seen.



New Lead

The EXACT MOMENT someone reaches out to your business, they are automatically put into the New Lead stage.

Goal: Instant contact + don’t let them slip.

What should happen automatically:





Lead has a marketing source tagged (PPC / Thumbtack / GBP / LSA / Website / etc.)



Immediate auto text goes out: “Hey this is ___ with ___. Saw you reached out for cleaning - when is a good time to call you?"



Immediate email (optional) confirming you got their request + what happens next



Missed call text (if they called and didn’t reach you)

What should happen manually (same day):





Call them ASAP (speed-to-lead is everything)



If they don't respond, you should double dial twice a day until you get in touch - or until they respond to your automatic texts over the next week.

Once you finally get in touch with someone, they should move to the next appropriate stage.



Follow-Up

Goal: Turn “no response yet” into a conversation.

Who goes here:





Leads who said “call me at xyz time"



Leads who you are actively texting, but haven't gotten on the phone to quote yet.

What should happen automatically:





Reminders/tasks for your team to call

What should happen manually:





Wait to call them until the time they have asked (or follow an automatic task system built into your CRM like Harvest CRM has)



Once you get them on the phone: you will either quote them, book them, or move them to Long Term Follow Up if timing isn’t right



Quoted

Goal: Close the deal (most bookings happen here with good follow-up).

Who goes here:





Anyone you gave pricing to (phone, text, email, or in-person)



Anyone you sent a booking link to with a price attached

What should happen automatically:





Reminders/tasks for your team to call/text

What should happen manually:





If they’re hesitating: ask why (timing, price, trust, decision maker)



Offer the simplest next step: “I can get you booked in 2 minutes”



Follow up with them every 1-2 days, get them booked. You are NOT bothering them! You need a yes or no, so call/text often to get that answer.

Exit criteria:





If they book once → Booked - One Time (This should happen automatically in BookingKoala, or your pricing/scheduling software which is integrated into Harvest CRM)



If they start recurring → Booked - Recurring (This should happen automatically in BookingKoala, or your pricing/scheduling software which is integrated into Harvest CRM)



If they say not now → Long Term Follow Up



If they are a bad fit for you → mark them as DNC (Do Not Contact)



Booked - One Time

Goal: Perfect first experience + convert to recurring later.

What should happen automatically:





Confirmation email/text with expectations + policy reminders (We use Harvest CRM for this)



5 days out reminder (We use BookingKoala for this)



24 hours out reminder (We use BookingKoala for this)



“On the way” text (We use BookingKoala for this)



Added to a "One Time Clean Follow-Up"  Auto-Text/Email automation that follows up with them 2-3 months later with store credit that will expire if they don't use it. Harvest CRM has this auto-built in.

What should happen manually:





Make sure job notes are clear for the cleaner!

Next step:





Assign an available cleaner



Booked - Recurring

Goal: Keep them long-term (this is where the business becomes stable).

What should happen automatically:





Confirmation email/text with expectations + policy reminders (We use Harvest CRM for this)



5 days out reminder (We use BookingKoala for this)



24 hours out reminder (We use BookingKoala for this)



“On the way” text (We use BookingKoala for this)

What should happen manually:





Track preferences (pets, priorities, areas of focus)



Handle issues quickly (retention lives or dies here)



Periodic quality check / manager touch (even if short)

Next step:





Assign an available cleaner



Long Term Follow Up

Goal: Turn old leads and past clients into future bookings automatically.

Who goes here:





Leads who said “not right now”



Leads who ghosted after quote/follow-up



Past one-time clients who didn’t rebook



Past recurring clients who paused/canceled (optional)

What should happen automatically:





Long-term reactivation cadence:





30 days: “Still need help?”



60–90 days: “Want to get back on the schedule?”



6 months: seasonal check-in



12 months: annual check-in



These should feel human, not spammy

What should happen manually (when they respond):





Move them back to Quoted or New Lead depending on situation



Get them on the phone quickly and book!
