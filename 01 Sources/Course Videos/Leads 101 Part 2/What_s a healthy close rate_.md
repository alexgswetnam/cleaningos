## Problems This Solves
- Comparing close rates without considering the lead source.
- Not knowing whether a low close rate is a marketing problem or a sales problem.

- Needing to diagnose whether pricing, lead quality, or the salesperson is hurting bookings.
## Quick Summary
A healthy close rate depends on the lead source. Google Business Profile and organic SEO leads can close very high, sometimes around 60–70% or even higher. Meta ads may have a much lower close rate, sometimes around 10%, because the intent and quality can be different. PPC for us has been around 40–45%, but the right number still depends on your area, your spend, and your offer. The real definition of healthy is whether the numbers are making money. If the close rate is not healthy, the fix may be better lead quality, a better salesperson, better pricing, or better call handling. The best way to diagnose it is by looking at the numbers and listening to the sales calls.
## Core Teaching
Do not judge close rate without looking at the lead source. A Google Business Profile lead is not the same as a Meta ads lead. For us, Google Business Profile and organic SEO have closed around 60–70% or higher at times. Meta could be closer to 10%. PPC has been around 40–45% for us. Those are not universal numbers; they depend on your area, your spend, and the quality of the leads.

The better definition of healthy is: are you making money? If the close rate is high enough that the channel is profitable, that can be healthy. If you want to improve beyond that, then you are optimizing.

When the close rate is bad, it points to something that needs to be diagnosed. It could be lead quality. It could be the salesperson. It could be pricing. You figure that out by listening to calls and actually walking through the numbers instead of guessing.
## Key Principles
- Close rate depends on lead source.
- Healthy means the channel is making money.
- Optimal means there is still room to improve.
- Bad close rate can mean lead quality, sales, or pricing is off.
- You diagnose close rate by listening to calls and reviewing the numbers.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Break close rate down by lead source.
2. Compare each channel based on the quality and intent of its leads.
3. Look at whether the channel is profitable, not just the close-rate percentage.
4. If close rate is low, listen to calls to see whether sales is the issue.
5. Review pricing and lead quality before blaming the platform.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not compare Meta leads to Google Business Profile leads as if they are the same.
- Do not assume a low close rate automatically means marketing is bad.
- Do not ignore pricing or salesperson quality when diagnosing close rate.
- Do not optimize blindly without listening to the calls.
