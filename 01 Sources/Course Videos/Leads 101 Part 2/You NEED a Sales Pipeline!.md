## Core Teaching
If you are relying on memory, a notes app, or random texts to track leads, Convert will always feel chaotic. A pipeline gives you a simple visual answer to two questions: where is this lead right now, and what should happen next?

A simple cleaning pipeline looks like this: New Lead, Follow-Up, Quoted, Booked - One Time, Booked - Recurring, and Long Term Follow Up. Those stages help you organize the lead based on what has happened and what needs to happen next.

But the magic is not just having the stages. The magic is the clarity and discipline that come from keeping the pipeline accurate. When your pipeline is accurate, you stop forgetting people. You stop double texting randomly. You always know what to do next.

It also shows you bottlenecks. If you have tons of quotes sent but very few bookings, that is probably a follow-up problem. If you want to scale to more than 100 bookings in a month without chaos, you need a CRM and a pipeline that tracks the current status of every lead.

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Stop relying on memory, notes apps, or random texts to track leads.
2. Create simple pipeline stages for your cleaning business.
3. Use stages like New Lead, Follow-Up, Quoted, Booked - One Time, Booked - Recurring, and Long Term Follow Up.
4. Put every lead into the correct stage.
5. Keep the pipeline accurate and updated.
6. Use the pipeline to know the next action for each lead.
7. Review the pipeline to spot bottlenecks, especially where quotes are not turning into bookings.
8. Use a CRM so the lead status is tracked clearly as you grow.
