If you’re relying on memory, notes app, or random texts to track leads, Convert will always feel chaotic.



A pipeline is just a visual way to answer:





Where is this lead at right now?



What should happen next?



A simple cleaning pipeline looks like:





New Lead



Follow-Up



Quoted



Booked - One Time



Booked - Recurring



Long Term Follow Up



The magic isn’t the stages... It’s the clarity + discipline.



When your pipeline is accurate:





you stop forgetting people



you stop “double texting” leads randomly or whenever you remember to



you always know what to do next



you can see bottlenecks instantly (ex: tons of quotes sent but no bookings = follow-up problem)



you can scale (like we have) to more than 100 bookings in a month without any chaos



Just about every serious business that has a sales process like ours uses a CRM that tracks the current status of a lead.
