## Core Teaching
Old cold leads should not disappear. If someone did not book after you called and texted them, put them into a long-term follow-up stage. That way they continue hearing from you monthly or every three months instead of being forgotten.

The follow-up can be simple: ask if they want to book, mention a special discount, mention a credit, or let them know spots are available. If the calendar has openings, go back through the cold list and call people. A person who did not book before might book now.

The key is segmentation. Know who did not book, who booked once, who became recurring, and who is in long-term follow-up. When the holiday season or an opening comes up, you can call the right list and turn old opportunities into bookings.
## Key Principles
- Cold leads are not worthless.
- Not booking now does not mean never booking.
- Long-term follow-up should be automated where possible.
- Calling old leads still works.
- Segmenting leads makes reactivation possible.
- Calendar openings can be filled from old lead lists.
- Specials, credits, and availability give people a reason to respond.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Identify leads who did not book or ghosted.
2. Move them into a long-term follow-up stage.
3. Send automated texts or emails monthly or every three months.
4. Use specials, credits, or open spots as reasons to reach out.
5. When the calendar has openings, call the cold list manually.
6. Move responding leads back into the active pipeline.
7. Book them quickly when they re-engage.
## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
- “Hey, would you like to book a cleaning?”
- “We have this special discount going on.”
- “We have this credit you can use.”
- “We’ve got spots available.”
- “If somebody didn’t book right then, it doesn’t mean that they won’t book in the future.”
## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not forget old cold leads.
- Do not assume a ghosted lead is dead forever.
- Do not mix all contacts together without segmentation.
- Do not rely only on new leads when old leads could fill openings.
- Calling cold leads works better when you have enough leads in the system and can sell.
