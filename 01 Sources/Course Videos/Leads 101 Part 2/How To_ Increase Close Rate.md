## Core Teaching
You increase close rate by practicing, but practice only helps if you are practicing the right form. If you practice a bad golf swing over and over, you get better at a bad golf swing. Sales is the same. Bad sales practice reinforces bad sales habits.

First, get the right fundamentals. Then practice them repeatedly. That means getting comfortable at the beginning of the call, presenting the offer, presenting pricing, handling objections, asking for the sale, and scheduling the client.

In sales, this kind of practice is roleplay. Roleplay different objections. Try different phrases. Test how they sound. Do not try something once and decide it does not work. Sometimes the concept is right, but you need more reps.

Also pay attention to tonality, pausing, upward inflection, downward inflection, exact words, and confidence. Confidence sells, but confidence comes through repetition and knowing why you are saying what you are saying.

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Learn or define the right sales fundamentals.
2. Practice the beginning of the call.
3. Practice presenting the offer clearly.
4. Practice saying the price without getting weird.
5. Roleplay common objections repeatedly.
6. Practice asking for the sale and scheduling.
7. Review tonality, pauses, upward inflection, downward inflection, and confidence.
8. Keep repeating the right form until it becomes comfortable.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not practice bad fundamentals repeatedly.
- Do not assume one failed attempt means the method is wrong.
- Do not ignore tonality, pausing, or inflection.
- Do not get down on yourself; treat sales as a skill.
- Do not skip roleplay if you want close rate to improve.
