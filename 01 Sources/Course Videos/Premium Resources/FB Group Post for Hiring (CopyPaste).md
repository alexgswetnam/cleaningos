## Quick Summary
This resource is a copy/paste Facebook group hiring post for The Faithful Cleaners. It positions the role as a 1099 independent contractor opportunity, not hourly employment. It explains that cleaners are paid per job and gives realistic effective hourly averages based on speed and quality. The post is aimed at experienced, reliable residential cleaners who can work independently and take pride in their work. It lists requirements like two or more years of professional cleaning experience, reliable transportation, basic English, and insurance or willingness to get insurance. It also communicates that the schedule is flexible and cleaners can choose jobs and hours. The tone is straightforward, local, and values-based.
## Core Teaching
The main teaching is that a hiring post should be direct, clear, and specific about the type of cleaner you want. You are not trying to attract everybody. You are trying to attract experienced, reliable cleaners who understand that this is a contractor role, are comfortable being paid per job, and take ownership of their work.

The post also teaches that pay should be explained in a way that is attractive but honest. Instead of only saying “paid per job,” it gives effective hourly ranges and examples from top and lower performers. This helps cleaners understand the upside without misrepresenting the role as hourly employment.

The post makes the requirements simple: professional cleaning experience, transportation, independent work ability, detail orientation, accountability, basic English, and insurance. It also emphasizes the kind of person the business wants: high-energy, positive, proud of their work, and aligned with a solid Christian company.
## Key Principles
- Be clear that the role is 1099 independent contractor work.
- Say cleaners are paid per job, not by the hour.
- Give realistic effective hourly examples so cleaners understand the opportunity.
- Require real professional cleaning experience.
- Make insurance clear without making it sound impossible to get.
- Emphasize reliability, accountability, professionalism, and pride in the work.
- Keep the post copy/paste simple for local groups.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Post in local Facebook groups for the target service area.
2. Lead with the location and that the business is growing.
3. State clearly that the role is 1099 contractor work, not hourly employment.
4. Explain pay as per-job pay with effective hourly examples.
5. List the work: residential standard and deep cleans, checklists, professionalism, and showing up on time.
6. List requirements: experience, transportation, independent work, attention to detail, English, and insurance.
7. Close with the flexible schedule and the type of person you are looking for.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Do not make the role sound like hourly employment if it is a 1099 contractor role.
- Do not hide that pay is per job.
- Do not attract inexperienced cleaners by being vague about requirements.
- Do not forget to mention insurance expectations.
- Do not post without the service area; local context matters.

This resource is a ready-to-use Facebook group hiring post. It is written for The Faithful Cleaners and targets the McKinney, Frisco, Prosper, and Allen area.
