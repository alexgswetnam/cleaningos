MOVE IN REQUIRED QUESTIONS
Ask these questions if they are wanting a move-in cleaning:
Is anyone working in the home (contractors, painters, etc.?
Are there new or recently installed surfaces (e.g., floors,
cabinets, tile, paint) that are still curing or dusty?
Is the home completely empty of furniture and personal items?
Is debris removal needed (adhesive, plastic wrap, protective film,
caulk)?
If “yes” to #1, #2, or #4—or any debris removal—this is a Post
Construction clean, not a Move-In.
Explain difference: Move-In = sanitization/detailing of a finished
home thatʼs already “broom-clean.”
Post-Construction = removal of fine dust/film, residues, and
protection cleanup; requires different tools, PPE, and pricing.
