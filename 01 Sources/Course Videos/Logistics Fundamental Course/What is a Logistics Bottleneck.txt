Logistics bottleneck is scheduling chaos, scattered client communication, re-dos, missing supplies or keys, constant fires.



Basically: "We're busy… but it feels like we're scattered and relying on our memory to do things."



We lived here. When we started, we used a Google Voice number.

We forgot to follow up.

We didn't consistently rebook old one-time cleans.

We'd message people who didn't book from like two or three months ago at random times… because we were trying to remember everything in our heads.



And that's the point:



Logistics problems don't get solved by trying harder. They get solved by systems.



So we built a CRM that handles follow-ups automatically.

And even recently, in the past couple weeks, many other cleaning business owners started using it... because the pain is the same for everyone: dropped follow-up equals dropped money. Not to mention, using a CRM is going make your life a lot.



How do you solve logistics, depending on your season?







Survival season: one or two essential checklists, and basic scheduling rules so you stop bleeding time and money.



Stability season: CRM + auto-SMS follow-ups + a single source of truth for every job, note, and client conversation.



Scale season: internal operating processes plus a weekly ops cadence so the business can run without you being the brain.



Sustain/Sell season: formalize everything — document and audit — master operating manual, SOP library, the whole machine becomes transferable.
