## Quick Summary
This lesson explains that VA pay depends on the VA and their range, but higher pay does not automatically mean better work. The speaker has found some VAs from Bangladesh or Pakistan at $2 to $3 per hour who were great, and others asking $8 to $10 who did not seem like they would perform well. They use Wise to pay VAs, while mentioning Alipay as another platform they do not use. The speaker also notes that overseas VA payments are treated as a business expense in their situation, but tells students to consult a tax professional. Their own main VA is around $7.50/hour and runs almost everything in the business. The lesson is clear that performance matters more than hourly rate.
## Core Teaching
The core teaching is that VA pay should be judged by performance, not by assuming higher hourly cost means better quality. Some lower-cost VAs can be excellent, and some higher-cost VAs may not be a fit.

The speaker mentions seeing VAs from places like Bangladesh and Pakistan at $2 to $3 per hour who are great, while also seeing VAs asking $8 to $10 who do not seem capable based on conversation and fit. Their own VA, at around $7.50 per hour, runs nearly everything and performs extremely well.

For payment logistics, the speaker uses Wise because many VAs use it as a worldwide payment platform. They mention Alipay but do not use it. They also caution that they are not a tax professional and students should consult one for tax treatment.

The practical point: do not hire based on pay alone. Hire based on whether the VA can do the job well.
## Key Principles
- VA pay varies by person and country.
- More expensive does not automatically mean better.
- Lower-cost VAs can be excellent.
- Performance matters more than hourly rate.
- Wise is the payment platform used in the business.
- Tax treatment should be confirmed with a professional.
## Step-by-Step Process
1. Ask the VA for their rate range.
2. Do not assume the highest-priced VA is best.
3. Evaluate skill, communication, and fit through conversation or tests.
4. Choose pay based on performance potential and business value.
5. Use Wise for payments if it fits the VA and business.
6. Consult a tax professional for tax treatment of overseas VA payments.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Equating higher VA rates with better quality.
- Hiring without testing fit.
- Not having a clear payment platform.
- Taking tax assumptions as advice without consulting a professional.
- Ignoring great lower-cost candidates.
