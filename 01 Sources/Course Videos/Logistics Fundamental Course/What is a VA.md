## Quick Summary
A VA is a virtual assistant. In this lesson, the speaker explains that when they reference their team manager or team, they are often talking about one of their VAs. Their main VA runs almost everything in the business, while a support VA helps with tracking, sheet management, CRM checks, and follow-up calls. The reason this works is that talented people in countries like the Philippines can bring real skill at a much lower cost than hiring someone with similar ability in the United States. The speaker pushes back on the assumption that cheaper means worse, explaining that many VAs are educated, skilled, and trained in entrepreneur-friendly tools. A VA helps the owner stop being a one-man show, take calls when the owner cannot, and eventually step back from parts of the business.
## Core Teaching
The core teaching is that a VA is not just cheap admin help. A VA is a virtual assistant who can take real operational work off the owner's plate. In this business, the main VA can run the day-to-day, and a support VA can help with simpler but important work like tracking, CRM checks, sheets, and follow-up calls.

The speaker's point of view is that a good VA can create massive leverage. To hire someone in the United States at the same level might cost thousands per month, but a skilled overseas VA can cost far less because of differences in cost of living, not because the work is automatically lower quality.

A VA is especially useful when the owner is stuck as a one-person show, has a 9-to-5, misses calls, or wants to step back from handling every conversation and follow-up. The VA can become the person who answers calls, manages the CRM, tracks details, and keeps the business moving while the owner focuses elsewhere.
## Key Principles
- VA stands for virtual assistant.
- Cheap does not automatically mean low quality.
- A strong VA can run real parts of the business.
- The owner should not be the only person handling calls and follow-up forever.
- A VA can be full-time, part-time, main support, or specialized support.
- The value is leverage: time back, missed calls covered, and operations handled.
