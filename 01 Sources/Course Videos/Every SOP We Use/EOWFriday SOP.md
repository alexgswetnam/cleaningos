Quick Summary
Fix next week before it starts. Every Friday, confirm cleaner availability, update payment tracking, and audit next week’s schedule. Review booking notes for frequency, special requests, and access instructions. Use past duration data to refine time estimates for recurring clients. This rhythm prevents surprises, improves pricing accuracy, and aligns the team.

Core Teaching
Run a two-part weekly system: first align the team (availability and payment tracking), then harden the schedule (audit bookings, review notes, adjust time estimates). Ten focused minutes now prevent hours of chaos later.

Key Principles
Prevent, don’t react.
Availability confirmed beats assumptions.
Refine time estimates weekly to improve scheduling and pay accuracy.
Step-by-Step Process
Confirm Cleaner Availability
Verify availability and awareness of upcoming jobs via manual outreach or automated reminders.
Update Payment Tracking
Ensure completed jobs, durations (if tracked), pay amounts, and adjustments are current.
Audit Next Week’s Schedule
Check for conflicts, incorrect dates (esp. recurring), special instructions, and clients who shouldn’t be scheduled.
Review Booking Notes
Confirm frequency, special requests, and access instructions per job.
Adjust Time Estimates
Use past durations for recurring clients to update future estimates.
Rules and Checklist
Don’t skip the weekly review; always confirm availability and keep payment tracking updated.
