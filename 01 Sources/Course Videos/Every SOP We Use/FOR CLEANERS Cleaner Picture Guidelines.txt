Skool Lesson: FOR CLEANERS: Cleaner Picture Guidelines

Objective: Learn how to take clean, professional before/after photos that showcase your work and protect your reputation.



🗣 Why This Matters





Shows clients your professionalism and attention to detail.



Protects your work from disputes or misunderstandings.



Helps the team track quality and consistency across jobs.



📸 When to Take Photos





First-time cleans only (unless otherwise instructed).



Before photos: at the start of cleaning a room.



After photos: at the end of the clean, during the final walkthrough.



Damage or uncleanable areas: anytime you encounter issues.



✅ Photo Best Practices





Use natural light when possible. Turn on lights if necessary.



Hold the camera steady and horizontal (landscape mode).



Step back to capture the full room or area.



Remove personal items or clutter (tools, trash, bags) before snapping after photos.



❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid





Blurry or dark photos



Overly zoomed-in shots



Including personal clutter or body parts



Taking photos while actively cleaning



💡 Extra Tips





Take 1 wide photo per room to show overall cleanliness.



For deep cleans or move-outs, include baseboards, ovens, and inside fridge.



Make before/after angles match for easy comparison.



📤 How to Submit





Upload all photos to Job Media in Booking Koala.



This ensures documentation, client transparency, and accountability.









Student Version: FOR CLEANERS: Cleaner Picture Guidelines

1️⃣ Before Photos





Take photos after starting cleaning, not during walkthrough.



Capture 2–3 wide shots of each room.



Focus on areas with visible dirt, clutter, or buildup (e.g., sinks, counters, stovetops).



2️⃣ After Photos





Take photos at the end of the clean, during final walkthrough.



Match angles of before photos as closely as possible.



Make sure all surfaces are dry, wiped, and neatly staged (towels folded, beds made).



3️⃣ Damage or Uncleanable Areas





Take pictures of anything that cannot be cleaned or is damaged.



Send to the office if needed for reference or client questions.



4️⃣ Photo Quality Standards





Use natural or sufficient lighting.



Hold camera steady, in landscape mode.



Step back to fit the full area in frame.



Clear away personal clutter (tools, trash, bags).



5️⃣ Submission Procedure





Upload all before/after/damage photos to Job Media in Booking Koala.



Check that photos are clear, properly framed, and show progress.



Notify the team if anything cannot be documented properly.



✅ Notes for Staff





Photos protect your work, improve client trust, and help maintain team standards.



Consistent photo practices lead to better reviews, tips, and accountability.
