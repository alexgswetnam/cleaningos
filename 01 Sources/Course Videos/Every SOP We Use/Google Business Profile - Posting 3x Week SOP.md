Quick Summary
Post on your Google Business Profile three times weekly (Mon/Wed/Fri): 80% updates and 20% offers (≤15% discount). Use 1–3 real job photos per post—avoid stock and sensitive content; rename images consistently. Write copy with city + neighborhood, focus on one service, friendly tone, CTA “Tap Call now to book,” 650–900 characters, max one emoji. Offer posts have short validity and a single condition, with no prices or phone numbers in text. Use a simple post structure and track photo usage; review performance monthly and template your winners.

Core Teaching
Consistency and authenticity win. Real photos, local SEO cues, and a clear CTA make posts convert. Keep offers modest and time-bound. Maintain brand discipline in tone, length, and image standards. Track and iterate based on performance.

Key Principles
3x/week cadence with 80/20 updates-to-offers.
Real photos only; protect privacy; consistent filenames.
Local SEO in copy; single CTA.
Short, time-bound offers with one condition; no prices or phone numbers in text.
Step-by-Step Process
Schedule
Post Mon/Wed/Fri; 3 posts/week with 80% updates, 20% offers.
Photos
Attach 1–3 real photos (before/after, rooms, team in action); avoid faces and sensitive items; rename files city_service_cleaningbiz_###.jpg.
Copywriting
Include City + Neighborhood/Landmark; focus on one service; friendly, professional tone; CTA “Tap Call now to book”; 650–900 chars; max 1 emoji.
Offer Posts
Discount ≤15% with start/end dates; one condition (e.g., new clients only); no prices/phone numbers in text.
Posting Structure
Hook/Title → Body (benefit/service/solution) → CTA → optional hashtags → attach 1–3 real photos.
Quality Checklist
Correct city + neighborhood, correct CTA, real photos only, no sensitive images, discount ≤15% (if applicable), correct length.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Tap Call now to book.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Using stock photos or showing sensitive items.
Stuffing posts with multiple services.
Posting prices or phone numbers in text.
Reusing the same photo repeatedly without tracking.
