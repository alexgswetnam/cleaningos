Skool Lesson: How to Collect Payment from Clients

🧠 The Goal

After every job, your team should:





Confirm payment



Log it properly



Avoid awkward client situations



Keep your cash flow tight

If you mess this up, you get:





Lost revenue



Confused cleaners



Awkward client conversations



Bad reviews



⚙️ The Core Principle



Every clean ends with one thing: payment confirmed + logged.

No exceptions. No “we’ll figure it out later.”



🔁 The 3-Step System

1. Check Payment Method First

Before doing anything:





Look in your CRM/booking notes



Identify:





Card on file?



Cash/check?



Other?



2. Follow the Right Flow

💳 If Card on File





First-time client:





Follow your post-clean script



Let them know you’re charging the card



Recurring client:





Charge card once cleaner confirms completion



💵 If Cash / Check





Ask cleaner if they received payment



If yes:





Log it immediately



If no:





Contact client to confirm payment method



📱 If Digital Payment (Zelle, etc.)





Only allow if it fits your business policy



If allowed:





Confirm payment received



Log it clearly



3. Log Everything (THIS IS WHERE PEOPLE FAIL)

Every payment must be logged:





In CRM



In job notes



In payment tracking system

If it’s not logged:



it didn’t happen



⚠️ Rules That Will Save You Headaches





Never assume payment was handled



Always confirm with cleaner OR system



Always log immediately



Keep payment methods consistent



Escalate anything unclear immediately



🔥 Pro Tip

The best companies don’t just clean well…

They collect money cleanly.











Student Version: How to Collect Payment from Clients



🧾 PAYMENT HANDLING SOP (POST-CLEAN)

Step 1: Check Payment Method

Before taking action:





Review CRM + booking notes



Identify payment type:





Card on file



Cash



Check



Approved digital method



Step 2: Handle Based on Payment Type

💳 Card on File

First-Time Client:





Follow Post-Clean Script



Inform client you are charging the card on file

Recurring Client:





Charge card once cleaner confirms job completion



💵 Cash / Check





Contact cleaner to confirm if payment was received

If payment WAS received:





Log payment in CRM + notes immediately

If payment was NOT received:





Contact client to confirm how they will be paying



📝 Checks





Checks should be made out to the business name

If check is collected:





Log in payment tracking system

If cleaner leaves without check:





Ask if cleaner can return to collect



If not, escalate to manager



💵 Cash





Cleaners may collect cash if allowed by company policy

If cash is collected:





Log in payment tracking system



Ensure it is accounted for in weekly payouts



📱 Digital Payments (Zelle, etc.)





Only accept if approved by company policy

If approved:





Confirm payment received



Log clearly in CRM and payment tracker



Step 3: Log the Payment (REQUIRED)

Every payment must be recorded:





CRM notes



Booking/job notes



Payment tracking system



🚨 Escalation Rules

Escalate to a manager if:





Payment method is unclear



Payment was not received



Client is unresponsive



Exception to policy is requested



✅ Completion Checklist

Before closing a job:





[ ] Payment method confirmed



[ ] Payment received or charged



[ ] Logged in all systems



[ ] Notes updated
