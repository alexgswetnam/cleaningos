Quick Summary
This lesson sets a simple system for paying cleaners fairly and on time. Track every clean daily in a centralized tracker, including notes on re-cleans and reschedules. Review all logs weekly, adjust for changes, and verify accuracy before issuing payment. Pay tips directly to the cleaner and keep them separate from base pay. Keep the process consistent, documented, and simple. The weekly rhythm eliminates guesswork and prevents disputes. Verification is non-negotiable before payout.

Core Teaching
Create a tight daily-to-weekly loop: log every clean the day it happens, then review and reconcile at week’s end. Separate tips from base pay to keep pay fair and transparent. Apply adjustments for re-cleans, cancellations, and reschedules so cleaners are compensated correctly. Verification before payment is the safeguard that prevents errors from slipping into payouts. Keep payouts on a predictable weekly cadence and document everything.

Key Principles
Track daily, pay weekly.
Tips go to the cleaner and are not part of base pay.
Adjust for re-cleans, cancellations, and reschedules.
Verify entries and adjustments before paying.
Keep the process simple, consistent, and documented.
Step-by-Step Process
Daily Tracking
Log every clean immediately in a centralized tracker.
Include cleaner name, date, type of service, and notes for re-cleans/reschedules.
Weekly Review
At week’s end, review all logs.
Adjust for re-cleans, cancellations, and reschedules; ensure hours/work are accounted for fairly.
Verification
Double-check entries and confirm adjustments are reflected correctly.
Keep tips separate from base pay.
Payment Process
Pay cleaners weekly via your chosen method (bank transfer, app, etc.).
Keep the process simple, consistent, and documented.

Common Mistakes / Warnings
Skipping daily logs and trying to reconstruct at the end of the week.
Mixing tips with base pay.
Forgetting to adjust for re-cleans, cancellations, or reschedules.
Paying without verification.
