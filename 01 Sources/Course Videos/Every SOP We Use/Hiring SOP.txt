Skool Lesson: Hiring SOP

Objective: Teach students how to efficiently hire, interview, and onboard cleaners while ensuring quality, reliability, and professionalism.



🗣 Why This Matters





Hiring the right cleaners ensures client satisfaction and safety.



Standardized onboarding reduces mistakes and confusion on the job.



A structured process helps scale operations consistently.



✨ Key Principles





Qualification first: Experience, location, reliability.



Structured interviews: Assess warmth, communication, and professionalism.



Documentation is critical: Agreements, W-9, background checks.



Gradual onboarding: Pair new cleaners with experienced ones for first jobs.



Follow-up: Gather feedback from new cleaners and current staff.



📌 Hiring & Onboarding Flow





Find applicants: Use job boards like Indeed, Nextdoor, Facebook groups.



Screen resumes: Look for relevant experience and professionalism.



Interview: Video or phone calls to evaluate warmth, reliability, and skills.



Decision: Hire, decline, or waitlist.



Agreement & paperwork: Subcontractor agreement, W-9, background check.



Onboarding: Create system accounts, provide apps, guides, and checklists.



First job pairing: Partner with experienced cleaner for shadowing.



Feedback & evaluation: Collect information from new and paired cleaners before scheduling independently.



💡 Tips for Students





Always check references and prior experience.



Be consistent with scoring and evaluation.



Document everything in your management system.



Follow up on first jobs to ensure quality standards.









Student Version: Hiring SOP

1️⃣ Qualify Applicants





Use job boards: Indeed, Nextdoor, Facebook groups.



Accept if: 6+ months residential cleaning experience, nearby location, complete resume, friendly tone.



Message if unsure about experience.



Reject if: no relevant experience, missing transport/contact, hostile tone.



2️⃣ Schedule Interview





Offer 3 time slots within 24–72 hours.



Video call (Google Meet) or phone call using company line.



3️⃣ Conduct Interview





Assess using rubric (score 1–5 for each):





Warmth/friendliness



Communication clarity



Professionalism (appearance, punctuality)



Reliability (transportation, schedule honesty)



Residential cleaning knowledge



Watch for red flags: late, aloof, argumentative, blames previous clients.



4️⃣ Decision





Hire / Decline / Waitlist.



If hire: proceed to agreements and onboarding.



If decline: send polite rejection.



5️⃣ Agreements & Paperwork





Send subcontractor agreement to sign.



Collect W-9 and conduct background check.



Wait for all documents cleared before assigning jobs.



6️⃣ Onboarding





Create system account (Booking app, communication platforms).



Provide handbook, checklists, and guides.



Ensure cleaner has access to all apps and instructions.



7️⃣ First Job Assignment





Pair new cleaner with an experienced cleaner.



Notify both parties about pairing and expectations.



Observe performance during the first job.



8️⃣ Post-Job Evaluation





Call new cleaner for feedback.



Call experienced cleaner for evaluation.



Decide whether to schedule new cleaner independently or provide additional support.
