Quick Summary
Your database hides revenue. Segment clients into three groups: last-minute openings, warm reactivation (2–6 months), and full reactivation (6+ months). Start with SMS, then call if needed; keep messages short, polite, and helpful. Exclude recent contacts and “do not contact” clients. Track responses and schedule follow-ups to convert gaps and revive relationships.

Core Teaching
Segment → Reach Out → Automate & Track. Treat every unbooked slot and inactive client as an opportunity. Personalization and rules prevent spam; tracking keeps outreach efficient.

Key Principles
“Every unbooked slot or inactive client is an opportunity.”
Start with SMS; escalate with a call.
Respect exclusions and track everything.
Step-by-Step Process
Create Client Segments
Last-minute openings (fill within 72 hours).
Warm reactivation (booked 2–6 months ago, no outreach in last 30 days).
Full reactivation (6+ months; quarterly).
Outreach Messages
Last-minute: “We have a spot open in your area… want me to check times?”
Warm: “How’s the house looking… want times for a touch-up this month?”
Full: “It’s been a while… seasonal refresh options—want details?”
Optional call script for first-come-first-served slots.
Automation & Rules
Exclude recent contacts and DNC.
Track responses to avoid duplicates.
Schedule follow-ups for no-replies.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“We have a spot open in your area in the next couple of days. Want me to check times?”
“We’re offering seasonal refresh options this week — want details?”
“It’s first-come, first-served — want me to check available times?”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Blasting the entire list without exclusions.
Failing to track responses and double-messaging.
Waiting until the slot has already passed to start outreach.
