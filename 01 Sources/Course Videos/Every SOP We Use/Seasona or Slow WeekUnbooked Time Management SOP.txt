Skool Lesson: In-Depth Breakdown of GHL Stages/Responsibilities

Lesson Goal

Learn how to respond to leads quickly, track follow-ups, and convert more clients.



1️⃣ Why a Lead Follow-Up System Matters





Leads are most likely to book when contacted immediately.



Consistency keeps your business organized and professional.



Notes and follow-ups ensure no lead falls through the cracks.



2️⃣ Key Pipeline Stages





New Lead – Lead just reached out; needs initial contact.



Follow-Up – Lead responded but hasn’t booked yet.



Quoted – Price or service quote has been provided.



Booked – Lead has scheduled a service.



Cancelled / Declined – Lead chose not to book.



Do Not Contact – Lead asked not to be contacted again.



💡 Tip: Even a simple spreadsheet can work as a pipeline — you don’t need complex software to stay organized.



3️⃣ Follow-Up Principles





Respond quickly — ideally within minutes to hours.



Be consistent — follow up multiple times if no response.



Document everything — calls, texts, emails, notes.



Respect the lead’s preferences — stop contacting if requested.



4️⃣ Multi-Touch Follow-Up Approach





Initial Contact: Welcome + service inquiry.



Follow-Up 1: 4–6 hours later.



Follow-Up 2: 24–48 hours later.



Long-Term Follow-Up: 30–90 days later (for unresponsive leads).



🔑 Key: Every touch should be polite, professional, and helpful.



5️⃣ Takeaways for Students





Speed + consistency = higher booking rates.



Clear pipeline stages prevent missed opportunities.



Documentation protects your business and builds professionalism.



You can implement this system with just a spreadsheet or simple CRM.









Student Version: In-Depth Breakdown of GHL Stages/Responsibilities

1️⃣ Check for New Leads





Review all new messages daily (email, text, social media, website form).



Respond to every new lead as quickly as possible—ideally within 30 minutes.

2️⃣ Initial Contact





Send a friendly, professional message or make a call. Example: "Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out about [Service]. When would be a good time to chat about your cleaning needs?"

3️⃣ Follow-Up Schedule ⏰





If the lead doesn’t respond, follow this sequence:





Follow-Up 1 (4–6 hours later): "Hi [Name]! Just checking in—happy to answer any questions about [Service]!"



Follow-Up 2 (24–48 hours later): "Hi [Name]! Are you still interested in scheduling your cleaning?"



Long-Term Follow-Up (30–90 days later): "Hi [Name]! We’d love to help with your cleaning if you’re ready. Let us know!"

4️⃣ Track Leads 📝





Keep a simple spreadsheet or CRM with:





Name



Contact Info



Service Interested In



Last Contact Date



Next Follow-Up Date



Status (New Lead / Follow-Up / Quoted / Booked / Cancelled / Do Not Contact)



Notes (any client preferences or details)

5️⃣ Booked Leads ✅





Confirm date/time with the client.



Assign staff or yourself to complete the job.



Record service type and any special requests.

6️⃣ Do Not Contact 🚫





If a lead asks to stop being contacted, mark them as “Do Not Contact.”



Never reach out to these leads again.

7️⃣ Weekly Review 🔄





Review all leads in the spreadsheet or CRM.



Check for unresponsive leads and schedule next follow-ups.



Update any statuses or notes as needed.
