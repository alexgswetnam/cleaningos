Quick Summary
Call after each clean with a warm, relationship-first script. Celebrate wins; if issues arise, listen and offer a plan to fix. Confirm the next clean or set up a recurring schedule. If needed, handle payment politely and send the review link during the call. Close with sincere appreciation. This increases reviews, retention, and smooth payment handling.

Core Teaching
Connection first, then business. Use a simple, repeatable call flow to gather feedback, confirm schedules, handle payment, ask for reviews, and make clients feel valued.

Key Principles
Warm tone; mirror client energy.
Fix issues proactively; promise and deliver follow-up.
Ask for reviews casually and gratefully.
Step-by-Step Process
Warm Introduction
“Hi [Client Name]… how did your clean go today?”
Connection & Feedback
Celebrate positives; if not perfect, ask what was missed and propose a fix.
Confirm Recurring Schedule
“Next clean is [Date], does that work?” If none, offer to set one.
Payment (If Needed)
“I noticed we don’t have a payment on file. How would you like to take care of today’s clean?”
Ask for a Review
“Would you mind leaving us a 5-star review? Mentioning your cleaner helps them get recognized!”
Close With Appreciation
“Thanks again for trusting [Your Business]… I’m a call or text away.”
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“That seriously makes my day to hear! I’ll make sure your cleaner knows.”
“Thank you for letting me know—that’s exactly what I want to hear so we can fix it.”
“Would you mind leaving us a 5-star review? If [Cleaner’s Name] stood out…”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Sounding transactional or robotic.
Asking for reviews without confirming satisfaction.
Forgetting to send the review link while on the call.
