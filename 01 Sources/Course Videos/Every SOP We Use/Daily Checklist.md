Quick Summary
Closing the day properly prevents problems from piling up. This checklist ensures tomorrow’s jobs are assigned, payments are accounted for, and leads are fully worked. It standardizes customer follow-ups and reviews, keeps tracking current, and enforces team communication. The simple rule: don’t end the day until everything is accounted for. You remove reliance on memory and eliminate gaps in operations. Consistency here protects revenue and client experience.

Core Teaching
Run a structured EOD across six areas: operations, payments, leads/follow-ups, customer experience, tracking, and team communication. Assign all cleans, collect and log all payments, and make sure every lead has a next action. Follow up with first-time customers, engage reviews, and post an update. Log completed jobs and issues, and send team summaries so nothing slips through the cracks.

Cleaned Lesson Notes
Core Idea: If you don’t close the day properly, problems stack up. Missed follow-ups = lost clients; unassigned jobs = chaos; no tracking = no growth.
Why This Works: Tight operations, prevents missed revenue, improves client experience, builds accountability.
Rule: Don’t end the day until everything is accounted for.
4 Pillars:
Tomorrow is already handled (assign jobs).
Money is accounted for (track payments).
Leads are fully worked (calls/texts and next actions).
Everything is logged and communicated.
Student Version Checklist Highlights:
Operations: assign cleans, review upcoming bookings/notes.
Payments: collect and confirm all payments; log in system.
Leads: call/text new leads 2x; schedule follow-ups; ensure next action.
Customer Experience: follow up with first-time customers; check/respond to reviews; post/update Google Business.
Tracking: update leads; log completed jobs; add notes for issues.
Team Communication: send summaries of leads and cleans; log mistakes and fixes; update on projects.
Daily Rule: Finish everything before ending the day.
