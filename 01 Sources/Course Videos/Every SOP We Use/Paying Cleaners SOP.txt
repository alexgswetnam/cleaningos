Skool Lesson: Paying Cleaners SOP

Lesson Content:





Daily Tracking 🗂️





Every clean should be logged in a centralized tracker (spreadsheet or app).



Include: cleaner name, date, type of service, and any notes about re-cleans or reschedules.



Weekly Review 🕒





At the end of the week, review all logs.



Adjust for any re-cleans, cancellations, or reschedules.



Make sure all hours and work are accounted for fairly.



Pay Principles 💰





Ensure each cleaner receives fair pay for the hours they worked.



Tips go directly to the cleaner and are not included in base pay.



Verification ✅





Double-check entries for accuracy before issuing pay.



Confirm that adjustments for re-cleans or canceled jobs are reflected.



Payment Process 🏦





Pay your cleaners weekly using your chosen method (bank transfer, app, etc.).



Keep the process simple, consistent, and documented.

Key Takeaways:





Track daily, review weekly.



Adjust for changes (re-cleans/reschedules).



Pay fairly and on a consistent schedule.







Student Version: Paying Cleaners SOP

SOP: Weekly Cleaner Pay Process

Purpose: Ensure cleaner pay is consistent, accurate, and fair.

Scope: All cleaners performing work for the business.

Step 1: Daily Tracking 🗂️





Log every clean immediately after completion.



Include:





Cleaner name



Date of clean



Type of service



Notes for re-cleans, reschedules, or cancellations

Step 2: Weekly Review 🕒





At the end of the week, review all clean logs.



Adjust hours or pay for:





Re-cleans



Canceled jobs



Rescheduled jobs

Step 3: Verify Pay ✅





Ensure all entries are accurate.



Make sure adjustments are reflected correctly.



Confirm tips are accounted for separately (paid directly to cleaners).

Step 4: Process Payment 💰





Issue pay weekly using your chosen method (bank transfer, PayPal, etc.).



Document payments in your tracking system.



Maintain consistency for all cleaners.

Step 5: Optional Notes 📝





Keep a simple checklist or spreadsheet to confirm weekly steps are complete.



Include a space for “reviewed by” or manager initials to ensure accountability.
