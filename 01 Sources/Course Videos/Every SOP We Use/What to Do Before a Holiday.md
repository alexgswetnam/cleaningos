Quick Summary
Holidays don’t kill demand—poor confirmations do. Control the schedule before clients cancel: contact 2–3 weeks ahead, double-confirm during holiday week, and align cleaners. On the day, execute professionally and backfill last-minute gaps. Post-holiday, thank clients and convert them into repeat bookings. The earlier you confirm, the fewer cancellations you’ll face.

Core Teaching
Run a four-phase system: Pre-Holiday outreach and scheduling; Week-Of double-confirmations and alignment; Day-Of clean execution with contingency handling; Post-Holiday follow-up and retention. Document changes immediately to keep the calendar accurate.

Key Principles
“Control the schedule before the client cancels.”
Confirm early; offer flexibility; stay organized.
Backfill last-minute gaps using waitlists/standby.
Step-by-Step Process
Pre-Holiday (2–3 Weeks Before)
Identify affected bookings; send confirmation/reschedule message; call if needed; update schedule immediately.
Week Of
Send 1–2 day reminders; confirm cleaner assignments, supplies, and special notes; confirm arrival times night before.
Day Of
Arrive on time; follow checklists; take photos if part of system; if last-minute cancel: notify team, attempt reschedule, backfill slot.
Post-Holiday
Send thank-you; optionally offer a small bonus; review what went well/what to improve.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“We noticed your cleaning is scheduled for [Date], which is close to the holidays… keep or reschedule?”
“Reminder for your [Date] cleaning… if plans changed, let us know by [cutoff time].”
Post-holiday thank-you message.
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Waiting until the last minute to confirm.
Failing to update the calendar in real time.
Not coordinating cleaner availability ahead of time.
