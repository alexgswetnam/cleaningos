Skool Lesson: How to Handle a Cancel/Re-Schedule

🧠 The Goal

When a client cancels, your team should:





Try to save the booking



Apply your policy consistently



Keep the client relationship intact



Protect your revenue + schedule stability

If you don’t have a system for this, you’ll get:





Last-minute gaps in your schedule



Lost revenue



Frustrated cleaners



Inconsistent client experience



⚙️ The Core Principle



Every cancellation is either saved, rescheduled, or monetized.

No gray area. No “we’ll deal with it later.”



🔁 The 3-Step Cancellation System

1. Identify Timing (This Determines Everything)





More than 72 hours notice → No fee



Less than 72 hours notice → Cancellation fee applies



2. Attempt to Save the Booking (Always Do This First)

Before canceling:





Ask why (politely, not pushy)



Look for a solution:





Reschedule



Adjust scope



Address quality concerns



Offer a targeted incentive (only if it makes sense)

👉 Important:

Do NOT overuse discounts. You are running a business, not negotiating every job.



3. Apply Policy + Confirm Next Steps

If More Than 72 Hours Notice:





Cancel with no fee



Ask:





“Would you like to reschedule?”



“Do you want to keep future cleanings?”



If Less Than 72 Hours Notice:





Apply cancellation fee (example: 25%, capped)

Exceptions:





Emergencies (illness, death, etc.)





You may waive the fee once as goodwill

If client resists fee:





Politely reinforce policy



Offer solutions:





Pay now



Add to next cleaning



If repeated refusal:





Escalate and consider discontinuing service



🧾 Logging & Tracking (MOST PEOPLE SKIP THIS)

Every cancellation must be logged:





Booking system



CRM/client notes

Track:





Reason



Date



Who handled it

👉 If you don’t track cancellations, you can’t fix churn.



🔥 Pro Tip

The best companies don’t avoid cancellations…

They control how cancellations affect revenue.









Student Version: How to Handle a Cancel/Re-Schedule

🧾 CANCELLATION HANDLING SOP



Step 1: Determine Timing

Check how far in advance the client is canceling:





72+ hours before appointment → No fee



Less than 72 hours → Cancellation fee applies



Step 2: Attempt Retention (REQUIRED)

Before processing cancellation:

Ask for reason (politely):





“Thanks for letting us know — do you mind sharing what’s causing the cancellation?”

Based on response:





Offer to reschedule



Address concerns (price, quality, timing)



Offer incentive ONLY if appropriate and profitable



Step 3: Apply Cancellation Policy

✅ 72+ Hours Notice





Cancel booking with no fee



Ask:





“Would you like to reschedule?”



“Should we keep your future appointments active?”



⚠️ Less Than 72 Hours Notice





Apply cancellation fee:





Example: 25% of booking (with max cap)



Step 4: Handle Exceptions

If client has a serious emergency:





You may waive the fee once



Document reason clearly



Step 5: Handle Payment for Cancellation Fee

Follow company-approved payment methods.

If client does not pay:





Offer to add fee to next booking



If refusal continues:





Escalate to manager



Consider discontinuing service



Step 6: Update Systems

Update in your booking system:





Cancel appointment



Apply or waive fee correctly



Select cancellation reason

Update in CRM:





Add note including:





Date



Reason



Action taken



Your name



Step 7: Notify Cleaner (If Needed)





If appointment is within 7 days → notify cleaner



If more than 7 days away → no notification needed



Step 8: Optional Follow-Up

If cancellation is temporary (travel, schedule conflict, etc.):





Set reminder to follow up later



🚨 Rules





Always attempt to save the booking first



Do not skip cancellation fees unless justified



Do not overuse discounts



Always log every cancellation



Escalate unclear or difficult situations



✅ Completion Checklist

Before closing:





[ ] Timing confirmed (72+ hrs vs <72 hrs)



[ ] Retention attempt made



[ ] Fee applied or waived correctly



[ ] Payment handled or documented



[ ] Booking system updated



[ ] CRM notes added



[ ] Cleaner notified (if needed)

This system:





Protects your revenue



Keeps your schedule full



Maintains professionalism



Builds long-term client relationships
