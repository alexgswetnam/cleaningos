Quick Summary
Prepare before you arrive, greet professionally, and do a brief walkthrough to set expectations. Take before/after photos for first-time cleans and issues. Work efficiently and respectfully, then do a final walkthrough to invite feedback. Follow the do-not-do rules to maintain trust and professionalism.

Core Teaching
Preparation, communication, documentation, and respectful execution are the pillars of a great client experience.

Key Principles
Be prepared and friendly.
Communicate clearly via walkthroughs and notes.
Document with photos.
Follow professionalism rules.
Step-by-Step Process
Before Arrival
Check schedule; notify if late; mark “on the way”; clock-in/out; read notes; contact team for access issues.
Arrival
Bring supplies; knock/ring; call team after 3 minutes with no response.
Walkthrough
Invite walkthrough; ask preferences; set expectations for difficult areas; build rapport.
Photos
First-time cleans: 2–3 wides before; match angles after; upload to Booking Koala; capture impossible/damage areas.
During the Clean
Work calmly; keep noise low; respect pets/work-from-home; follow notes; aim for delight.
Final Walkthrough & Goodbye
Review areas; invite feedback; text completion and send photos if client away; mention review bonus if allowed.
Do Not Do List
No client supplies unless allowed; no eating/smoking; no guests; no loud calls; don’t leave early; check BK for timing.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Would you like to walk through your home with me?”
“I’d love to touch up anything you notice — it’s no problem at all!”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Skipping notes and walkthroughs.
No photo documentation for first-time or problem areas.
Unprofessional behavior on-site.
