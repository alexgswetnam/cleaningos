Quick Summary
Connect after the clean with a warm check-in, confirm the next booking, then ask casually for a 5-star review—ideally while sending the link during the call. If there’s an issue, address it first before asking for a review. Mentioning the cleaner by name helps recognition and motivation. This simple, human approach raises review counts and retention.

Core Teaching
Lead with gratitude and service, not a hard ask. Make the path to review effortless by sending the link immediately and keeping the tone friendly and genuine.

Key Principles
Connection before request.
Make reviewing easy (link ready).
Confirm recurring schedule.
Step-by-Step Process
Warm Introduction
Friendly check-in about today’s clean.
Connection & Feedback
Celebrate positives; gather details if something was missed and promise a fix.
Confirm Recurring Schedule
Confirm next date or offer to set one.
Ask for a Review
Casual, grateful ask; send the link during the call.
Close With Appreciation
Thank them and stay available.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Would you mind leaving us a 5-star review? If [Cleaner’s Name] stood out, mentioning them is a great way to recognize their work!”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Asking for reviews before resolving issues.
Not having the review link ready.
Sounding scripted or pushy.
