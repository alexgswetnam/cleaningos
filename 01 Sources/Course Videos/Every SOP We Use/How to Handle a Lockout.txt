Skool Lesson: How to Handle a Lockout

🧠 The Goal

When a cleaner can’t access a home, your team should:





Confirm the situation quickly



Attempt to reach the client immediately



Minimize wasted time



Apply your policy consistently

If you don’t have a system for this, you’ll get:





Lost time and revenue



Frustrated cleaners



Confused clients



Inconsistent handling



⚙️ The Core Principle



Move fast, communicate clearly, and protect your team’s time.



🔁 The 5-Step Lockout System



1. Confirm It’s a Real Lockout





Cleaner:





Knocks / rings doorbell



Waits a few minutes

👉 Rule:

Don’t assume — confirm first



2. Notify the Team Immediately





Cleaner reports:





Client name



Address



Scheduled time

👉 This should happen right away (no waiting around)



3. Contact the Client Fast





Assigned team member:





Calls client within a few minutes



If no answer:





Leaves voicemail



Sends text message

👉 Speed here often saves the job



4. Set a Wait Window





Cleaner waits a limited time (example: ~15 minutes past arrival)

If client responds:





Grant access → proceed with clean

If no response:





Confirm lockout status



Move to next step



5. Apply Lockout Policy

Your company should have a clear policy that includes:





A lockout fee (often a % of the job with a cap)



When the fee is applied (immediate vs after contact)



How disputes are handled

👉 Important:





Be consistent



Stay professional



Assume client error, not bad intent



💰 Cleaner Compensation





Compensate cleaners for their time spent waiting



Set:





A fair rate



A maximum cap

👉 Goal:

Protect your team without hurting your margins



📞 Follow-Up

After the lockout:





Send a message confirming:





What happened



Any applicable fee



Rescheduling options



🧾 Documentation

Log the incident in your system:





Client



Date



Outcome



Any fees applied

👉 This helps:





Spot repeat issues



Protect against disputes



🔥 Pro Tip

Most companies handle lockouts emotionally…

The best ones handle them systematically.
