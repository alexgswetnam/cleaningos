Skool Lesson: How to Get Cleaners on Discord

🧠 The Goal

Every cleaning business needs clear, consistent communication so nothing slips through the cracks.

With the right system, your team will:





Know exactly where to find updates



Respond quickly to client changes



Stay organized without endless texts or missed calls



⚙️ Key Principle



Use a text-based platform for day-to-day communication (job updates, announcements, scheduling) and a call app for urgent voice communication.



🔁 Step-by-Step Communication System

1. Choose Your Platforms





Primary: Team messaging platform (Discord, Slack, Microsoft Teams, etc.)



Secondary: Call app for urgent voice communication (Google Voice, phone system, etc.)

Tip: Keep both apps installed on all team devices.



2. Set Up Your Messaging Platform





Install the app on your phone and computer



Create an account using a business email



Join your team server or workspace



Organize channels for:





#announcements – important updates



#daily-jobs – cleaning schedule updates



#questions – quick team support



Optional: #supplies, #feedback, etc.



3. Enable Notifications





Turn on notifications for all messages in critical channels



Check your phone’s system notifications to make sure alerts are allowed



4. Team Best Practices





Check messages daily – before and after jobs



Use mentions (@Name) when you need someone’s attention



Send photos or videos when reporting issues or completed work



Reply to threads to keep conversations organized



5. When to Use the Call App





For urgent questions during jobs (e.g., access issues, keys, lockboxes)



Keep it installed and ready to use, but text-based updates remain your primary communication



6. Training & Onboarding





New team members should:





Install both apps



Join your workspace



Learn how to send messages, tag, and upload photos



Provide a short tutorial or checklist for first-week setup
