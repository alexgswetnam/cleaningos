Quick Summary
Every complaint should be resolved, clarified, or escalated—never ignored. Start by triaging severity (minor vs larger), then gather context with photos and specifics to avoid miscommunication. Default solution: offer a re-clean within the guarantee window, confirming access and instructions. Handle refunds within defined limits and escalate larger ones. Apply fair limitations when issues fall outside scope. Track every incident to identify training opportunities.

Core Teaching
Lead with fixing the work, not refunding the money. Triage, collect details, offer a timely re-clean, and only then consider refunds within guardrails. Document outcomes for pattern recognition and training. Apply limitations fairly to maintain trust without rewarding misuse.

Key Principles
“Every complaint is either resolved, clarified, or escalated — never ignored.”
Default to re-clean; refunds are bounded and escalated when large.
Use a guarantee window (e.g., 48 hours).
Logging creates training opportunities.
Step-by-Step Process
Confirm Eligibility
Reported within guarantee window; related to scope.
Triage Issue
Minor: apologize; fix next visit (with client’s OK) and note it.
Larger: proceed to full re-clean.
Collect Details
Ask for photos; description; review original scope.
Offer Re-Clean
Provide 2–3 time windows within guarantee; confirm access, pets, special instructions.
Handle Refund Requests
Approve up to defined limit; escalate larger refunds.
Assign Cleaner
Original returns or assign new; ensure fair compensation; manage accountability per policy.
Confirm with Client
Send confirmation with window, tasks, and access.
Apply Limitations (if outside scope, pre-existing damage, declined add-ons, or blocked work).
Log the Issue
Record client, cleaner, issue, date, resolution, and notes/lesson.
Completion Checklist
Triage done; details gathered; re-clean offered/scheduled; refund handled if applicable; booking updated; client confirmed; issue logged.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
