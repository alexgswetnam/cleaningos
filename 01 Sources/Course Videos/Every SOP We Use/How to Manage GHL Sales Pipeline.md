Quick Summary
Keep every lead moving with daily dashboard checks, completing due tasks, and always assigning the next step. New Hot Leads get a daily call; texting can be automated. For Follow-Up/Quoted leads, either move to long-term follow-up or set a new task in two days. Ensure every lead has an active task—no opportunity should sit idle.

Core Teaching
An SOP anyone can follow: check, complete, reassign or advance the stage. Consistency beats speed; daily calls matter. Update the SOP as you learn what works better. Reward adherence.

Key Principles
Daily cadence: check dashboard; clear due/overdue tasks.
Always assign the next task or update the stage.
Long-term nurture when short-term attempts stall.
Step-by-Step Process
Check Dashboard Daily
Clear due/overdue tasks; after completion, assign new task or update stage.
Follow-Up / Quoted
Many attempts with no response: move to long-term follow-up.
Few attempts: complete current task; assign next for +2 days.
If lead responds: update stage and assign next task.
New Hot Leads
Call each daily; let automated texts run.
Double-Check Tasks
Ensure every Follow-Up/Quoted lead has an assigned next task.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Consistency beats speed—call leads daily, even if you don’t get a response.”
“When you complete a task, either assign a new one or update the pipeline stage.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Leaving leads without a next task.
Only texting; skipping daily calls.
Letting the dashboard accumulate overdue tasks.
