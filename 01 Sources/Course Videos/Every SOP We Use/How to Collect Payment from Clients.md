Quick Summary
Every clean must end with payment confirmed and logged—no exceptions. Start by checking the payment method in the CRM or booking notes. Follow the correct flow for card on file, cash/check, or approved digital methods. Confirm receipt with the cleaner or system, then log payments in all required places. Keep methods consistent and escalate anything unclear immediately. This disciplined process keeps cash flow tight and client interactions clean.

Core Teaching
Payment handling is a three-step system: identify the payment method, follow the correct handling flow, and log everything in CRM, job notes, and your payment tracker. Never assume payment—confirm and log immediately. Keep to approved methods and escalate exceptions. The best companies collect money cleanly, not just deliver good service.

Step-by-Step Process
Check Payment Method
Review CRM/booking notes; identify card on file, cash/check, or approved digital.
Follow the Right Flow
Card on file: for first-time clients, follow post-clean script and inform charge; for recurring, charge after cleaner confirms completion.
Cash/Check: confirm with cleaner; if received, log immediately; if not, contact client to confirm method. For checks: ensure made to business, log in tracker, arrange collection or escalate.
Digital (if allowed): confirm received and log clearly.
Log Everything (Required)
Record in CRM notes, booking/job notes, and payment tracker.
Escalation Rules
Escalate unclear payment, non-receipt, unresponsive clients, or exceptions to policy.
Completion Checklist
Payment method confirmed, payment received/charged, logged in all systems, notes updated.

Common Mistakes / Warnings
