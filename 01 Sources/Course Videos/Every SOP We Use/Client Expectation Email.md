Quick Summary
Send an expectations message after booking and again 24–48 hours before the appointment. Cover prep instructions, scope (what’s in/out), limitations, payment, cancellation policy, and quality assurance (walkthrough, on-the-spot fixes). This single system reduces issues by a wide margin and positions your business as professional.

Core Teaching
Proactive clarity beats reactive fixes. Set boundaries, explain processes, and make payment expectations explicit.
