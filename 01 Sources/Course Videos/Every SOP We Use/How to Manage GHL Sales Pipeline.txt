Skool Lesson: How to Manage GHL Sales Pipeline

🎯 Lesson Objective:

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:





Keep your dashboard clean and organized ✅



Follow up with leads efficiently 📞



Assign and complete tasks without missing anything 🗂️



Implement this SOP directly in your own business 💼



🔍 Step 1: Understand the Goal

Goal: Keep every lead moving through your pipeline efficiently so you never miss a sale.

Why it matters: Staying consistent with follow-ups increases bookings and client satisfaction. 🌟



🛠️ Step 2: Gather What You Need

Before you start, make sure you have:





Access to your CRM or dashboard 🖥️



Your task and follow-up lists 📋



Phone ready for calls 📱



Automated texting system (if available 🤖)



✏️ Step 3: SOP – Lead & Task Management

Copy-Paste SOP Template for Your Biz

📌 Title: Lead & Task Management SOP

💡 Purpose: Ensure every lead is followed up and tasks are completed in a timely manner.

👤 Owner: [Insert Your Name / Team Member]

🛠️ Tools Needed: CRM/Dashboard, Phone, Automated Texting System

⏰ Frequency: Daily

Step-by-Step Instructions:





📊 Check Dashboard Daily





Complete all due or overdue tasks ✅



When you complete a task, either assign a new one or update the pipeline stage 🔄



📩 Follow Up / Quoted Stage





Leads with multiple follow-ups and no response → move to long-term follow-up 🕒



Leads with fewer follow-ups and no response → complete the current task and assign a new one for 2 days later 📆



If a lead responds → update the pipeline stage and assign the next task (e.g., move quoted → booked) 🎯



🔥 New Lead Hot Stage





Call each new lead once daily 📞



Texting is handled automatically 🤖



✅ Double-Check Tasks





Ensure every lead in Follow Up / Quoted Stage has a task assigned 📌



Make sure no opportunities are falling through the cracks 🚫

🏆 Outcome: All leads are followed up on consistently, and your pipeline stays organized.

📝 Notes / Tips:





Consistency beats speed—call leads daily, even if you don’t get a response ⏱️



Update your SOP whenever you find a more efficient workflow ✨



Track what works and tweak your scripts or task timing as needed 🛠️



🧪 Step 4: Test & Iterate





Follow this SOP exactly for 1 week 📅



Ask yourself: Can someone else pick up this SOP and handle all leads without asking questions? 🤔



Make updates for clarity and efficiency 📝



🚀 Step 5: Implement in Your Business





Assign SOPs to your team members 👥



Monitor task completion daily 📊



Reward team members for following SOPs correctly 🏅
