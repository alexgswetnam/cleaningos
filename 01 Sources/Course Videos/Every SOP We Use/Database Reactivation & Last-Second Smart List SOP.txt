Skool Lesson: Database Reactivation & Last-Second Smart List SOP

Core Idea:





Many clients simply need a gentle reminder

Your business has hidden revenue in old clients and unfilled slots.





Last-minute openings can be turned into revenue with smart outreach



Reactivation can stabilize slow weeks



Rule: “Every unbooked slot or inactive client is an opportunity — don’t leave it on the table.”



Why This Works:





Prevents revenue loss during slow periods



Keeps your client list active



Strengthens client relationships



Creates predictable cash flow



Common Mistake:

Waiting until it’s too late or contacting clients without rules → spammy impression or lost trust



3-Step Smart Reactivation System:





Segment Clients → last-minute openings, warm reactivation, full reactivation



Reach Out → SMS first, call if needed, keep messages polite and helpful



Automate & Track → exclude recent contacts, track responses, and schedule follow-ups







Student Version: Database Reactivation & Last-Second Smart List SOP

📌 OLD LEADS & LAST-MINUTE OPENINGS SOP



🎯 Goal

Maximize revenue from old clients and unfilled slots by creating controlled outreach campaigns.



1️⃣ CREATE CLIENT SEGMENTS

Segment 1 — Last-Minute Openings





Clients who haven’t booked recently but are eligible for a quick fill



Use when a cleaner has open slots within 72 hours

Segment 2 — Warm Reactivation





Clients who booked 2–6 months ago



No recent outreach in last 30 days



Positive client history

Segment 3 — Full Reactivation





Clients who booked over 6 months ago



Did not opt-out



Targeted quarterly



2️⃣ OUTREACH MESSAGES

SMS Templates

Last-Minute Openings:



Hi [Client Name]! We have a spot open in your area in the next couple of days. Want me to check if it works for you?

Warm Reactivation:



Hey [Client Name]! How’s the house looking lately? Want me to pull up available times for a touch-up cleaning this month?

Full Reactivation:



Hi [Client Name]! It’s been a while since your last cleaning. We’re offering seasonal refresh options this week — want details?



Call Script (Optional)



Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Company Name]. We had a last-minute opening and wanted to see if you’d like to grab a spot. It’s first-come, first-served — want me to check available times?



3️⃣ AUTOMATION & RULES





Exclude clients who recently received outreach



Skip clients marked as “do not contact”



Track responses to avoid duplicates



Schedule follow-ups for unresponsive clients



🧠 Key Rules





Always personalize messages



Keep tone polite & helpful



Follow rules for exclusions — don’t spam



Log all responses and schedule follow-ups consistently



🔁 Quick Flow

Segment Clients → Send SMS → Call if Needed → Track Responses → Schedule Follow-Up → Update Client Status





This system ensures old leads and last-minute openings are always opportunities, never wasted.
