Cleaned Lesson Notes
General: microfiber (color-coded), dusting tools, all-purpose, glass, polish, disinfectant, erasers, vacuum, mop, broom, bucket, stool, bags, basket.
Kitchen: degreaser, stainless cleaner, counter-safe cleaners, dish soap, non-scratch sponges, detailing brushes, oven/fridge cleaners, polishing cloths.
Bathroom: tile/grout, shower/soap scum, mildew, toilet cleaner + brush, scrub brushes, gloves.
Floor care: vacuum, hardwood/tile cleaners, mop solution.
Optional: handheld vac, caddy, bottles, masks, knee pads, sticky rollers.
