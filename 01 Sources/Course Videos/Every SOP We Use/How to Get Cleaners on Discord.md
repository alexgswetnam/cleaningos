Quick Summary
Use a text-first platform (Discord, Slack, Teams) for daily communication and a call app for urgent voice. Set up core channels (#announcements, #daily-jobs, #questions) and enable notifications. Train the team on mentions, media uploads, and threaded replies. Keep both apps installed; use calls only for urgent, on-the-job issues. Onboard every new team member with a short tutorial and checklist.

Core Teaching
Choose a primary messaging platform and a secondary call app. Structure channels by function, enforce notification hygiene, and standardize daily practices. Make onboarding explicit so every cleaner can send messages, tag teammates, and share photos/videos from day one.

Key Principles
Text-based for day-to-day; calls for urgent issues.
Clear channel structure reduces noise.
Notification discipline ensures responsiveness.
Step-by-Step Process
Choose Platforms
Messaging: Discord/Slack/Teams. Voice: Google Voice/phone.
Set Up Messaging
Install apps; create account with business email; join server/workspace; create #announcements, #daily-jobs, #questions; optionally #supplies, #feedback.
Enable Notifications
Turn on notifications for critical channels; verify device settings.
Team Best Practices
Check messages before/after jobs; use @mentions; send photos/videos; reply in threads.
Use the Call App
For urgent issues (access, keys, immediate changes) only.
Training & Onboarding
New members install apps, join workspace, learn messaging basics; provide a quick setup checklist.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Use mentions (@Name) when you need someone’s attention.”
“Reply to threads to keep conversations organized.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Relying solely on calls or ad-hoc texts.
Failing to enable notifications on critical channels.
Not training new members on uploads and tagging.
