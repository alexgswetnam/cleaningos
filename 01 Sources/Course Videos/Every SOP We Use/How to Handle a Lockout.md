Quick Summary
Move fast, communicate clearly, and protect your team’s time. Confirm it’s a real lockout, notify the team immediately, and contact the client quickly. Set a short wait window (e.g., ~15 minutes). If access is granted, proceed; if not, apply a consistent lockout policy with a clear fee and dispute handling. Compensate cleaners for wait time with a fair rate and cap. Follow up with the client and document the incident for patterns and protection.

Core Teaching
A five-step lockout system prevents wasted time: confirm, notify, contact, wait briefly, then apply policy. Keep communication professional and consistent. Compensate cleaners fairly for waits while protecting margins. Always follow up and log the incident.

Key Principles
Speed saves the job; clarity protects time.
Consistent policy builds trust and reduces disputes.
Cleaner time is valuable—compensate within defined caps.
Step-by-Step Process
Confirm Lockout
Knock/ring; wait a few minutes; don’t assume.
Notify Team
Report client name, address, scheduled time immediately.
Contact Client
Call within minutes; if no answer, voicemail + text.
Wait Window
Wait up to a defined time (~15 minutes). If access, proceed; if not, confirm lockout and move on.
Apply Lockout Policy
Apply fee per policy; define timing of charge and dispute handling.
Compensate Cleaner
Pay for wait time with a fair rate and cap.
Follow-Up with Client
Send summary, fee (if applicable), and rescheduling options.
Log Incident
Record client, date, outcome, fees, and notes.
