Skool Lesson: FOR CLEANERS: Cleaner Schedule SOP

Objective: Learn how to set and update your availability in Booking Koala to avoid scheduling conflicts and help the team plan efficiently.



🗣 Why This Matters





Accurate availability ensures clients get the time slots they need.



Helps prevent double-bookings and conflicts.



Keeps the team running smoothly and professionally.



✨ Key Principles





Default availability: Your baseline schedule for the week.



Weekly updates: Adjust for vacations, appointments, or special circumstances.



Advance notice: Updates should be made at least one week in advance.



Emergency changes: Notify the team if last-minute adjustments are necessary.



💡 Step-by-Step Workflow





Set Default Availability:





Open the Booking Koala app → click the menu → Manage Availability → Change Availability → Default Availability.



Include all days/times you are consistently available.



This serves as your baseline for scheduling.



Update Weekly Availability:





Open Booking Koala → Manage Availability → Change Availability → Specific Date.



Review upcoming weeks and adjust for extra availability or days you cannot work.



Notify the team as soon as possible for any significant time off.



Best Practices:





Fridays are the preferred day to update your schedule, but you can update anytime.



Do not change availability within 72 hours of a scheduled cleaning unless it’s an emergency.



Always try to make updates at least one week in advance.



✅ Summary





Keep default availability accurate.



Update weekly availability every Friday for the upcoming week.



Notify the team early for time off or emergencies.



Maintain professional scheduling habits to avoid conflicts.
