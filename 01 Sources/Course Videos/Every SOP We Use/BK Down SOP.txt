Skool Lesson: BK Down SOP

Core Idea:

Your business shouldn’t stop when your software fails.

Even if your scheduling system goes offline:





Cleaners know exactly what to do



Clients stay informed and confident



No jobs are missed, no revenue is lost



Rule: “Your software supports your business — it doesn’t run it.”



Why This Works:





Keeps operations running smoothly



Maintains client confidence



Shows your team strong leadership



Prevents last-minute confusion and mistakes



Common Mistake:

Freezing when systems fail.

Solution: Switch immediately to a manual backup process.



The 3-Step Emergency System:





ADMIN – Gather all bookings from email and update team



CLEANERS – Follow the email schedule and report issues



RESTORE – When the system is back online, log everything and confirm accuracy







Student Version: BK Down SOP



🎯 Goal

Keep operations running smoothly and ensure all scheduled cleanings happen without disruption if your booking system goes offline.



1️⃣ ADMIN ACTIONS – IMMEDIATE

Step 1: Gather All Booking Info





Check your email inbox for:





“Cleaning Scheduled”



“New Booking”



“Cleaning Assigned”



Pull:





Date & time



Client name & contact



Address & access instructions



Add-ons



Notes



Assigned cleaner

Step 2: Create Temporary Schedule





Use a spreadsheet or your team communication channel



Include:





Cleaner • Client • Time • Address • Notes

Step 3: Notify Cleaners

Message Template:



Booking system is down. Please check your email for full booking details.

Step 4: Respond to Client Inquiries

Message Template:



Your booking is confirmed. Our system is temporarily down, but your cleaning is still on schedule.



2️⃣ CLEANER ACTIONS





Check email for assignments



Follow email details for Date/Time, Address, Instructions, Add-ons, Notes



Report missing or unclear bookings to admin immediately



3️⃣ RESTORE SYSTEM





Once the booking software is back online:





Add any manual notes into the system



Confirm schedule accuracy



Notify the team that normal operations are resumed



🧠 Key Rules





Act immediately — downtime is temporary, but client trust isn’t



Document everything — this prevents mistakes when restoring the system



Communicate clearly — both team and clients need reassurance



🔁 Quick Flow

System Down → Admin Pulls Bookings → Temporary Schedule → Notify Cleaners → Cleaners Execute → System Restored → Confirm & Log
