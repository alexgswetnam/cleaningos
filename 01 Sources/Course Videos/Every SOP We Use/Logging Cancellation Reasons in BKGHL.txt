Skool Lesson: Logging Cancellation Reasons in BK/GHL

Objective:

Learn how to properly track and record all booking cancellations so your business stays organized and client follow-ups are smooth.



🔹 1. When a Client Cancels

Who does this: Admin / Scheduler / Account Manager

Steps:





When a client cancels, politely ask for the reason.

Example:



“Thanks for letting us know! May I ask what’s the reason for the cancellation? Your feedback really helps us improve.”



Always stay professional, understanding, and friendly—your tone matters.



🔹 2. Record the Reason in Your Booking System

Goal: Keep internal records accurate.

Steps:





Open the client’s booking in your system.



Find the Cancellation Reason or Notes section.



Write the reason clearly and briefly.

Examples:





“Client rescheduled due to travel.”



“Client canceled – found another service.”



Save the update.



🔹 3. Add Notes in Your CRM (e.g., GoHighLevel)

Goal: Keep all client information consistent.

Steps:





Open the client’s profile in your CRM.



Go to Notes.



Add a note that includes:





Date of cancellation



Reason for cancellation



Your name

Example:



Cancelled on Oct 23, 2025 – Client out of town for 3 weeks. Noted by [Your Name].



Save the note.



🔹 4. Optional Follow-Up





If the reason is temporary (travel, renovation, moving), create a follow-up task in your CRM to reach out after the client’s return.



🔹 5. Quality Check





Supervisors or team leads should review cancellations weekly to ensure:





Every cancellation has a reason recorded.



Notes exist in both systems.



✅ Summary Checklist





Always get the cancellation reason.



Log it in all systems (Booking + CRM).



Include your name and date for accountability.



Set reminders for possible follow-ups.



💡 FAQ for Students

Q: What if the client complains about fees?

A: Handle politely. If it’s a serious hardship, consider waiving the fee once. Otherwise, explain the cancellation policy and escalate to the owner if needed.

Q: What if the client pays with cash or check?

A: Ask for payment through a preferred method. If they refuse, add the fee to their next service or consider removing them from the schedule if necessary.

Q: What if the client wants to reschedule instead?

A: Always clarify if they want to reschedule or cancel completely, including any future cleanings.

Q: Do cleaners always get notified of cancellations?

A: Notify cleaners only if the job is less than 7 days away. For cancellations beyond 7 days, no need to notify them.







Student Version: Logging Cancellation Reasons in BK/GHL

Purpose:

To ensure every booking cancellation is properly tracked, the reason is recorded, and any necessary follow-up is set up for potential future business. This SOP helps maintain consistent client communication, accountability, and system accuracy.

Responsible Roles:





Admin



Scheduler



Account Manager



1️⃣ Receiving the Cancellation

Objective: Capture the cancellation professionally and respectfully.

Steps:





When a client calls, emails, or messages to cancel:





Respond promptly.



Thank them for notifying you.



Ask politely for the reason for cancellation.

Example script:



“Thank you for letting us know! Could you please share the reason for canceling? Your feedback helps us improve our service.”



Listen carefully and note down the client’s exact reason.



Maintain a friendly, professional tone. Avoid debating or arguing with the client.

Tips for students:





Be empathetic if the client has a genuine issue (e.g., illness, travel).



Be firm but polite if cancellation policies or fees need to be explained.



2️⃣ Recording the Reason in the Booking System

Objective: Keep internal booking records accurate for reporting and follow-ups.

Steps:





Log in to your booking system.



Locate the client’s booking.



Find the “Cancellation Reason” or Notes field.



Enter the reason clearly and concisely.

Examples:





“Client rescheduled due to work travel.”



“Client canceled – hired another cleaning service.”



“Client postponed due to home renovation.”



Save the record.



If applicable, update the booking status to Cancelled.

Tips for students:





Avoid vague notes like “Canceled” – always specify the reason.



Use consistent wording for easy reporting later.



3️⃣ Adding Notes in the CRM (e.g., GoHighLevel)

Objective: Maintain consistent records across all systems for follow-up and accountability.

Steps:





Open the client’s profile in your CRM.



Navigate to Notes.



Record the following details:





Date of cancellation



Reason for cancellation



Your name (who recorded the note)

Example:



Cancelled on March 26, 2026 – Client out of town for 2 weeks. Noted by [Your Name].



Save the note.



If the reason is temporary (travel, renovation, moving), create a follow-up task in the CRM to reach out later.

Tips for students:





Notes in CRM serve as the official record if a client disputes anything.



Always include your name and the date for accountability.



4️⃣ Handling Fees and Policies

Objective: Ensure cancellations are handled according to company policy.

Steps:





If a cancellation fee applies:





Politely inform the client of the fee.



If it’s a first-time hardship case, the fee may be waived (check with supervisor/owner).



If the client refuses to pay:





Add the fee to their next service invoice.



If they continue refusing, the client may need to be removed from future scheduling.

Tips for students:





Always maintain a professional tone.



Document any exceptions to policy in the CRM.



5️⃣ Handling Reschedules vs. Cancellations

Objective: Clarify client intent to ensure correct scheduling.

Steps:





Ask the client if they want to reschedule this cleaning or cancel completely.



If rescheduling:





Update the booking system with the new date.



Confirm with the client.



If canceling completely:





Confirm whether future bookings should also be canceled or kept.



Update all systems accordingly.

Tips for students:





Always repeat back what the client requested to avoid misunderstandings.



Make clear notes in both the booking system and CRM.



6️⃣ Notifying Cleaners

Objective: Ensure team members are informed only when necessary.

Steps:





Notify cleaners only if the job is less than 7 days away.



For cancellations more than 7 days in advance, no notification is needed.

Tips for students:





Always double-check the date before notifying the cleaner.



Include the client’s reason for cancellation in the notification if relevant.



7️⃣ Weekly Quality Check

Objective: Ensure all cancellations are recorded correctly and consistently.

Steps:





Supervisors or team leads should review cancellations weekly.



Verify each cancellation has:





A recorded reason in the booking system



A note in the CRM with date and name



Follow-up task if applicable



Provide feedback to team members if records are missing or incomplete.

Tips for students:





This step ensures accountability and helps improve client retention.



Regular review identifies patterns in cancellations for better business strategy.
