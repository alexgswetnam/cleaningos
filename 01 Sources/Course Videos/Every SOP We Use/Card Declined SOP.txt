Skool Lesson: Card Declined SOP

🎯 Lesson Objective

By the end of this lesson, you’ll have a system that prevents last-minute cancellations, protects your cleaners, and ensures you only send teams to paid/verified jobs.



🧠 Why This System Is Critical

Most cleaning businesses lose money because:





Clients cancel last minute due to failed cards



Cleaners show up and don’t get paid



Schedules become chaotic

👉 This SOP fixes that by verifying payment 48 hours in advance.



⚙️ The Core Concept (Simple Version)

You will:





Check payment 48 hours before the job



Give client 24 hours to fix issues



Cancel if not resolved

That’s it. Simple, strict, and effective.



🔁 The 48-Hour Payment Rule

⏰ 48 Hours Before Appointment





Attempt a payment authorization hold



If it fails → trigger notification + client outreach



📲 If Payment Fails

Send this message:

Message

Hi [Client Name], we attempted to verify your payment method for your upcoming cleaning, but the card on file was declined.

Please update your payment method within 24 hours to keep your booking active. Thank you!

👉 If no response:





After 30 minutes → follow up



After 2 hours → call client



⏰ 24 Hours Before Appointment

✅ If Payment Is Fixed:

Send confirmation:

Message

Thank you, [Client Name]! Your payment method has been updated and your cleaning is confirmed. We look forward to serving you!



❌ If Payment Is NOT Fixed:

You cancel the job.

Send:

Message

Hi [Client Name], we were unable to verify your payment method for your upcoming cleaning, so the appointment has been canceled.

You’re welcome to reschedule anytime once your payment method is updated.



💵 Cleaner Protection Rule

To build a reliable team, you must protect their time.

Cleaners get paid $25 if:





They already started traveling, OR



They arrived at the job

👉 This builds trust and keeps good cleaners long-term.



🚨 Non-Negotiable Rules





NEVER send cleaners without verified payment



ALWAYS enforce the 24-hour deadline



DO NOT “make exceptions” (this is where businesses break)



🧩 Implementation Homework





Set up:





Payment hold system (via your booking software)



Automated message OR manual reminder system



Create:





A tracking sheet for failed payments



A tag for “high-risk clients”



Train:





Your VA or admin on this SOP









Student Version: Card Declined SOP

📌 SOP: 48-Hour Payment Verification

1. Purpose

To ensure all bookings have a verified payment method before dispatching cleaners.



2. Timeline





Begin process 48 hours before appointment



3. Process

A. 48 Hours Before Appointment





Attempt payment authorization hold

If successful:





No action needed

If failed:





Notify client



Log issue in tracking sheet



B. Client Notification

Send message:

Message

Hi [Client Name], your payment method could not be verified for your upcoming cleaning.

Please update it within 24 hours to keep your booking active.

Follow-ups:





30 minutes → send reminder



2 hours → call client



C. 24-Hour Check

If updated:





Retry authorization



Confirm booking

If NOT updated:





Proceed to cancellation



D. Cancellation Process





Cancel booking



Notify client



Notify cleaners



Update tracking sheet



4. Cleaner Compensation

Pay $25 if:





Cleaner is en route OR



Cleaner has arrived

Log as:

“Cancellation Fee – Payment Failure”



5. Special Cases





First-time clients (optional):





Require deposit (e.g., 25%)



Repeat failed payments:





Tag as “High Risk”



Require prepayment
