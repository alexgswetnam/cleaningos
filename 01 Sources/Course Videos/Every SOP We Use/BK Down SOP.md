Quick Summary
Your software supports your business—it doesn’t run it. When systems fail, switch to a manual backup immediately. Admin pulls all booking info from email, builds a temporary schedule, and notifies cleaners and clients. Cleaners follow email assignments and report issues fast. When the system is restored, log all notes, confirm accuracy, and resume normal ops.

Core Teaching
Act immediately, communicate clearly, and document everything. Use email as your source of truth, create a lightweight schedule, and keep service running. Restore and reconcile as soon as the system returns.

Key Principles
Act immediately; don’t freeze.
Communicate clearly with both team and clients.
Document now to avoid errors later.
Step-by-Step Process
Admin Actions
Gather bookings from email (“Cleaning Scheduled,” “New Booking,” “Cleaning Assigned”).
Create temporary schedule (spreadsheet or team channel): Cleaner • Client • Time • Address • Notes.
Notify cleaners: system down; check email for full details.
Reassure clients: booking confirmed; system is down but their cleaning is on.
Cleaner Actions
Check email; follow details; report missing/unclear bookings to admin immediately.
Restore System
Re-enter manual notes; confirm schedule accuracy; notify team that normal ops resume.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Booking system is down. Please check your email for full booking details.”
“Your booking is confirmed. Our system is temporarily down, but your cleaning is still on schedule.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Waiting for the system to return instead of switching to backup.
Not documenting manual actions, causing reconciliation errors later.
Vague comms that erode client confidence.
