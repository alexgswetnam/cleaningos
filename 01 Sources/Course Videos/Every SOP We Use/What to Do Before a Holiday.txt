Skool Lesson: What to Do Before a Holiday

Core Idea:

Holidays are when most cleaning businesses lose money — not because of lack of demand, but because of poor confirmation systems.

Clients get busy, plans change, and if you don’t stay ahead…

👉 you end up with last-minute cancellations and empty slots.



Why This Works:





You confirm early → fewer surprises



You offer flexibility → fewer cancellations



You stay organized → smoother operations



The Rule:



“Control the schedule before the client cancels.”



The 4-Phase System:

1. Pre-Holiday (2–3 Weeks Before)

Reach out early and secure schedules.

2. Week Of Holiday

Double-confirm everything and align your team.

3. Day Of Cleaning

Execute smoothly despite busy conditions.

4. Post-Holiday Follow-Up

Turn seasonal clients into repeat customers.



Common Mistake:

Waiting until the last minute to confirm.

👉 By then, the client has already made other plans.







Student Version: What to Do Before a Holiday

📌 GOAL



Confirm schedules early, reduce cancellations, and keep your calendar full during holidays.



🗓 PRE-HOLIDAY PREPARATION (2–3 WEEKS BEFORE)

Step 1: Identify Key Dates





Review upcoming holidays in your area



Identify bookings scheduled on or near those dates



Step 2: Contact All Affected Clients

Message Template:



Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Company Name] 😊

We noticed your cleaning is scheduled for [Date], which is close to the holidays.

Just checking if you’d like to keep your booking or reschedule before/after.

Let us know what works best so we can secure your spot!



Step 3: Call Script (Optional)



Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Company Name].

I’m calling to confirm your cleaning on [Date]. Since it’s around the holidays, we wanted to check if you’d like to keep it or move it to a better time.

What would you prefer?



Step 4: Update Schedule Immediately





Confirmed → leave as is



Rescheduled → update calendar/system right away



No response → mark for follow-up



📅 WEEK OF THE HOLIDAY

Step 1: Send Reminder Messages (1–2 Days Before)

Message Template:



Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Company Name].

Just a quick reminder for your [Date] cleaning!

If your plans have changed, please let us know by [cutoff time] today 😊



Step 2: Internal Checks





Confirm cleaner assignments



Ensure supplies are ready



Review any special notes



Step 3: Cleaner Coordination





Cleaners confirm arrival time the night before



Handle schedule conflicts at least 48 hours early



🧼 DAY OF CLEANING

Execution Standards:





Arrive on time (or 5–10 minutes early)



Maintain professional and friendly attitude



Follow checklist as usual



Take before/after photos (if part of your system)



If Last-Minute Cancellation Happens:





Notify team immediately



Attempt to reschedule



Fill slot if possible (standby clients, waitlist, etc.)



🎁 POST-HOLIDAY FOLLOW-UP

Step 1: Send Thank You Message

Message Template:



Hi [Client Name], thank you for trusting [Your Company Name] this holiday season! 🎄

We appreciate you and hope you had a great holiday!



Step 2: Offer Optional Bonus (Optional)





Free add-on service OR small discount for next booking

Example:



As a thank-you, enjoy a free add-on service with your next cleaning 😊



Step 3: Review & Improve





What went well?



What caused issues?



What should be improved next time?



🗣 TEAM REMINDERS





Confirm schedules early



Communicate time-off requests in advance



Update all schedule changes in your system



Keep communication clear and professional



🔁 QUICK FLOW

Identify Holiday → Contact Clients Early → Confirm/Reschedule → Send Reminders → Execute → Follow-Up



🧠 CORE RULE

The earlier you confirm, the fewer cancellations you get.
