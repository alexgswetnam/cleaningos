Quick Summary
Qualify applicants on experience, location, and reliability. Interview via phone/video using a simple rubric. Complete agreements, W-9, and background checks before assigning jobs. Onboard with accounts, apps, guides, and checklists. Pair new cleaners with experienced ones for first jobs and collect feedback before scheduling independently.

Core Teaching
Structure beats guesswork: clear qualification criteria, consistent interviews, documented onboarding, and deliberate first-job pairing.

Key Principles
Qualification first.
Document everything.
Gradual, supported onboarding.
Step-by-Step Process
Qualify Applicants
Source from job boards; accept 6+ months residential experience with professionalism; reject lack of fit; clarify uncertain cases.
Schedule Interview
Offer 3 time slots in 24–72 hours; conduct via Meet/phone.
Conduct Interview
Rubric: warmth, communication, professionalism, reliability, residential knowledge; note red flags.
Decision
Hire/Decline/Waitlist; if hire, proceed to paperwork.
Agreements & Paperwork
Subcontractor agreement; W-9; background check; clear before assigning.
Onboarding
Create accounts; provide apps, guides, checklists; confirm access.
First Job Assignment
Pair with experienced cleaner; communicate expectations; observe performance.
Post-Job Evaluation
Get feedback from both; decide on independent scheduling or added support.
