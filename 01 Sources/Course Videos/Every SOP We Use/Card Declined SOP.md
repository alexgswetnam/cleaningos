Quick Summary
Start verifying payment 48 hours before the job. Attempt an authorization hold; if it fails, notify the client immediately and give 24 hours to fix. Follow up at 30 minutes and 2 hours; call at 2 hours. If unresolved by 24 hours pre-appointment, cancel the job and notify all parties. Protect cleaners with a $25 travel/arrival compensation. Set up systems to track failed payments and tag high-risk clients.

Core Teaching
Simple, strict, effective: 48-hour check, 24-hour fix window, cancel if unresolved. Never send cleaners without verified payment. Enforce consistently and document everything. Train your admin/VA to run the SOP.

Key Principles
Verify 48 hours before.
24-hour deadline to resolve failures.
No verified payment, no dispatch.
Protect cleaner time with fair compensation.
Step-by-Step Process
48 Hours Before
Attempt authorization hold. If failed: notify client and log in tracking sheet.
Client Outreach
Message immediately; follow up at 30 minutes; send reminder; at 2 hours, call client.
24-Hour Check
If updated, retry authorization and confirm booking. If not, cancel appointment.
Cancellation Process
Cancel booking; notify client and cleaners; update tracking.
Cleaner Protection
Pay $25 if en route or arrived; log as “Cancellation Fee – Payment Failure.”
Special Cases
Optional deposit for first-time clients; tag repeat failures “High Risk”; require prepayment.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Hi [Client Name], we attempted to verify your payment method… please update within 24 hours to keep your booking active.”
“Your payment method has been updated and your cleaning is confirmed.”
“We were unable to verify your payment method… the appointment has been canceled.”
