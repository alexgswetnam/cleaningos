Quick Summary
Every positive review deserves a warm, personalized response. Thank the customer, mention their city, celebrate their experience, and restate your mission/guarantee in simple language. Close with a friendly sign-off that fits your brand voice. Personalized gratitude strengthens loyalty and signals professionalism to prospects.

Core Teaching
Use a simple response flow: greeting + thanks → city mention → celebrate outcome → mission/guarantee → warm closing. Keep it human, concise, and on-brand.

Key Principles
Start with gratitude.
Personalize with city/location.
Reinforce mission and satisfaction guarantee.
Close warmly; avoid stiff corporate tone.
Step-by-Step Process
Greeting & Thank You
“Thank you for the five-star review and for trusting us in [CITY]!”
Celebrate Experience
“We’re thrilled our service made a difference for you.”
Mission & Guarantee
“Our mission is to provide reliable, detail-oriented service with a 100% satisfaction guarantee.”
Friendly Closing
“We’re glad you chose us. Blessings and thanks again — The Team.”
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Thank you for trusting us as your maid service in [CITY]!”
“We’re thrilled to hear our home cleaning service made a difference for you.”
“100% satisfaction guaranteed.”
“Blessings and thanks again, The Team”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Sounding generic or copy-paste without city personalization.
Overstuffing replies with marketing claims.
Using emojis or discounts in replies unnecessarily.
