Skool Lesson: Sales Call Outline

🎯 Lesson: How to Run a High-Converting Cleaning Sales Call

This lesson will teach you how to:





Control the conversation with leads



Gather the right information



Position your service professionally



Handle objections and close confidently



🧠 Step 1: Lead the Call (Don’t Just Answer Questions)

Most beginners lose control by jumping straight into pricing.

Instead, guide the call:



“Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. Thanks for reaching out! I’d love to help—can I ask a few quick questions so I can get you an accurate quote?”

This positions you as a professional, not just a service provider.



🤝 Step 2: Build Light Rapport





Use their name



Be conversational (not robotic)



Respond to what they say (pets, move, etc.)

👉 Important: Don’t force small talk. Match their energy.



🧾 Step 3: Gather Key Information

You are diagnosing the job—not just collecting info.

Always gather:





Size of home (beds/baths or sqft)



Last professional cleaning



Current condition (1–10)



Problem areas



Type of clean (recommend, don’t ask)



Add-ons (fridge, oven, etc.)



Pets, clutter, special notes

👉 This is what separates pros from amateurs.



🧠 Step 4: Recommend, Don’t Ask

Instead of:



“What type of cleaning do you want?”

Say:



“Based on what you told me, I’d recommend a deep clean for the first visit, then standard going forward.”

This builds authority and trust.



📅 Step 5: Confirm Availability FIRST

Before giving a price:



“When were you hoping to get this done?”

Then guide them into a slot:



“I have a 9–10 AM arrival window available Monday—would that work?”

👉 This increases close rate significantly.



💰 Step 6: Deliver the Price Professionally





Recap the job first



Give clear pricing



Pause after speaking

Example:



“For that deep clean with [details], you’re looking at $259 plus tax.”

Then stop talking.



⚖️ Step 7: Handle Objections the Right Way

Never argue. Ask questions.

Example:



“Totally understand—what’s your main concern?”

Then respond with:





Value (quality, consistency)



Flexibility (adjust scope if needed)



Fit (not every client is ideal)



✅ Step 8: Close with Confidence



“We do have limited availability—would you like me to reserve this spot for you?”

Simple. Direct. Professional.



🔁 Step 9: Set Expectations Clearly

Great companies prevent problems early.

Set expectations around:





Cancellation policy



Payment timing



Home preparation (clutter, access)



Scope limitations



🔑 Key Takeaways





Control the call



Ask before quoting



Recommend with confidence



Sell value, not cheap pricing



Set expectations early



Follow up consistently









SOP: Sales Call Outline



📥 Lead Call SOP

Opening Script:
“Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. Thanks for reaching out! I’d love to help—can I ask a few quick questions to get you an accurate quote?”



🤝 Rapport SOP





Use client’s name



Match their tone/energy



Respond naturally (don’t force conversation)



🧾 Information Gathering SOP

Collect:





Beds/Baths or Sqft:



Last cleaning:



Condition (1–10):



Areas of concern:



Cleaning type (you recommend):



Add-ons:



Pets:



Preferred date/time:



🧠 Service Recommendation SOP

Deep Clean Trigger:





Not cleaned in 6+ months



Condition 5+/10

Script:
“Based on what you told me, I’d recommend a deep clean for the first visit, then standard moving forward.”



📅 Availability SOP

Ask:
“When were you hoping to get this done?”

Offer:
“I have a [time window] available on [day]—would that work?”



💰 Pricing SOP

Structure:





Recap details



Give price



Pause

Script:
“For [service details], the total is $___ plus tax.”



⚖️ Objection Handling SOP

If hesitant:
“Totally understand—what’s your main concern?”

If price concern:
“We focus on quality and consistency so you get reliable results every time.”



✅ Booking SOP

Before confirming:





Service type confirmed



Condition confirmed



Add-ons confirmed



Schedule confirmed

Confirmation Script:
“You’re all set for [date/time]. Our team will arrive fully equipped. You’ll receive a confirmation shortly.”



💳 Payment SOP





Collect payment method securely



Prefer sending secure payment link



Inform client:
“Payment is processed after the cleaning once everything is completed.”



⚠️ Expectation Setting SOP

Say:





“If the condition differs upon arrival, adjustments may be needed.”



“Please remove clutter to allow efficient cleaning.”



“We require advance notice for cancellations or rescheduling.”



📞 Post-Clean SOP

“Hi! Just checking—how was your cleaning? Were you satisfied with everything?”



💸 Upsell SOP

Recurring:
“Most clients go on a recurring schedule to maintain results—would you like to set that up?”

Add-ons:
“We can also take care of [fridge/oven/windows] if you’d like to add that.”



🔁 Follow-Up SOP

24 hrs:
“Just checking if you still need help with your cleaning 😊”

48 hrs:
“We have limited availability this week—let me know if you’d like to secure a spot.”

Final:
“No worries if now isn’t the right time—feel free to reach out anytime!”



✅ Daily Checklist





Respond to leads



Qualify properly



Recommend service



Offer add-ons



Follow up



Confirm bookings



Send post-clean messages
