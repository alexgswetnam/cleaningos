Skool Lesson: Bad Review Expected?

Objective: Teach students how to respond professionally to negative reviews in a way that protects your brand, demonstrates accountability, and converts unhappy clients whenever possible.



🗣 Why This Matters





Every negative review is public, so your response reflects your brand.



Timely, professional replies build trust with future clients.



Resolving issues quickly can turn a dissatisfied customer into a loyal client.



✨ Key Principles for Responding





Respond quickly – ideally within 12 hours.



Categorize the review – verified client vs. unverified.



Remain calm and professional – never defensive.



Apologize when needed and offer a solution.



Document everything internally – for tracking and process improvement.



💡 Example Workflow





Monitor GBP reviews daily.



Identify type of reviewer:





Unverified client → respond politely with a request to contact privately.



Verified client → check booking details and choose the appropriate response template.



Post a public response first to demonstrate professionalism.



Follow up privately if needed via SMS, phone, or email.



Document the incident internally and flag operational issues.



Escalate if serious concerns arise (legal, safety, theft, viral attention).







Student Version: Bad Review Expected?

Step 1 – Daily Monitoring





Check Google Business Profile reviews at least once per day.



Flag any new negative reviews (1–3 stars) for immediate follow-up.



Step 2 – Categorize the Review





Unverified Client





Name/email/phone does not match internal records.



Verified Client





Client exists in the system and had a real cleaning.



Review contains a legitimate complaint.



Step 3 – Responding to Unverified Clients



Hi there! We take all feedback seriously, but we’re unable to find your name or booking details in our system. We want to make sure this concern is addressed correctly. Please reach out to us at [PHONE NUMBER] so we can help.

Thank you, and we’re happy to look into this right away.

Notes:





Polite but firm tone.



Do NOT accuse the reviewer of lying.



Document internally after responding.



Step 4 – Responding to Verified Clients





Confirm client details in your system.



Review booking info, cleaner assigned, type of clean, notes/photos.



Choose the response template based on the situation:

A. Standard Apology + Resolution



“Hi [Name], thank you for your feedback. We’re very sorry to hear about your experience — this is not the level of service we aim to provide. We have reached out privately to learn more and make this right. We appreciate the opportunity to improve.”

B. If resolved already



“Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. We sincerely apologize for the issues you experienced. We’re glad we were able to speak with you and resolve this together. Thank you for giving us the chance to make it right.”

C. If details are incomplete



“Hi [Name], thank you for letting us know. We’re sorry to hear about this and want to understand what happened. Please message us directly with your booking details so we can help resolve your concerns.”

D. If complaint is exaggerated (still polite)



“Hi [Name], thank you for your feedback. We’re sorry you felt this way. We’ve reviewed the details of your booking and would love the chance to discuss this further and clear up any concerns. Please reach out to us directly so we can help.”



Step 5 – Documentation





Record in your incident log:





Date of review



Reviewer name



Category: Unverified / Verified



Summary of complaint



Actions taken



Resolution status



Flag operational issues for process improvement.



Step 6 – Escalation Rules

Escalate to leadership if:





Claims involve theft, damage, or safety issues



Customer requests refund beyond your approval



Review is going viral or attracting attention



Legal threats are present



Step 7 – Tone Guidelines





Professional, calm, apologetic



Never defensive



Never share private details publicly



No emojis or public discounts



Match previously approved tone templates



Step 8 – Optional Review Removal





Only flag reviews for removal if:





Reviewer is not a real client



Contains profanity or hate speech



Irrelevant/spam content



✅ Notes for Students





Negative reviews are opportunities to show professionalism.



Always respond consistently.



Follow templates to maintain brand reputation.



Document every interaction for accountability and learning.
