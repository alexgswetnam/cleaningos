Skool Lesson: Daily Checklist

Core Idea:

If you don’t close your day properly, problems stack up fast.

Missed follow-ups → lost clients

Unassigned jobs → chaos tomorrow

No tracking → no growth

👉 This checklist makes sure nothing slips through the cracks.



Why This Works:





Keeps operations tight and predictable



Prevents missed revenue (leads + payments)



Improves client experience (follow-ups + reviews)



Builds accountability inside your team



The Rule:



“Don’t end the day until everything is accounted for.”



The 4 Pillars of a Strong EOD System:

1. Tomorrow Is Already Handled

If jobs aren’t assigned → you start the next day stressed.

2. Money Is Accounted For

If payments aren’t tracked → you lose money without noticing.

3. Leads Are Fully Worked

Most money is lost from poor follow-up, not bad marketing.

4. Everything Is Logged + Communicated

If it’s not tracked, it didn’t happen.



Mistake Most Owners Make:

They rely on memory instead of systems.

👉 This checklist removes thinking — you just execute.











Student Version: Daily Checklist

🧼 OPERATIONS







[ ] All cleans for this week + next week are assigned



[ ] Review upcoming bookings for missing details or notes



[ ] (Weekly) Review cleaner notes for upcoming jobs



💰 PAYMENTS





[ ] All card payments are collected



[ ] Cash/check payments are confirmed received



[ ] Payment notes are logged in system



📞 LEADS & FOLLOW-UPS





[ ] All new leads have been:





Called at least 2x



Texted at least 2x



[ ] Follow-ups scheduled for unresponsive leads



[ ] No lead left without a next action



⭐ CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE





[ ] Called/texted first-time customers after their clean



[ ] Checked for new reviews



[ ] Responded to all reviews



[ ] Posted or scheduled a Google Business update



📊 TRACKING





[ ] Leads updated in tracking system



[ ] Completed jobs logged



[ ] Notes added for any issues or special situations



🗣 TEAM COMMUNICATION





[ ] Sent summary of new leads + status



[ ] Sent summary of today’s cleans + notes



[ ] Logged any mistakes + how they were fixed



[ ] Updated team on projects or important updates



🧠 DAILY RULE

If it’s not assigned, tracked, or followed up — it doesn’t exist.

Finish everything before ending the day.
