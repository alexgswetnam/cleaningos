Quick Summary
Every negative review is public—and your reply is your brand. Monitor GBP daily and categorize reviews as verified or unverified before responding. Post a professional public response within 12 hours, then follow up privately if needed. Use clear templates: apology + resolution, resolved confirmation, request for more details, or a calm clarification when the complaint seems exaggerated. Document each incident and escalate serious claims. Flag only truly removable reviews (spam, profanity, not-a-client).

Core Teaching
Respond quickly, calmly, and professionally. Treat verified clients with accountability and solutions; guide unverified posters to private channels. Keep public responses concise and brand-safe, then move to private resolution. Track incidents internally for learning and escalate only when appropriate. Use removal requests sparingly and only for violations.

Key Principles
Respond within 12 hours.
Categorize: verified vs unverified.
Professional, non-defensive tone.
Apologize when needed; offer a solution.
Document internally; escalate serious cases.
Step-by-Step Process
Daily Monitoring
Check GBP reviews at least once per day; flag 1–3 star reviews.
Categorize the Review
Unverified: details don’t match records.
Verified: real client/job; legitimate complaint.
Respond Publicly
Unverified: polite request to contact privately.
Verified: choose the right template (apology + resolution, already resolved, request details, or polite clarification).
Follow Up Privately
Use SMS/phone/email for details and resolution.
Documentation
Log date, name, category, complaint summary, actions taken, and status; flag any ops issues.
Escalation Rules
Escalate theft, damage, safety, legal threats, or viral attention.
Optional Removal
Flag only if not a real client, profanity/hate, or irrelevant/spam.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
Unverified: “Hi there! We take all feedback seriously, but we’re unable to find your name or booking details in our system. Please reach out to us at [PHONE NUMBER] so we can help.”
Standard Apology + Resolution: “Hi [Name], thank you for your feedback… we’re very sorry… we have reached out privately to make this right.”
Resolved Already: “Hi [Name]… we’re glad we were able to speak with you and resolve this together.”
Details Incomplete: “Hi [Name]… please message us directly with your booking details so we can help resolve your concerns.”
Polite Clarification: “Hi [Name]… we’ve reviewed the details of your booking and would love the chance to discuss this further and clear up any concerns.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Responding defensively or sharing private info publicly.
Ignoring or delaying responses beyond 12 hours.
Accusing unverified reviewers of lying.
Failing to document incidents internally.
