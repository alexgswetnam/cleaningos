Skool Lesson: FOR CLEANERS: Preferred Cleaning Supplies

Objective: Teach students which cleaning supplies are essential for delivering a professional, thorough, and safe cleaning service.



🗣 Why This Matters





Using the right tools makes cleaning faster, safer, and more effective.



Color-coded or specialized supplies prevent cross-contamination.



Being prepared with the right products improves client satisfaction.



✨ Key Principles





Stick to core supplies whenever possible.



Use specialized products for kitchens, bathrooms, and floors.



Optional tools can make your work more efficient and precise.



Always follow manufacturer instructions and safety guidelines.



💡 Recommended Supplies

🧽 General Cleaning





Microfiber cloths (different colors)



Dusting mitts



Disposable dust cloths



All-purpose cleaner



Glass cleaner (streak-free)



Furniture polish



Disinfectant spray/wipes



Magic erasers



Vacuum cleaner with attachments



Mop (preferably washable microfiber heads)



Broom and dustpan



Bucket for water/solutions



Step stool for high-reach areas



Trash bags (various sizes)



Laundry basket

🍴 Kitchen





Degreaser (stovetops, ovens, hoods)



Stainless steel cleaner (appliances)



Countertop cleaner (granite, quartz, other)



Dish soap



Non-scratch scrub sponges



Detailing brushes (grout, vents, tight spots)



Oven cleaner



Refrigerator cleaner (interior safe)



Polishing cloths for faucets/fixtures

🛁 Bathroom





Tile and grout cleaner



Toilet bowl cleaner + dedicated brush



Shower cleaner (soap scum/hard water)



Mildew remover



Scrub brushes (small and large)



Rubber gloves

🧹 Floor Care





Vacuum cleaner



Hardwood floor cleaner



Tile floor cleaner



Mop cleaning solution

🧼 Optional But Useful





Handheld portable vacuum



Caddy/organizer for supplies



Spray bottles (custom solutions)



Face masks (strong chemicals)



Knee pads



Sticky roller brushes (blinds, delicate fabrics)









Student Version: FOR CLEANERS: Preferred Cleaning Supplies

1️⃣ General Supplies





Always bring microfiber cloths (color-coded) and dusting tools.



Use all-purpose, disinfectant, and furniture cleaners as needed.



Vacuum, mop, broom, dustpan, bucket, step stool, trash bags, laundry basket.



2️⃣ Kitchen Supplies





Use degreaser, stainless steel cleaner, and countertop cleaners.



Bring dish soap, non-scratch sponges, detailing brushes.



Oven/refrigerator cleaners and polishing cloths.



3️⃣ Bathroom Supplies





Tile and grout cleaners, shower and mildew cleaners.



Toilet cleaner + dedicated brush.



Rubber gloves and scrub brushes (small + large).



4️⃣ Floor Care





Vacuum for all surfaces.



Floor-specific cleaners (hardwood, tile).



Mop solution.



5️⃣ Optional Tools





Handheld vacuum for detail work.



Supply caddy/organizer.



Spray bottles for custom solutions.



Knee pads and masks if desired.



Sticky roller brushes for delicate surfaces.
