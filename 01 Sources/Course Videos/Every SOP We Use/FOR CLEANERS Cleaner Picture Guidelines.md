Quick Summary
For first-time cleans, take wide before photos at the start and matching after photos at the end. Use good lighting, hold the camera steady in landscape, and clear clutter before “after” shots. Photograph damage or uncleanable areas anytime. Upload all images to Booking Koala Job Media.

Core Teaching
Clear, consistent photo documentation builds trust, protects your work, and supports training.

Key Principles
First-time cleans: before and after with matching angles.
Quality standards: lighting, steady, wide-framed, clutter-free afters.
Upload everything to Job Media.
Step-by-Step Process
Before Photos
2–3 wide shots per room after starting; focus on visibly dirty areas.
After Photos
End of clean, during final walkthrough; match angles; stage surfaces.
Damage/Uncleanable
Photograph and send to office if needed.
Quality Standards
Natural/sufficient light; steady, landscape; step back; remove clutter from afters.
Submission
Upload all to Booking Koala Job Media; verify clarity and framing.
