Quick Summary
Lead the conversation before you quote. Ask essential questions (home size, type, condition, last clean, schedule, add-ons), then position value—not just price. Qualify for fit and close confidently with limited-availability language. Follow up at 24 hours, 48 hours, and with a final message. This keeps pricing accurate, clients aligned, and close rates higher.

Core Teaching
Control the call, gather the right info, recommend the right service, and sell value clearly. Qualify the client, set expectations, and follow up consistently.

Key Principles
Ask before quoting.
Sell value over hourly rates.
Close with confident leadership.
Follow up more than once.
Step-by-Step Process
Lead Response
“Hi! This is [Name] with [Company]… may I ask a few quick questions to get you an accurate quote?”
Qualification
Gather beds/baths, cleaning type, condition 1–10, last clean, schedule, add-ons.
Value Framing
“We price based on scope so you know exactly what to expect.”
Client Fit
Prefer respectful, clear, flexible; be cautious with price-only, unrealistic expectations.
Booking Confirmation
Confirm service, condition, add-ons, and schedule; send confirmation message.
Expectations
Note potential adjustments if condition differs.
Follow-Up
24 hrs check-in; 48 hrs scarcity; final open door.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“We don’t charge hourly; we price based on scope so everything is clear upfront.”
“We do have limited availability for your date—would you like me to reserve this slot for you?”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Quoting without proper discovery.
Competing on hourly price instead of value and scope.
Skipping follow-ups.
