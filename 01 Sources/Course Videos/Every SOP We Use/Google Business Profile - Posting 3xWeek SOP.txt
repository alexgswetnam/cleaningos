Skool Lesson: Google Business Profile - Posting 3x/Week SOP

Lesson Objective

Learn how to post consistently on your Google Business Profile to improve local visibility, showcase real jobs, and drive client calls.



Why This Matters





Boosts local SEO for your city/neighborhood.



Builds trust with potential clients with real before/after photos.



Encourages direct bookings via the “Call now” button.



Maintains professional and consistent branding.



Posting Cadence





3 posts per week (Mon / Wed / Fri)



80% updates (services, tips, testimonials)



20% offers (discount ≤15%)



Photo Tips





Always use real job photos. Avoid stock images.



Take before & after shots. Focus on cleaned rooms.



Avoid faces, family items, documents, or anything sensitive.



Rename images consistently: city_service_cleaningbiz_001.jpg.



Copywriting Tips





Include city + neighborhood/landmark for SEO.



Focus on one service per post.



Friendly, professional tone.



CTA: “Tap Call now to book.”



Limit: 1 emoji, 650–900 characters.



Offer Post Rules





Discount ≤15%, short validity dates.



Include one condition (“new clients only”).



Never put a price or phone number in text.



Post Structure





Hook/Title



Body (benefit/service/solution)



CTA: Tap Call now to book



Hashtags (optional)



1–3 real photos



Student Tip

Track photo usage in a sheet to avoid repeating images across posts. Review post performance monthly and save your best posts as templates.
