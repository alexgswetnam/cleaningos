Skool Lesson: How to Handle a Re-Clean

🧠 The Goal

When a client complains, your team should:





Fix legitimate issues quickly



Protect your reputation



Avoid unnecessary refunds



Hold cleaners accountable (without creating chaos)

If you don’t have a system for this, you’ll get:





Refund leaks



Bad reviews



Inconsistent service quality



Team frustration



⚙️ The Core Principle



Every complaint is either resolved, clarified, or escalated — never ignored.



🔁 The 4-Step Re-Clean System

1. Triage the Issue (How big is it?)

Ask:



“Is this a quick fix or a real service failure?”





Minor issues (small miss, quick touch-up):





Offer to handle it on the next visit (if the client is okay with that)



Apologize and document it



Larger issues (noticeable missed areas, multiple items):





Move to full re-clean process



2. Gather Context (Don’t Skip This)





Ask for photos (politely, not aggressively)



Identify:





What was missed



Which areas



Expected vs delivered result

👉 This protects you from:





Miscommunication



Unfair refund requests



Repeating the mistake



3. Offer a Solution (Default = Re-Clean)

Your first option should almost always be:



Fix the work, not refund the money





Offer 2–3 time windows within your guarantee period (e.g., 48 hours)



Confirm:





Access (lockbox, home, code)



Pets



Special instructions



4. Handle Exceptions Smartly

If client wants a refund instead:





Allow small refunds within a defined team limit



Escalate larger refunds to management

If client delays too long:





After a few days, conditions change (dust, usage, etc.)



Offer a partial refund instead of a re-clean



⚠️ When the Guarantee Does NOT Apply

Examples:





Pre-existing damage or permanent stains



Client declined necessary add-ons (deep cleaning, etc.)



Property condition was misrepresented



Access or utilities prevented proper cleaning

👉 Important:

Use this fairly, not defensively. The goal is trust, not avoidance.



🧾 Logging (THIS IS WHY YOU IMPROVE)

Every complaint should be tracked:





What went wrong



Who handled it



Resolution

👉 Patterns = training opportunities



🔥 Pro Tip

The best cleaning companies don’t avoid mistakes…

They recover better than competitors.









Student Version: How to Handle a Re-Clean

🧾 RE-CLEAN & COMPLAINT HANDLING SOP



Step 1: Confirm Eligibility

Re-clean requests must:





Be reported within your guarantee window (example: 48 hours)



Be related to missed or incomplete work based on scope



Step 2: Triage the Issue

Determine severity:

Minor Issue (quick fix):





Apologize



Offer to fix on next cleaning (if client agrees)



Add note to next booking

Larger Issue:





Proceed to full re-clean process



Step 3: Collect Details





Ask client for:





Photos (optional but recommended)



Description of missed areas



Review original scope of work



Step 4: Offer Re-Clean





Offer 2–3 time windows within guarantee period



Confirm:





Access method



Pets



Special instructions



Step 5: Handle Refund Requests

If client requests refund instead:





Approve refunds up to your company’s defined limit



Escalate larger refunds to a manager



Step 6: Assign Cleaner

Option A: Original cleaner returns





Schedule re-clean



Clearly list what must be corrected

Option B: New cleaner assigned





Schedule replacement cleaner



Ensure job is worth their time (fair compensation)



Follow company policy for handling original cleaner accountability



Step 7: Confirm with Client

Send confirmation including:





Date/time window



What will be re-cleaned



Access instructions



Step 8: Apply Limitations (If Needed)

Re-clean or refund may not apply if:





Issue is outside agreed scope



Pre-existing damage or stains



Required add-ons were declined



Cleaning was blocked by access, clutter, or utilities

Use judgment and communicate clearly.



Step 9: Log the Issue

Record in your system:





Client name



Cleaner



Issue



Date



Resolution



Notes/lesson



🚨 Rules





Always lead with fixing the problem



Do not jump to refunds immediately



Do not ignore small issues



Do not over-deny valid complaints



Always document everything



✅ Completion Checklist





[ ] Issue triaged (minor vs major)



[ ] Photos/details collected



[ ] Re-clean offered or scheduled



[ ] Refund handled (if applicable)



[ ] Booking updated



[ ] Client confirmed



[ ] Issue logged



🧠 Why This Works

This system:





Protects your brand



Reduces refunds



Improves cleaner performance



Creates consistency across your team
