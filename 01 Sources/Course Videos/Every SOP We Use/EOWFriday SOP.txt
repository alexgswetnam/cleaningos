Skool Lesson: EOW/Friday SOP

🧠 The Goal

This system ensures:





Your schedule is clean going into the next week



Your cleaners are aligned and available



You avoid last-minute issues



Your time estimates get more accurate over time

If you skip this, you’ll get:





Scheduling mistakes



No-shows or confusion



Underpaid or overpaid jobs



Constant firefighting



⚙️ The Core Principle



Fix next week before it starts.

High-level operators don’t react to problems…

They prevent them every week.



🔁 The Weekly Friday System (2 Sections)

You’ll run this every Friday before the end of day.



1. Cleaner Check (Team Alignment)

Step 1: Confirm Availability





Ensure all cleaners are:





Available for their assigned jobs



Aware of upcoming bookings

You can do this:





Manually (text/call)



Or using an automated reminder system

👉 Goal: No surprises next week



Step 2: Update Payment Tracking





Make sure your payment tracking sheet is fully up to date



Include:





Completed jobs



Time spent (if tracked)



Pay amounts



Any adjustments

👉 This ensures:





Accurate payouts



Better job pricing over time



2. Schedule Check (Prevent Problems)



Step 1: Audit Next Week’s Schedule

Go through every booking for the upcoming week:

Check for:





Scheduling conflicts



Incorrect dates (especially recurring clients)



Special instructions or notes



Clients who shouldn’t be on the schedule

👉 This step takes ~10 minutes but prevents major issues



Step 2: Review Booking Notes

For each job:





Read client notes carefully



Confirm:





Frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly)



Special requests



Access instructions

👉 This catches:





Wrong scheduling patterns



Missed expectations



Step 3: Adjust Time Estimates

For recurring clients:





Look at past job duration data



Update future estimated cleaning times accordingly

👉 Why this matters:





Improves scheduling accuracy



Prevents overbooking



Protects cleaner earnings and satisfaction



🔥 Pro Tip

Most cleaning companies guess their schedules…

The best ones refine them every single week.
