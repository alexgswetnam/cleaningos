Skool Lesson: Respond to Reviews on Google

Objective: Teach your team how to reply to positive reviews in a professional, warm, and friendly tone that builds trust and encourages loyalty.

🗣 Why This Matters





Every review is an opportunity to strengthen your brand.



Personalized responses show gratitude and make customers feel valued.



Highlighting your mission and guarantees reinforces professionalism.



✨ Key Principles for Responding





Start with gratitude – thank the customer for their review.



Acknowledge their city or location – makes it feel personalized.



Reinforce your mission or promise – why your service is special.



Keep it warm and friendly – don’t be robotic.



Close with a personal touch – blessings, thanks, or team sign-off.



💡 Example Response Flow





Greet and thank the customer.



Mention their city: “Thank you for trusting us in [CITY]!”



Celebrate the positive experience: “We’re thrilled to hear our service made a difference for you.”



Remind them of your mission and guarantee.



Close warmly with a team-friendly sign-off.







Student Version: Respond to Reviews on Google

Step 1 – Greeting & Thank You



“Hey!”

“Thank you for the five-star review and for trusting us as your maid service in [CITY]!”

Step 2 – Celebrate the positive experience



“We’re thrilled to hear that our home cleaning service made a difference for you.”

Step 3 – Highlight mission & guarantee



“Our mission is to provide faith-based house cleaning services in [CITY/COUNTY] that are reliable, detail-oriented, with a 100% satisfaction guaranteed.”

Step 4 – Friendly closing



“We’re glad you chose us as your house cleaners :)”

“Blessings and thanks again,”

“The Team”
