Skool Lesson: How to Get Reviews

🎯 Lesson Objective

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:





Connect with clients after a clean 🤝



Gather feedback gracefully 💬



Confirm recurring bookings 🗓️



Encourage reviews naturally ⭐



Use a proven SOP to make calls efficient and professional 💼



🔍 Step 1: Understand the Goal

Goal: Build trust, strengthen client relationships, and encourage reviews without “selling.”

Why it matters: Happy clients leave reviews, refer friends, and stay on recurring schedules 🌟



🛠️ Step 2: Gather What You Need





Client names & contact info 📋



Access to your schedule/calendar 🗓️



Phone ready for calls 📱



Review link ready 🔗









Student Version: How to Get Reviews

Warm Introduction

Smile, use a friendly tone, and mirror the client’s energy.



“Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Business]. I wanted to check in and see how your clean went today!”



Connection & Feedback

If everything went well:



“That makes my day! I’ll make sure your cleaner knows—they’ll love that.”

If something wasn’t perfect:



“Thanks for letting me know. Can you tell me a bit more about what was missed? I’ll make sure we fix it.”



Confirm Recurring Schedule

If they have a recurring booking:



“We’ll see you on [Next Scheduled Date]!”

If they don’t have a recurring booking:



“I noticed you don’t have another clean scheduled yet. Would you like to set one up?”



Ask for a Review

Keep it casual and grateful:



“Would you mind leaving us a 5-star review? If [Cleaner’s Name] stood out, mentioning them is a great way to recognize their work!”

Send the review link while still on the call if possible.



Close With Appreciation

Thank the client sincerely and reinforce availability:



“Thanks again for trusting [Your Business], [Client Name]! I’m always just a call or text away if you need anything.”



🏆 Expected Outcome





Clients feel valued and connected 🤝



Positive feedback and reviews increase ⭐



Recurring bookings are confirmed 🗓️
