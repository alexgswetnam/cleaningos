Quick Summary
Lead the call, build light rapport, and diagnose the job with the right questions. Recommend the service, confirm availability first, then present the price professionally and pause. Handle objections with questions, set expectations early, and close directly. Follow up and offer recurring and add-on options.

Core Teaching
Professionals recommend based on discovery, anchor availability, and deliver pricing with confidence. They set expectations to prevent issues later.

Key Principles
Lead before you quote.
Recommend, don’t ask.
Confirm availability first.
Price with a recap and pause.
Step-by-Step Process
Opening
“Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]… may I ask a few quick questions…?”
Rapport
Use their name; match tone; be natural.
Information Gathering
Beds/baths or sqft, last clean, condition 1–10, problem areas, recommended type, add-ons, pets, preferred time.
Recommend Service
Deep for initial if 6+ months or condition 5+/10, then standard.
Availability
“When were you hoping…? I have a [window] on [day]—would that work?”
Pricing
Recap details, give price, pause.
Objections
“What’s your main concern?” then respond with value/flexibility/fit.
Close
“Would you like me to reserve this spot for you?”
Expectations
Policy, payment timing, prep, scope limits.
Aftercare
Post-clean check-in; recurring and add-on suggestions.
Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Based on what you told me, I’d recommend a deep clean for the first visit…”
“For [service details], the total is $___ plus tax.” [pause]
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Pricing before discovery and availability.
Arguing objections.
