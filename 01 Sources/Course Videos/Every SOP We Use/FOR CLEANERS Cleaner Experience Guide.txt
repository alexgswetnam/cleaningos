Skool Lesson: FOR CLEANERS: Cleaner Experience Guide

Objective: Learn how to provide a professional, friendly, and reliable cleaning experience that earns client loyalty and positive reviews.



🗣 Why This Matters





First impressions and walkthroughs set the tone for the client relationship.



Consistent, professional service builds trust and repeat bookings.



Taking photos and documenting work protects you and highlights your effort.



✨ Key Principles





Be prepared — check your schedule, notes, and supplies.



Be professional and friendly — greet the client warmly.



Communicate clearly — walkthroughs, expectations, and questions matter.



Document your work — before/after photos protect your work and show progress.



Follow the do-not-do list — maintain professionalism and client trust.



💡 Workflow Overview





Before Arrival: Double-check schedule, mark “on the way” in Booking Koala, read notes, contact team if there are issues.



Arrival: Bring supplies, knock/ring bell, and call if no answer within 3 minutes.



Walkthrough: Greet the client, ask questions, set expectations for difficult areas, build rapport.



Photos: Take before/after pictures for first-time cleans. Upload to Booking Koala job media.



During the Clean: Work efficiently, respectfully, and quietly. Follow Booking Koala notes.



Final Walkthrough & Goodbye: Review completed areas, invite client feedback, text if client is not home, send after photos.



Do Not Do List: Follow the rules to maintain professionalism and client trust.









Student Version: FOR CLEANERS: Cleaner Experience Guide

1️⃣ Before Arrival





Double-check your schedule. Notify the team if there’s any chance you may be late.



Mark “on the way” in Booking Koala. Clock-in upon arrival and clock-out when leaving.



Read the Booking Koala notes for client preferences or pets.



Call/text the team if there are issues with parking, gate codes, or finding the entrance.



2️⃣ Professional and Friendly Arrival





Bring all supplies to the door before starting.



Knock first, then ring the bell if no answer within 30 seconds.



If no response after 3 minutes, call the team.



3️⃣ Walkthrough





Greet the client warmly and invite them to walk through the home:



“Would you like to walk through your home with me?”

During the walkthrough:





Ask questions about cleaning preferences and areas to avoid.



Build rapport by complimenting something genuine in the home.

Handling dirtier areas:





Set expectations for larger issues.



Spend extra time on small areas if needed.



For significant issues, take a photo and text the team before starting.



4️⃣ Photos – Before & After





Before: Take 2–3 wide shots once cleaning begins.



After: Take photos during the final walkthrough using similar angles.



Upload to Booking Koala job media for reference and storage.



Take pictures of any impossible-to-clean areas or damages.



5️⃣ During the Clean





Work calmly, efficiently, and with care.



Keep noise low; no loud music or speakerphone.



Respect pets, clients working from home, and personal belongings.



Follow Booking Koala notes if unsure.



Goal: leave the client extremely satisfied — extra effort can lead to tips and re-bookings.



6️⃣ Final Walkthrough & Goodbye





Do a personal walkthrough.



If working with a team, double-check each other’s areas.



If client is home:





Highlight areas focused on and tough spots.



Invite feedback:



“I’d love to touch up anything you notice — it’s no problem at all!”





Mention bonus for reviews if applicable.



If client is not home:





Text the team that the clean is complete.



Send after photos.



🚫 Do Not Do List





❌ Do not ask how long you’re supposed to stay; check Booking Koala or call if needed.



❌ Do not use client supplies unless explicitly allowed.



❌ Do not eat, drink (besides water), smoke, or vape on site.



❌ Do not bring guests or unapproved team members.



❌ Do not take personal calls or speak loudly on the phone.



❌ Do not leave early without confirming the clean is complete and client-approved.
