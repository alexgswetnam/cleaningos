SKOOL LESSON: PRICING GUIDE

🎯 Lesson: How to Handle Leads & Close Cleaning Jobs

In this lesson, you’ll learn how to:





Respond to inquiries professionally



Qualify clients properly



Position your service (without competing on price)



Close jobs confidently



🧠 Step 1: Control the Conversation

Most beginners lose jobs because they jump straight to pricing.

Instead, you should guide the conversation.

Start with:



“Thanks for reaching out! I’d love to help—can I ask a few quick questions to get you an accurate quote?”

This positions you as a professional, not just another cleaner.



🧾 Step 2: Ask the Right Questions

Always gather:





Home size (beds/baths)



Type of cleaning



Condition (1–10)



Last time cleaned



Preferred schedule



Add-ons

👉 This ensures:





Accurate pricing



No surprises



Higher-quality clients



⚖️ Step 3: Don’t Sell Price — Sell Value

If they ask for hourly:



“We actually don’t charge hourly since every home is different. We price based on the scope so you know exactly what to expect.”

If they say it’s expensive:



“Totally understand—most clients felt that way at first, but they really value the quality and consistency after the first clean.”



🚦 Step 4: Qualify the Client

Not every lead is worth closing.





Good clients → respectful, clear, flexible



Risky clients → price-focused, unclear, demanding

👉 Your goal isn’t just to close — it’s to close the right clients.



📅 Step 5: Close with Confidence

Never sound unsure.

Say:



“We do have limited availability for your date—would you like me to reserve this slot for you?”

This creates urgency and leadership.



🔁 Step 6: Follow Up (Most People Don’t Do This)





24 hrs → simple check-in



48 hrs → scarcity



Final → leave door open

👉 Most deals are closed in follow-ups, not first messages.



🔑 Key Lesson Takeaways





Ask before quoting



Lead the conversation



Sell value, not cheap pricing



Don’t chase bad clients



Follow up consistently

💬 Pricing & Sales SOP

🚦 Client Qualification SOP

Proceed if:

Respectful



Flexible

Be cautious if:

Unrealistic expectations



📅 Booking SOP

Before confirming:





Service type confirmed



Condition confirmed



Add-ons confirmed



Schedule confirmed

Confirmation Message:
“You’re all set for [date/time]. Our team will arrive fully equipped. See you then!”



⚠️ Expectation SOP

“In case the home condition differs upon arrival, adjustments may be needed.”



📞 Follow-Up SOP

After cleaning:
“Hi! Just checking—how was your cleaning? Were you satisfied with everything?”



💸 Upsell SOP

Recurring:
“Most clients go recurring to maintain results—would you like to set that up?”

Add-ons:
“We can also include [fridge/oven/windows] if you’d like.”



🔁 Lead Follow-Up SOP

24 hrs:
“Just checking if you still need help with your cleaning 😊”

48 hrs:
“We have limited availability—let me know if you’d like to secure a spot.”

Final:
“No worries if now isn’t the right time—feel free to reach out anytime!”
