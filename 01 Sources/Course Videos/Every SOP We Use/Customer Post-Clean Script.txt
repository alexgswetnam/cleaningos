Skool Lesson: Customer Post-Clean Script

🎯 Lesson Objective:

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:





Connect with clients in a personal, relationship-first way 🤝



Handle feedback gracefully (both positive and negative) 💬



Confirm recurring schedules 🗓️



Collect payment politely if needed 💳



Ask for reviews naturally ⭐



Use this SOP directly in your business workflow 💼



🔍 Step 1: Understand the Goal

Goal: Build trust and strengthen client relationships while keeping your business running smoothly.

Why it matters: Personal connection leads to more happy clients, reviews, and referrals 🌟



🛠️ Step 2: Gather What You Need

Before making calls, make sure you have:





Client names & contact info 📋



Access to your schedule/calendar 🗓️



Phone ready for calls 📱



Review links ready (if asking for reviews) 🔗



✏️ Step 3: Student SOP – Client Follow-Up Calls

📌 Title: Client Follow-Up Call SOP

💡 Purpose: Check in with clients, gather feedback, confirm schedules, and ask for reviews—connection first, business second.

👤 Owner: [Your Name / Team Member]

🛠️ Tools Needed: Phone, Calendar/Scheduling System, Review Links

⏰ Frequency: After every clean or per follow-up schedule



Step-by-Step Instructions

📞 Warm Introduction — Set the Tone





Smile, use a warm tone, mirror the client’s energy.



Script:



“Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Business]. I just wanted to check in and see how your clean went today!”



💬 Connection First, Then Feedback

If everything went well:



“That seriously makes my day to hear! I’ll make sure your cleaner knows—they’ll love that.”

If something wasn’t perfect:



“Thank you for letting me know—that’s exactly what I want to hear so we can fix it. Can you tell me a little more about what was missed?”





Follow up with a plan to correct any issues (re-clean, adjustments, etc.)



🗓️ Confirm Recurring Schedule





Check if their next clean is scheduled:



“I’ve got your next clean set for [Date], does that still work for you?”





If they aren’t on a recurring schedule, offer to set one up.



💳 Payment (If Needed)





Ask politely if payment hasn’t been completed:



“I noticed we don’t have a payment on file. How would you like to take care of today’s clean?”





If already on file, confirm the charge and mention tips lightly if appropriate.



⭐ Ask for a Review





Keep it casual and grateful:



“One last thing—would you mind leaving us a 5-star review? If your cleaner stood out, mentioning their name really helps them get recognized!”





Send the review link while still on the call if possible.



🌟 Close With Appreciation





Thank them sincerely and make them feel valued:



“Thanks again for trusting [Your Business], [Client Name]! I’m always just a call or text away if you need anything.”



🏆 Expected Outcome





Clients feel valued and connected 🤝



Positive feedback and reviews increase 🌟



Fewer missed payments or scheduling issues 💳
