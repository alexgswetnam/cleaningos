🎯 Lesson: How to Set Client Expectations (And Prevent Complaints Before They Happen)

One of the biggest mistakes new cleaning business owners make is not setting expectations early.

This leads to:





Complaints



Refund requests



Misunderstandings



Bad reviews

Top companies don’t just clean well—they communicate clearly before the job even starts.



🧠 Why This Matters

A simple expectation message can:





Reduce issues by 70–80%



Protect your time and team



Make clients easier to work with



Position your business as professional

👉 This is one of the highest ROI systems in your business.



📩 When to Send This

Send your expectation message:





After booking is confirmed



24–48 hours before the appointment



🧾 What You Must Cover

Every expectation message should include:

1. Preparation Instructions

Tell clients how to prepare:





Remove clutter



Prepare add-ons (like oven)



Ensure access to the home



2. Scope of Work

Clarify:





What you DO clean



What you DON’T clean

👉 This prevents “I thought this was included” issues.



3. Limitations

Set boundaries:





No heavy lifting



No ladder work



No cleaning blocked areas



4. Payment Expectations

Explain:





When payment happens



How it’s processed



5. Cancellation Policy

Be clear and upfront:





Required notice



Fees (if applicable)



6. Quality Assurance Process

Let them know:





There will be a walkthrough



Re-cleans happen on the spot

👉 This builds trust and reduces callbacks.



🔑 Key Takeaways





Set expectations BEFORE problems happen



Be clear, not vague



Be professional, not aggressive



Protect your time while serving the client







SOP: Client Expectation Email



📩 Client Expectation Email Template

Subject: Your Upcoming Cleaning – What to Expect



Hi [Client Name],

Welcome to [Your Company Name]! We’re excited to take care of your home 😊

To ensure everything goes smoothly and you get the best results, here are a few important details to review before your appointment:



🏠 Before We Arrive





Please pick up clutter from floors and surfaces (toys, clothes, etc.) so we can clean thoroughly.



If there is excessive clutter that limits access, additional time or charges may apply.



Please move any fragile or valuable items as needed.



For add-ons like oven cleaning, we recommend applying a standard oven cleaner beforehand for best results.



🧹 What’s Included





Dusting, wiping, scrubbing, vacuuming, and mopping



Detailed cleaning based on the service you selected

📌 Full checklist: [Insert Your Checklist Link]



🚫 What’s Not Included





Heavy lifting or moving large furniture



Climbing ladders or reaching unsafe areas



Biohazard, restoration, or repair work

We may not be able to complete areas that are blocked by clutter, pets, or ongoing work.



💳 Payment





Payment is processed after the cleaning is completed



A valid payment method is required to confirm your booking



🔁 Satisfaction Guarantee





Our goal is to ensure you’re happy with the results



We recommend doing a walkthrough with the cleaner before they leave



If anything needs attention, we’ll address it on the spot



📅 Cancellations & Rescheduling





Please provide advance notice if you need to reschedule



Late changes may be subject to a fee depending on your policy



These guidelines help us deliver the best possible service while keeping everything efficient and smooth for you.

If you have any questions before your appointment, feel free to reach out anytime!



Best regards,
[Your Company Name]
