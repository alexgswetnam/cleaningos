Skool Lesson: Weekend Operations & Lead Follow-Up Procedure



Most cleaning businesses waste weekends.

❌ Leads go cold

❌ Calls get missed

❌ Follow-ups never happen

❌ Schedules become disorganized

❌ Monday becomes chaos

Meanwhile…

The companies growing the fastest are using weekends to:





📈 Increase bookings



📅 Fill open schedule gaps



🔁 Lock in recurring clients



⚙️ Improve operations



💰 Generate extra revenue



🚨 The Biggest Mistake Cleaning Companies Make

Most owners think:



“Weekend staff are just there to answer phones.”

That mindset costs companies thousands in missed revenue opportunities.

Your weekend team should NOT only be reacting to problems.

They should also be:





📞 Following up with leads



🗂️ Organizing the CRM



📋 Reviewing the upcoming schedule



🤝 Supporting cleaners



⚡ Preventing operational issues before Monday



🧠 The 3 Core Functions of a Strong Weekend Team

1️⃣ Reactive Support

Handling:





📞 Incoming calls



💬 Client texts/messages



🚨 Cleaner issues



👥 Customer support

This keeps operations running smoothly.



2️⃣ Proactive Sales Activity 💰

This is where most businesses lose money.

Your team should:





📲 Follow up with old leads



📅 Attempt to close bookings



🔁 Push recurring services



🕒 Fill open schedule openings

Most cleaning companies NEVER follow up enough.

That’s why simply implementing weekend follow-ups can dramatically improve conversion rates.



3️⃣ Operational Maintenance ⚙️

Use slower periods to:





🗂️ Clean up CRM data



📋 Review appointments



⚠️ Catch scheduling conflicts



🚗 Improve route efficiency



❌ Prevent double bookings



🔥 Why This Matters

A strong weekend operations system helps improve:





📈 Booking rate



🤝 Client retention



💬 Team communication



📅 Schedule efficiency



💰 Revenue consistency



✅ Key Takeaway

Your weekend operations team should function like a:





🎧 Support department



💰 Sales department



⚙️ Operations department

…all at the same time.

The cleaning companies that grow the fastest are usually the ones that stay responsive and organized 7 days a week.






Student Version: Weekend Operations & Lead Follow-Up Procedure



🎯 Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to maintain smooth weekend operations while increasing lead conversion and protecting schedule efficiency.

Weekend coverage should include:





📞 Customer support



🧲 Lead management



📅 Booking follow-up



🗂️ Schedule maintenance



⚙️ Operational organization



👥 Scope

This SOP applies to:





🧑‍💼 Operations Managers



💻 Virtual Assistants



🎧 Customer Support Staff



💰 Sales Team Members

Coverage includes:





📆 Saturdays



📆 Sundays



📞 1. Handle Inbound Calls & Messages

🎯 Objectives





🧼 Convert inquiries into bookings



🤝 Support existing clients



👷 Assist cleaning teams



❌ Prevent missed leads

✅ Expectations





⚡ Respond quickly to inquiries



📲 Return missed calls promptly



🎙️ Check voicemail regularly



💬 Maintain professional communication



🧲 2. Manage New Leads

🌐 Lead Sources





🖥️ Website forms



📍 Google Business Profile



⭐ Yelp



📘 Facebook



🔨 Thumbtack



☎️ Phone inquiries



💬 Text inquiries

✅ Required Actions





⚡ Contact lead quickly



🏠 Gather cleaning details



💵 Provide estimate or quote



📅 Attempt to secure booking



🗃️ Update CRM accurately



🔄 3. Lead Follow-Up Process

Weekend staff should proactively follow up with:





⏳ Unresponsive leads



💵 Previously quoted leads



👀 Interested prospects



🔁 Recurring service opportunities



📋 Follow-Up Workflow

🗂️ Step 1: Review Pipeline

🚦 Prioritize:





⚡ Recent leads



🔁 Recurring opportunities



💰 Higher-ticket jobs



🙋 Engaged prospects



☎️ Step 2: Contact the Lead

🎯 Goals





🤝 Reconnect with the lead



❓ Answer questions



📅 Offer scheduling support



✅ Attempt to secure booking

💬 Example Script

“Hi [Lead Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. I wanted to follow up regarding your cleaning inquiry and see if you had any questions or wanted help scheduling a cleaning.”



📵 If No Answer

✅ Required Actions





🎙️ Leave voicemail



💬 Send follow-up text



🗃️ Update CRM notes



📅 Schedule next follow-up attempt



🗃️ CRM Documentation

Each follow-up should include:





🕒 Date/time



📞 Contact outcome



📝 Notes



❓ Client concerns



➡️ Next action step



🔄 Updated lead status



📅 4. Review Upcoming Schedule

Review:





🕳️ Open schedule gaps



⚠️ Staffing conflicts



🔁 Double bookings



🚗 Travel efficiency



❌ Unassigned jobs



👷 5. Support Cleaning Teams

Assist with:





📅 Schedule clarification



🔑 Access issues



💬 Client communication



🧴 Supply concerns



⏰ Time adjustments



⭐ 6. Monitor Client Experience

Review:





😡 Complaints



❌ Cancellations



📭 Unanswered messages



⭐ Negative reviews



💔 Retention concerns



✅ 7. Review Unconfirmed Appointments

Verify:





👤 Client confirmations



👷 Cleaner confirmations



🏠 Entry instructions



💳 Payment methods



🧹 8. CRM Cleanup

Use downtime for:





🗂️ Organizing duplicate leads



🔄 Updating statuses



📝 Improving CRM notes



🗑️ Removing inactive prospects



🏷️ Ensuring accurate tagging



🚀 Productivity Expectations

Weekend staff should balance:





🎧 Customer support



💰 Lead conversion



⚙️ Operational organization
