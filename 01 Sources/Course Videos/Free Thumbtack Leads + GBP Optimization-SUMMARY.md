## Core Teaching

### Thumbtack Free Leads Hack

Thumbtack can be a profitable lead channel, but only if configured correctly. Key settings: filter job types to remove unprofitable categories (move-out cleans, post-construction, vacation rental cleanings) and filter frequency to recurring only — not "just once." Thumbtack is the one platform where you can specifically target people looking for recurring service, which you can't do on PPC or Facebook.

For budget, set it to "unlimited" — but put Thumbtack on a dedicated prepaid card loaded with only what you want to spend that week (e.g., $400). When the card runs out, Thumbtack can't charge more and just shows a "payment failed" error. But because the "unlimited" setting stays on, Thumbtack will periodically offer batches of free direct leads. In the example, 21 free leads were offered starting the next day — at \~$60/lead average, that's \~$1,800 in free leads. The trick: set budget for \~$100, keep it there, and the free-lead offer usually pops up within a day or two. Click it, use the prepaid card, and wait it out for the free leads. This cycle repeats.

Another past hack: when Thumbtack prompts "expand your service area to get free leads," you add the city to get the free leads, then immediately change your settings back to your real service area. The free leads still come.

In the early days, the business spent $6K in two months on Thumbtack and learned \~$4K in that they weren't making money on one-time cleans. After fixing settings (recurring only, capped budget, free-lead cycling), it became a strong channel.

### Google Business Profile Optimization

GBP is the most profitable marketing channel in this business. In Q4 (tracked via the tracking sheet in Skool), 115 organic leads came in from Google/web form and 67 were booked — close rates from GBP are "stupid high" because people calling from Google are high-intent. The cost is \~$70/month for the virtual office address.

**The two moves that matter most:**

1. **Get a physical address on your GBP.** Without an address, you don't have a pin on Google Maps, and you won't show up when people search nearby. A service-area business without an address is spread too thin and loses to anyone with a pin. Use a virtual office or coworking space (\~$70/month) — not a P.O. box (Google will suspend you). Call the space, ask if they allow GBP verification, sign up for a virtual mail/business address plan, go in, and do the live video verification call with Google. On the call, they'll ask you to show the office door with your business name on it, show you have access, and show the office space — then you're verified in about five minutes. The host has a full video walkthrough of this process in the group.
2. **Add the most-searched keyword + city name to your GBP business name.** Example: instead of just "The Faithful Cleaners," make it "The Faithful Cleaners House Cleaning Service Prosper." Use Google Keyword Planner to confirm the top keyword in your city (it's almost always some variation of "house cleaning service"). Businesses with "house cleaning service" in the name show up when people search that phrase, even over competitors with more reviews. Multiple real examples on the call: one member (Jock/Sherry Maids) changed just the name and started getting calls within a week and booked his first recurring client. Another (Clean It Maids in Fort Worth) added an address and the keyword, went from page four to showing up at the top, and interactions skyrocketed.

**Why address placement matters:** Your pin radiates search relevance from its location. The closer a searcher is to your pin, the higher you rank. A business spread across 50–70 miles with no pin gets occasional leads from areas where no one else is, but those leads are often too far away to profitably service. Put your pin where your ideal customers are. If your office isn't in your ideal service area, get a virtual address there instead.

**Scaling with multiple GBPs:** One GBP can only dominate a limited radius. To cover more area, create additional GBP locations with different virtual addresses in different cities/areas. Each is a different Google account with the same business name format — "\[Business Name] House Cleaning Service \[City]." You don't need a separate LLC; a DBA (\~$25) covers it. The host has two locations and is planning a third. Each costs \~$70/month for the virtual address. All funnel leads to the same website/booking system. Start each new location at zero reviews, which is the hardest part.

**Only the top 3 matter:** On Google Maps, only three businesses show in the main view. Fourth and below requires clicking "more businesses." The top three get over 50% of clicks; the rest fight for scraps. That's why this optimization is worth the effort.

### Getting Reviews (The Post-Clean Phone Call Hack)

After every first clean, call the client and check in. Script: "Hey, this is Alex, one of the managers. Just want to check in — how did the clean go today?" If positive: "Would you mind leaving us a five-star review? It takes 30 seconds. \[Cleaner name] gets a bonus, so it means a lot." Then send the review link while still on the phone and confirm they received it. Don't say "I'll send it after we hang up" — do it live, confirm they got it, thank them for doing it, and assume they will. Conversion rate is much higher this way because they've committed and have no excuse.

### Sales Tax Awareness

A side topic: both the host and a member discovered they owed sales tax they didn't know about. The host's business didn't collect sales tax for the first six months, then collected but didn't pay it monthly. They went through a forgiveness program — no interest, no fees, just had to pay back the sales tax they'd actually collected. Another member owed from 2022 onward with no penalties or fees but did have to pay back. Lesson: check your state/province for sales tax requirements immediately and look into forgiveness programs if you're behind.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Thumbtack Free Leads Setup

1. Go to Thumbtack settings → Job Types. Remove move-out cleans, post-construction, and vacation rental cleanings.
2. Set frequency filter to recurring only — no "just once."
3. Set budget to "unlimited."
4. Create a dedicated prepaid card for Thumbtack loaded with only what you want to spend per week (e.g., $100–$400).
5. Assign that card as the Thumbtack payment method.
6. When the card runs dry, Thumbtack can't charge more ("payment failed") — but the "unlimited" setting stays on.
7. Wait 1–2 days — Thumbtack will offer a batch of free direct leads (e.g., "21 free leads starting tomorrow at 11am").
8. Accept the offer and receive free leads. Repeat the cycle.

### GBP Optimization Setup

1. Find a coworking space or virtual office in your ideal service area (not a P.O. box). Call ahead and ask: "Can I do my Google Business Profile verification video at your location?"
2. Sign up for a virtual mail / business address plan (\~$70/month).
3. Put your business name and suite number on the office door (it must match your GBP name exactly).
4. Schedule the GBP live video verification call with Google. On the call, show the office door with your business name, show you have access (open the door halfway), and show the interior. They screenshot each step and verify you in about five minutes.
5. Use Google Keyword Planner to confirm the most-searched keyword in your city (almost always "house cleaning service").
6. Change your GBP business name to "\[Your Business Name] House Cleaning Service \[City]."
7. Use Local Falcon (or a similar tool) to analyze your map ranking — it shows where you rank from different search locations around your pin.
8. Start getting reviews using the post-clean phone call method (send the link live on the call).
9. To expand: open a new GBP in a different city/area with a new virtual address, new Google account, same name format. Start building reviews from zero.

### Post-Clean Review Script

1. Call the client after the first clean.
2. "Hey, this is \[your name], one of the managers. Just want to check in — how did the clean go today?"
3. If positive: "I love to hear that. I'll make sure \[cleaner name] knows. She gets a bonus for great reviews, so it means a lot."
4. "Would you mind leaving us a five-star review? It takes 30 seconds."
5. When they say yes, immediately send the review link.
6. "Did you get it?" — confirm on the phone.
7. "Thank you for doing it. It really helps us."
8. Do NOT say "I'll send it after we hang up." Send it live, confirm receipt, and assume the close.
