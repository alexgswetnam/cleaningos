If you sound like everyone else… you lose.

From the customer’s perspective, the large majority of cleaning companies feel identical:





“We do great work.”



“We’re reliable.”



“We’re affordable.”



“We have great customer service.”

Almost everyone that is running a legit business likes to say that.

For us, our simple differentiator:

We’re a locally-owned Christian cleaning company that puts customers first with a satisfaction guarantee.

Super simple and we say it immediately when we talk to people! Seems small, but it communicates a few important things instantly:





we’re not a faceless national chain



we value character + integrity



and if something isn’t right, we’re going to make it right

That last part matters a LOT, because homeowners don’t just fear “bad cleaning”…
They fear letting the wrong people into their home and regretting it. 

Tip from Objection Handling Sales Course: If someone is up front about comparing your price to another company, ask them what they are comparing; and hone in on that as a differentiator.

Here’s the truth:

You don’t need some crazy “unique mechanism” to stand out.

Just make a clear reason to trust you + proof that you’re legit + a simple next step.

And that’s why if they don’t book on the first call, we follow up with something that builds trust - not something that explains “types of cleans.”

We tested a bunch of follow-up resources…
And the best-performing one has been our Trust Builder PDF.

Because it answers the real questions leads are thinking but not saying out loud:





“Are you legit?”



“Are your cleaners trustworthy?”



“What happens if I’m not happy?”



“Will this be a headache?”



“Can I feel confident hiring you?”

A “types of cleans” PDF doesn’t solve uncertainty.
A trust builder does.

That’s why it converts better.
