Quick Summary
Test your local market: most companies respond late, sound generic, and can’t explain why they’re different. The three biggest sales differences are speed-to-lead, confident objection handling, and a clear, credible “why us.” If you’re weak on any of these, you’re losing easy bookings you already paid to generate. The fixes are simple and force-multiplying.

Core Teaching
Conversion is a process advantage. Move first, answer hard questions calmly, and articulate what sets you apart in a sentence or two.

Key Principles
Respond fast (minutes, not hours).
Script common objections; deliver consistently.
Define and use your differentiators.
