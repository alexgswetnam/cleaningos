When we did market research and reached out to a bunch of cleaning companies to see how they do it...

We realized almost everyone is doing the same thing.

Most companies take an hour+ to call or text back.
And when you’re the customer, that delay feels like:





“They don’t need my business.”



“They’re disorganized.”



“They probably won’t be reliable.”

Here’s a challenge for you.
Fill out quote forms for 20 cleaning companies in your city and watch what happens.

You’ll see the same pattern in almost half of them:
Slow follow-up, generic responses, and no real reason why they're better than any other.

The 3 BIGGEST Sales Differences We Noticed:





How fast do you respond to a lead?



How confidently can you handle sales objections?



What makes you actually different than other cleaning companies?

If you know you aren’t strong in one (or all) of these… you’re missing out on easy bookings you already paid to get.
