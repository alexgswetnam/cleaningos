Quick Summary
Copy the sheet and log leads daily: date, source, cost (if per-lead), who took it, outcome (no contact, follow-up, booked, declined, unqualified), recurring, review, cancellations with date and notes. Track transactions to produce weekly pay stubs (date, hours, pay, tips, adjustments). Use manual spend entries for PPC to compute CPL automatically; the sheet aggregates per source and shows close rates per person.

Core Teaching
Discipline beats tools. A simple, consistent data habit produces CPL, CAC, close rates, and pay summaries you can actually use.

Key Principles
Enter leads daily; sales notes only for sales-relevant info.
Mirror costs correctly: per-lead vs. manual spend windows.
Track cancellations and reasons for reactivation insights.
Step-by-Step Process
Leads Tab
Fill date/source/cost (if applicable)/owner/outcome; note cancellations and reasons; mark recurring/review.
Transactions
Each clean: date, cleaner, hours, pay, tips, adjustment notes; ties into Pay Stub by pay date.
Lead Analytics
Select month; sheet shows per-source CPL, CAC, and close rates; enter manual PPC spend by date range.
Review
Use per-person close rate to coach; compare sources by CPL/CAC; adjust budgets.
