Cleaner Picture Guidelines
🕜
WHEN TO TAKE PHOTOS:
✅
BEFORE PHOTOS
Take once you begin cleaning a room, NOT during the walkthrough.
Snap 23 wide shots that show the spaceʼs original condition.
Focus on areas with visible buildup, clutter, or disorganization (sinks, 
stovetops, tubs, counters, floors).
✅
AFTER PHOTOS
Take at the very end of the clean, during your final walkthrough.
Match the angles of the before photos as closely as possible.
Make sure everything is dry, wiped, and staged (e.g., neatly folded towels, 
smoothed bedspreads).
📷
HOW TO TAKE CLEAN, PROFESSIONAL PHOTOS
✅
DO:
Use natural light when possible — open blinds or curtains. Turn on lights if not 
enough natural light.
Hold the camera steady and horizontal (landscape mode).
Step back to get the full room or full area in frame.
Clear the space of personal items (your tools, trash bags, etc.) before 
snapping the after photo.
❌
DON'T:
Take blurry, dark, or overly zoomed-in photos.
Include personal clutter like your lunch, bags, or feet in frame.

EXAMPLES

 Bathroom Sink

🍳
 Kitchen Stove
Before: Grease spots, crumbs, dirty burners
After: Clean stovetop, no streaks, shiny metal, surrounding counters also 
wiped
🛌
 Bedroom
Before: Unmade bed, things on floor or dresser
After: Smoothed, made bed, folded blankets, neat room layout
💡
EXTRA TIPS
Take 1 wide photo of each cleaned room to show overall order and sparkle.
For move-outs or deep cleans, take photos of baseboards, oven interior, and 
inside fridge.
📤
