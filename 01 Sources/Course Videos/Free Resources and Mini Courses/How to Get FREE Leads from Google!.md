Quick Summary
Most local wins come from GBP + on-page basics. In GBP: pick the right category, keep posting, add Q&A monthly, respond to reviews within 24 hours with location + service keywords, and fill Services/Products with real variants. On-page: one H1 (service + location), logical H2/H3s for services, add LocalBusiness schema, show full NAP + embedded Google Map, and tune title/description with top “near me” terms. Proximity matters; address presence beats a broad “service area” for rankings.
