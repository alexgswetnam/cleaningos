## Quick Summary

Customers become upset when reality fails to match their expectations. The lesson teaches that expectations must be actively set during the sales process, before the clean, and after the clean. By establishing clear expectations, businesses can consistently over-deliver and reduce dissatisfaction.

## Core Teaching

People are upset when they experience an unmet expectation.

Set expectations:

* During the sales call.
* In pre-cleaning communication.
* In post-clean communication.

The goal is to establish realistic expectations that you can exceed.

## Key Principles

* Expectations drive satisfaction.
* Under-promise and over-deliver.
* The business should define expectations.
* Consistency reduces complaints.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Set expectations during booking.
2. Reinforce expectations in pre-clean communication.
3. Deliver the service.
4. Reinforce expectations afterward.
5. Exceed the expectation whenever possible.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* “People are upset when they have an unmet expectation.”
* “You must set the expectations, not them.”
* “Under-promise and over-deliver.”

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Letting clients create their own expectations.
* Avoiding policy conversations.
* Assuming expectations are obvious.
