Quick Summary
This business is acquire → convert → fulfill. Most under $20k/month struggle on acquire/convert. Treat marketing like an equation: know average ticket, payout to cleaners, overhead, CAC, and close rates by channel. It’s normal to break even or lose a little on first cleans—recurring revenue makes the math work. Track every lead and transaction; scale spend once unit economics are healthy. Different channels require different expectations (e.g., PPC vs. Meta).

Core Teaching
You can’t scale what you don’t measure. Track KPIs, test channels, and increase spend when the numbers prove out, not before.

Key Principles
Know CPL, CPA/CAC, close rates, AOV by source.
Break-even on first clean can be fine if retention is strong.
Spend more on what works; stop what doesn’t—backed by data.
