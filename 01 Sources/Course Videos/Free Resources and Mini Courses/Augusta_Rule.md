## Quick Summary

This lesson explains the Augusta Rule, which allows a business owner to rent their personal residence to their business for up to 14 days per year under certain conditions. The rent must be fair market value and the property must actually be used for legitimate business purposes. The example shows how this rental expense can reduce business profit and potentially lower taxes. The lesson emphasizes consulting a CPA or tax professional before implementing the strategy.

## Core Teaching

The Augusta Rule allows a business owner to rent a personal residence to their business for up to 14 days per year. The rent must be fair market value and the property must be used for legitimate business activities such as team meetings, planning days, vision days, or client workshops. The lesson demonstrates how this expense may reduce taxable business profit.

## Key Principles

* Use fair market value.
* Use the property for legitimate business purposes.
* Maintain documentation.
* Consult a qualified tax professional.
* Tax planning should be intentional.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Determine fair market rental value.
2. Schedule legitimate business activities.
3. Rent the residence to the business.
4. Document the rental and business purpose.
5. Review with a CPA.
