## Quick Summary

This lesson focuses on documenting work through photos. Cleaners should take before-and-after pictures on new cleans and document any pre-existing damage before beginning work. This protects both the company and the client.

## Core Teaching

Photo documentation creates protection and accountability.

Required photo categories:

* Before photos on new cleans.
* After photos on new cleans.
* Photos of any pre-existing damage.

## Key Principles

* Document the condition before work begins.
* Capture proof of completed work.
* Protect the business from false claims.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Arrive at the property.
2. Photograph relevant areas before cleaning.
3. Photograph any pre-existing damage.
4. Complete the cleaning.
5. Take after photos.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* “Make sure you're getting before and after on new cleans.”
* “Take pictures of pre-existing damage.”

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Starting without documentation.
* Failing to record damage that existed beforehand.
