## Quick Summary

An SOP (Standard Operating Procedure) is simply documented instructions for how work is done. SOPs create consistency, speed, accountability, and scalability. The lesson explains how to create SOPs, store them, connect them to real business situations, and improve them over time.

## Core Teaching

If the business lives in your head, it is not systemized.

Benefits of SOPs:

* Consistency
* Faster training
* Accountability
* Scalability

How to build SOPs:

* Document the task.
* Turn it into a checklist.
* Store it in one place.
* Use it during real situations.
* Improve it after mistakes.

## Key Principles

* Systems beat memory.
* Checklists are easier to follow than paragraphs.
* Mistakes should improve SOPs.
* Documentation increases business value.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Document a task.
2. Convert it into a checklist.
3. Store it in a central location.
4. Attach it to relevant workflows.
5. Improve it whenever mistakes reveal gaps.

## Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep

* “This is how we do it.”
* “If your business lives in your head, you don't have systems.”
* “You become the bottleneck.”

## Common Mistakes / Warnings

* Keeping processes undocumented.
* Relying on memory.
* Failing to update SOPs.
* Storing information in multiple locations.
