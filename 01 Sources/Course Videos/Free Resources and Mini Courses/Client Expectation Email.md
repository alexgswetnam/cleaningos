Quick Summary
Send a clear expectation message that mirrors your Terms of Service. Ask clients to declutter floors, avoid furniture moves, and pre-spray ovens if purchased. Clarify what’s included (dusting/wiping/scrubbing/vacuum/mop) and what’s not (organizing, ladders, biohazard, restoration, exteriors, deep stain removal, specialized services). State satisfaction workflow: walkthrough before cleaners leave to fix anything on the spot. Payment: hold placed 24 hours before; payment due immediately after. Cancellations/reschedules within 72 hours carry a 25% fee. This upfront clarity protects the relationship and your schedule.

Core Teaching
Proactive expectation-setting prevents issues. Use a friendly, direct email that spells out prep steps, scope limits, satisfaction process, payment timing, and cancellation fees. Emphasize the end-of-clean walkthrough as the moment to request any re-cleaning.

Key Principles
Clarity before arrival prevents “I thought this was included.”
Walkthrough before departure fixes issues fast.
Payment timing and holds are non-negotiable and stated in advance.
Boundaries (no heavy moves, ladders, or restoration) keep the team safe and on-scope.

Scripts, Examples, or Phrases to Keep
“Please pick up clutter from the floors… If floor clutter causes a considerable increase in time, an additional fee may apply.”
“Our team can’t safely move furniture, large, or fragile items for insurance reasons.”
“If you purchased an interior oven cleaning, please spray inside the oven the day before…”
“We ensure you are 100% satisfied with your clean BEFORE we leave your home… please do a walkthrough with our cleaner.”
“Payment is due immediately after the cleaning; we place a hold 24 hours before.”
“Cancellations and reschedules have a 25% fee if made within 72 hours.”
Common Mistakes / Warnings
Skipping the walkthrough, then asking for re-cleans days later.
Not stating limits (organizing, ladders, restoration) and triggering mismatched expectations.
Failing to warn about holds and same-week cancellation fees.

Cleaned Lesson Notes
Welcome tone, then specifics:
Before arrival: declutter floors; no furniture moves; pre-spray oven if purchased.
Not included: professional organizing, biohazard, ladders, deep stain removal, garages/patios/exteriors, specialized services (deep tile/grout, carpet shampoo).
Can’t guarantee work where access is blocked (clutter, contractors, pets).
Satisfaction: on-site walkthrough; re-clean on the spot if needed.
Payment: hold 24 hours before; pay immediately after; expect a survey call.
Cancellations/reschedules: 25% fee within 72 hours.
Encourage questions; include signature with phone, email, site.
