## Quick Summary

The lesson argues that responding to leads within 30 seconds dramatically increases the likelihood of winning the sale. The first company to reply, sound organized, and provide a clear next step often wins. A CRM with automation makes this possible by immediately contacting leads, notifying the team, organizing the opportunity, and automating follow-up.

## Core Teaching

Fast response wins sales.

A CRM should:

* Text leads immediately.
* Notify the team.
* Place leads into a pipeline.
* Follow up automatically.

The lesson shares the belief that waiting too long to adopt a CRM is a costly mistake because it delays growth and creates operational headaches.

## Key Principles

* Speed matters.
* Organized businesses win trust.
* Automation prevents lost opportunities.
* Fast follow-up captures easy sales.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Capture the lead.
2. Automatically send a text.
3. Notify the appropriate team member.
4. Add the lead to a pipeline.
5. Continue automated follow-up until contact is made.
