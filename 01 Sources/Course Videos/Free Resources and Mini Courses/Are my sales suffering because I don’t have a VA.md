Quick Summary
Responding within five minutes can make you 21× more likely to convert; 78% buy from the first responder. If you can’t hit that window, hire a VA or answering service. Give them one job: make contact and book. Provide a simple assumed-close script, have them estimate in your system, confirm timing before price, and train them on the core objections. Even 1–2 extra jobs/month typically covers a part-time VA.

Core Teaching
Speed beats price when prospects are shopping multiple companies. A trained VA gives you speed and consistency.

Key Principles
Five-minute rule; first responder usually wins.
Assumed close: confirm timing before price.
Objection basics are mandatory VA training.
