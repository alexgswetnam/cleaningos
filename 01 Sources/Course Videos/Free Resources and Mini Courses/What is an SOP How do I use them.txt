An SOP is a Standard Operating Procedure.

It’s literally just: “This is how we do it.”
Step-by-step. So anybody on your team can do the job the right way… every time.

If your business lives in your head, you don’t have systems. You have an unscalable mess.

Without SOPs, everything becomes:





“Hey what do I do in this situation?”



“How do I handle this client?”



“What’s our process again?”



“Where do I find that thing?”



“Why did they miss that area?”

You become the bottleneck.

SOPs fix that.

They create:
✅ consistency (clients get the same quality no matter who shows up)
✅ speed (training becomes 10x faster)
✅ accountability (no more “I didn’t know”)
✅ scalability (you can grow without chaos)

How do you use SOPs in a cleaning business?

Here’s the simplest way:

1) Document the task once
Next time you do something - onboarding a cleaner, handling a complaint, doing a move-out clean - write the steps down.

2) Turn it into a checklist
People don’t follow paragraphs. They follow checkboxes.

3) Store it in one place
We use Notion. Every SOP, template, script, and checklist lives here.

4) Attach SOPs to real moments
New hire? Assign the onboarding SOP.
Client upset? Pull up the complaint SOP.
Team doing a deep clean? Use the deep clean checklist.

5) Improve it over time
Every mistake becomes an update to the SOP. I look forward to mistakes, because we can learn from them and improve our system. This last week, we realized that if we sent clients the review link while they are STILL ON THE PHONE, more people left reviews...

So we added it as a checkbox in our Post Clean Satisfaction Call Script I added below - so you can learn from our mistakes.

That’s why SOPs are so crucial...

They’re how you stop relying on memory and start relying on systems.

And once you’ve got SOPs… you can finally build a business that runs without you being in every conversation, every clean, and every fire.

Someone could buy my cleaning business today, and run it themselves within a week.

That's how well we have documented every process and system in our business.
