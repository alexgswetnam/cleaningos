Quick Summary
For first-time cleans, take before photos once cleaning begins (not during walkthrough), focusing on visible buildup. Take matching-angle after photos during the final walkthrough; stage the space and ensure surfaces are dry. Use natural/sufficient light, steady landscape framing, and step back to capture the whole area. Photograph damage/uncleanable areas. Upload all photos to the designated folder (or Job Media) to protect your work and standardize quality.

Core Teaching
Quality photos are part of the job. Timing, angles, lighting, and clean staging make the difference.

Key Principles
Before at start; after at end; match angles.
Light, distance, and landscape orientation.
Remove personal items before “after” shots.
