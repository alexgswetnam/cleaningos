

If you had to drive 2 hours to get somewhere... Would you be happy, or upset?

This is the tricky part: it all depends on your expectation!

If I was expecting a drive to take 4 hours, and it only took 2 - I'm happy!

But what if I was expecting it to take 30 minutes? Now I'm upset...



People are upset when they have an unmet expectation.



This why it is EXTREMELY important to set expectations properly on your sales call, pre-cleaning email, and post-clean call.



When we implemented this into our business, we massively reduced upset customers! 



The minute someone books, we use Harvest CRM to automatically send this Expectations Email and Welcome to Our Family SMS to make sure we're all on the same page.



YOU must set the expectations, not them. That way, you are always able to EXCEED the expectation you set... This is the formula for how to under-promise and over-deliver, so you'll have customers who feel like you did even better than they expected!



In case you're wondering, yes - we also set expectations on the first call when we book someone. It's not pretty, but at the end of the call, you should have 2 or 3 sentences explaining your annoying little policies that protect you.
