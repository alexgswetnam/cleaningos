## 1. Stop Guessing

Owners guess about almost everything: what to charge, where leads come from, why
prospects didn't book, which ad produced revenue, whether a cleaner is profitable, how
long jobs take, why clients cancel, what their margins are, and which part of the
business is actually preventing growth.

Every guess makes the decision emotional. A slow week means cutting prices. A busy week
means hiring too fast. One complaint means rewriting the service. One good lead means
spending more on an unproven channel.

**The cost is momentum, not just money.** Guessing makes owners change direction before
anything has time to work. It also makes the business impossible to teach, delegate, or
improve, because nobody knows what is actually true.

## 2. Clarity Creates Momentum

Owners do not need more motivation. They move when they can see the actual problem, the
target, and the next action.

## 3. Build In Order

Every good tactic becomes a distraction in the wrong Season. Solve the constraint in
front of you before building for a future version of the company.

## 4. The Roadmap Already Exists

A cleaning business is not an unknowable creative mystery. Other owners have hit the
same categories of problems. The details vary; the path is learnable.

## 5. One Step Wins

The business does not need transforming today. The owner needs to complete the right
next step. Doing that consistently beats consuming more information, rebuilding the
plan, or trying to repair all four Engines at once.

these names a primary index across the vault — which is why renaming one later is
