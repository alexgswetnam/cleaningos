# The Four Seasons

**Survival → Stability → Scale → Harvest**

## The key structural idea

**The business is not in one Season. Each Engine has its own.**

Leads can be in Scale while Labor is in Survival. Logistics can be stable while
Leadership has barely begun. The owner's job is to identify the Season of each Engine
and work on the one currently constraining the whole system.

This is what makes the model diagnostic rather than descriptive, and it's probably the
most valuable single idea in CleaningOS. It's what lets the vault answer *"what should I
do next"* for a specific owner instead of dumping everything it knows.

---

## Survival

Roughly **$0–5k/month**, though revenue alone doesn't define the Season.

No cleaners, one dependable cleaner, or a loose list of subs they hope will be available.

The day is posting for leads, answering every call, quoting, scheduling, finding someone
to fulfill, checking quality, collecting payment — and sometimes cleaning the house
themselves when the plan breaks. Not enough volume yet to know which problems are
patterns and which are one-offs.

Worried about: the next customer, covering the next job, paying for ads, whether this
can ever be a real business.

**Their job is not to optimize. Their job is to survive long enough to prove demand,
fulfillment, and basic unit economics.**

## Stability

The result becomes repeatable. Leads don't appear only by luck. Jobs sell at sustainable
prices. Dependable fulfillment, a growing recurring base, processes that work more than
once.

**Graduated from Survival when one bad day no longer threatens the existence of the
business.**

## Scale

Volume can be added without equal amounts of owner chaos. Unit economics proven, core
workflows documented, other people carrying recurring responsibilities.

**Entered Scale when growth is adding capacity to a machine that already works — not
inventing a new machine every week.**

## Harvest

Profit, freedom, and optionality beyond the owner's daily labor. Leadership exists below
the owner. Performance is visible through numbers. The business can fund the owner's
life, other investments, a new market, or a sale.

**Reached Harvest when the company continues operating and improving without the owner's
attention as its primary fuel.**

---

## What Owners Do Too Early

Automate, delegate, and scale before they can consistently acquire and serve a client
profitably. Build giant SOP libraries before enough jobs have happened to reveal the
real process. Hire management before there's enough stable work to manage. Expand
service areas, add services, obsess over SEO, build elaborate CRM workflows — while the
phone still isn't answered consistently.

**They try to become passive before becoming predictable.** Owner freedom is an output
of a proven, documented, delegated system. It is not the first setting you turn on.
