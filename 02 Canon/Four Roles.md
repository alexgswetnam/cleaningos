# The Four Roles

**Self-Employed → Supervisor → General Manager → Owner**

Each [[Four Seasons|Season]] has a role. The role is how you move between Seasons.

---

## The Roles

| Season | Role | What you're doing |
|---|---|---|
| **Survival** | **Self-Employed** | Everything. Cleaner, marketer, salesperson, scheduler. |
| **Stability** | **Supervisor** | You have cleaners, but you're managing everything. You're the glue. |
| **Scale** | **General Manager** | Managing managers. Systems run things, not you personally. |
| **Harvest** | **Owner** | You own an asset. Someone else could step in and run it. |

---

## The Mechanism

> "You pull the business to the next stage. The business doesn't pull you."

You move Seasons by **acting like the next role up**, before the business obviously
requires it.

Keep acting self-employed and you stay in Survival — regardless of revenue. Start acting
like a supervisor, bringing cleaners on and delegating, and you move to Stability.

This is the practical version of [[Five Laws|Build In Order]]. The Season doesn't change
and then let you change your behaviour. Your behaviour changes and drags the Season with
it.

---

## Why This Matters

It converts an abstract question — *"how do I get to the next Season?"* — into a concrete
one: *"what would the next role up do this week that I'm not doing?"*

It also names a specific failure mode. An owner can be doing $30K/month and still be
acting self-employed: answering every call, cleaning when someone's out, holding every
process in their head. The revenue says Scale. The behaviour says Survival. **The
behaviour wins**, and the business stalls.

---

## Diagnostic Question

> Which role am I actually playing this week — not which one do I want to be playing?

Then: what does the next role up do that I don't?
