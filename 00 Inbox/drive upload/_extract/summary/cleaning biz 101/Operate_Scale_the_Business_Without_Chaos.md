## Core Teaching
Operate is your internal operating system. It sits alongside the other pillars: Acquire gets leads, Convert gets bookings, Fulfill delivers the service, and Operate is what makes the business stable.

When Operate is weak, the symptoms are clear — everything depends on you, cleaners need you for every question, mistakes keep repeating, quality is inconsistent, and you're always putting out fires. The business can't function without your constant attention.

The goal of Operate is simple: the business runs the same way every time, even when you're not paying attention.

Operate is built on four things: communication, SOPs (how we do things), quality control, and reporting rhythms.

The real-world proof: when the owner got nose surgery in mid-December, they didn't even look at the cleaning business for almost three weeks. They knew the VA and cleaners would follow the right systems — and if they needed help, they would ask. That's what a strong Operate function makes possible.
