## Core Teaching
Old leads are not dead leads, and this point is huge. Some of the easiest bookings come from three sources: leads that didn't book two months ago, old one-time clients who rebook later, and leads that went quiet and then responded months later.

That's exactly why long-term follow-up matters. Your CRM should be checking in automatically over time, so these contacts keep getting touched without manual effort. The logic is simple: if you paid for a lead once, why would you throw it away?

The mindset shift is that your database is an asset. Most cleaning businesses treat it like trash — and that's where the easy bookings get lost.

## Key Principles
- Old leads are not dead leads.
- Some of the easiest bookings come from old or quiet leads and past one-time clients.
- Long-term follow-up is what captures these bookings.
- Your CRM should check in automatically over time.
- A lead you already paid for shouldn't be thrown away.
- Your database is an asset, not trash.
