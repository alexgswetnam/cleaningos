## Quick Summary

# Wave Your Money Scoreboard (Keep It Simple)

We use Wave as our finance tracking tool.



You don't need fancy reports.

Wave’s job is to make sure every transaction ends up in a category so you can see:





How much came in



Where it went



What you actually kept



At a high level, you want categories that answer:





What did we spend on labor?



What did we spend on marketing?



What did we spend on software?



What’s left?
