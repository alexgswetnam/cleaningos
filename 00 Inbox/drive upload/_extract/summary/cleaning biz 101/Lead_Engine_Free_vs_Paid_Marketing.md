## Core Teaching
Most cleaning businesses don't actually have a lead engine. If you want to operate like a real business and not a side hustle, you need inbound leads showing up consistently — even when you're busy, tired, or not in the mood to "go hunt."

There are really only two buckets for your lead engine: free marketing and paid marketing.

**Free marketing** includes posting in Facebook groups, asking friends for referrals, commenting and networking, word of mouth, and organic posts. Free is fine, but it's unpredictable. It depends on your energy, your time, and your motivation. Miss a week and leads slow down. Get busy and leads slow down. Free marketing depends on manual effort rather than a true lead engine.

**Paid marketing** is where a cleaning business starts acting like a machine. It's you deciding: "I want leads every day, so I'm going to pay for attention." Paid marketing gives you something free marketing almost never can — control. Specifically, control over how many people see you, control over consistency, and control over scale.

The hard truth: if you don't have paid marketing, you don't really have a lead engine — you have a social strategy. A social strategy can help, but it can't carry the business.

The goal of this section is simple: get you thinking like an operator. You need predictable inbound lead flow.
