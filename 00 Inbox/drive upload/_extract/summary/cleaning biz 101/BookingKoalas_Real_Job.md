## Core Teaching

BookingKoala is the fulfillment source of truth. The rule is simple: if a job is quoted or booked, BookingKoala is where you trust the scheduling and pricing details to live. It is the single place that holds what's real about a job.

The problem it solves is a common failure pattern — owners try to run scheduling in their heads, in texts, or in random calendars. That's when the business starts breaking. Memory and scattered tools don't scale.

In this system, BookingKoala is responsible for pricing and packages (so quoting stays consistent), calendar availability and scheduling rules, booking confirmations, reminder texts and emails, payment-charged notifications, the recurring scheduling structure, and giving cleaners visibility into their pay and schedule through the BookingKoala app. The whole point is that it prevents your business from being run by memory.

## Key Principles

* BookingKoala is the single fulfillment source of truth for quoted and booked jobs.
* Scheduling and pricing details should live in one trusted system, not in your head, texts, or random calendars.
* Running the business by memory is what causes it to break.
* Consistent quoting comes from pricing and packages living in the system.
* The system should handle confirmations, reminders, and payment notifications automatically.
* Recurring work needs a defined scheduling structure.
* Cleaners should be able to see their own pay and schedule through the app.
