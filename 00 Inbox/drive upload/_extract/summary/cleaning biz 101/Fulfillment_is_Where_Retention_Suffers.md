## Core Teaching
Most people think retention starts after the clean. It doesn't. It starts the moment a client sees your website or speaks to you on the phone. The whole experience leading up to and around the service is what determines whether they come back.

When the client experience is smooth — confirmations are clear, reminders are consistent, arrival feels professional, and expectations are set — you automatically increase repeat bookings, reviews, and referrals. Those outcomes aren't separate goals you chase later; they're byproducts of a smooth experience.

That's why Fulfill is one of the biggest "silent" drivers or killers of retention. It works quietly in either direction — either steadily building loyalty or steadily eroding it without an obvious single cause.

In this business, many clients have applauded the outstanding communication. That communication is a big part of what makes the business different and gives it a more luxury feeling.

## Key Principles
- Retention starts at the first touchpoint (website or phone call), not after the clean.
- A smooth client experience automatically increases repeat bookings, reviews, and referrals.
- Clear confirmations, consistent reminders, professional arrival, and set expectations are the drivers.
- Fulfill is a "silent" driver or killer of retention — it works either way without being obvious.
- Outstanding communication is a differentiator that creates a more luxury feeling.
