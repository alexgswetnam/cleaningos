## Core Teaching
Paying cleaners shouldn't be a weekly nightmare — but it was, until it was kept simple.

The core setup is to use a bank that supports Zelle, because that makes payouts fast and simple. The goals with paying cleaners are straightforward: pay accurately, pay on time, keep it easy to track, and reduce back-and-forth.

To handle accuracy and tracking, the Free Tracking Sheet in the Free Resources module produces a pay stub showing how many hours each cleaner worked and how much to pay them at the end of every week.

The specific bank used here is PNC Bank, and they raised the Zelle limit after a month of using them — useful to know if payout limits become a constraint as you scale.

## Key Principles
- Paying cleaners should be simple, not a weekly nightmare.
- Use a bank that supports Zelle for fast, simple payouts.
- Pay accurately.
- Pay on time.
- Keep it easy to track.
- Reduce back-and-forth.
- Use a tracking sheet that produces a weekly pay stub (hours worked + amount owed).
