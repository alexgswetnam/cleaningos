* Strangers never raising their hand because there's no clear next step offered.

## Quick Summary

Acquire is the part of the business that functions as your lead engine. Its job is to turn strangers into people interested enough to raise their hand. This happens by getting in front of the right people, building trust quickly, and giving them a clear next step like a call, quote request, or booking link. When Acquire is weak, the whole business feels stressful because you're always trying to find business rather than working a system. The goal is predictable lead flow. That means knowing where leads come from, knowing what a good lead looks like, and having a clean path from attention to contact info.

## Core Teaching

Acquire is your lead engine — the part of the business responsible for making strangers interested enough to raise their hand. It works through three moves: getting in front of the right people, building trust quickly, and giving them a clear next step (a call, a quote request, a booking link, or similar).

The reason this matters is that a weak Acquire function makes everything else feel stressful. Without it, you're constantly trying to "find business" instead of simply working the system you've built. A strong lead engine replaces that scramble with predictability.

Predictable lead flow comes down to three things you should be able to answer: you know where your leads come from, you know what a "good lead" looks like, and you have a clean path for turning attention into contact info.

## Key Principles

* Acquire exists to turn strangers into interested people who raise their hand.
