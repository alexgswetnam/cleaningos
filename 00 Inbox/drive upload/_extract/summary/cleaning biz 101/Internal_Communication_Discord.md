## Core Teaching
Discord is used for internal communication and daily monitoring. The goal is simple and comes down to consolidation: one place for cleaner communication, one place for daily reporting, and one place for issues and escalations.

The problem this solves is scatter. When communication is spread between texts, calls, and random DMs, you lose visibility, accountability, and consistency. Discord fixes that by giving you a single "ops room" where everyone knows where to go.

To roll this out, the team created an SOP for moving cleaners to Discord, and new cleaners are now given that same SOP as part of onboarding.

Beyond communication, the team uses Zapier to connect BookingKoala and HarvestCRM, so they can quickly see how many leads came in today, how the cleans went, and how many new bookings they got — turning Discord into a live daily monitoring hub, not just a chat tool.

## Key Principles
- Consolidate communication into one place: cleaner communication, daily reporting, and issues/escalations.
- Scattered communication (texts, calls, random DMs) costs you visibility, accountability, and consistency.
- A single "ops room" means everyone knows where to go.
- Standardize onboarding with an SOP that every new cleaner receives.
- Connect your systems (via Zapier) so daily numbers are visible at a glance.
- Daily monitoring matters: leads in, how cleans went, new bookings.
