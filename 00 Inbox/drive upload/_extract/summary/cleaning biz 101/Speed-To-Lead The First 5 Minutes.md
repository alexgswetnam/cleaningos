If a lead hits your website or calls your phone, and they don’t hear back quickly, one of two things happens:





they book someone else



they cool off and become harder to close



This is why your CRM matters so much!



Even if you can’t personally respond instantly, your CRM can:





send an immediate auto-text



send a missed call text



send an email confirmation



create a task/reminder so you actually follow up



Speed-to-lead isn’t a “nice to have.” It will drastically increase your close rate!!

The sooner you use a good CRM, the better. We missed out on hundreds if not thousands of dollars by trying to penny pinch and wait to get our CRM.



Even if you aren't using a CRM (which you should be) make sure you are responding to new leads WITHIN SECONDS! Call them ASAP!!!
