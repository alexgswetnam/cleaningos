## Core Teaching
The team is moving its main communication platform from Google Voice to Discord. The reasons: Google Voice doesn't send message notifications reliably, so important messages get missed; Discord notifies instantly on all devices (phone, tablet, computer); and Discord lets the team organize conversations into channels for different topics (like job updates, supplies, and scheduling). Google Voice is still kept for phone calls because it's faster and more convenient for calling, though cleaners can also be called on their Discord accounts.

The rest of the document is a setup walkthrough for cleaners: installing the app, creating or logging into an account with a clearly identifying username, joining the team server via an invite link, turning on notifications (both in Discord and in the phone's system settings), learning the core actions (sending messages, sending photos/videos, tagging people, replying), and understanding that Google Voice remains the tool for quick calls. It ends with usage habits: keep both apps installed, check Discord daily before and after jobs, scroll up to find missed updates, and ask for help with setup.

## Key Principles
- Reliable, instant notifications matter — that's the main reason for switching to Discord.
- Organize communication by topic using channels (job updates, supplies, scheduling, etc.).
- Use the right tool for the job: Discord for messaging, Google Voice for quick calls.
- Cleaners should use a username that clearly identifies who they are.
- Notifications must be enabled both inside Discord and in the phone's system settings.
- Check Discord daily, especially before and after jobs, so nothing is missed.
- All updates stay in the channels, so missed messages can be found by scrolling up.

## Step-by-Step Process
Setting up Discord (for cleaners):

1. **Install Discord.** On your phone, go to the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android), search for "Discord," and tap Install/Get.
2. **Create or log into your account.** Open the app, tap "Register" (or "Log In" if you have an account). Register with your work email or phone number. Choose a username that clearly identifies you (e.g., "Maria C. – Cleaner").
3. **Join the team server.** Once logged in, tap the "+" icon on the left side to add a server, tap "Join a Server," and enter the invite link provided (ask your supervisor if you don't have it). The team server will then appear in your sidebar.
4. **Turn on notifications.** Tap the server name at the top, select Notifications → choose All Messages. Do this for important channels (like "Announcements" or "Daily Jobs"). Also check your phone's Settings → Notifications → Discord → Allow Notifications.
5. **Learn the basics.** Send a message by tapping a channel and typing. Send photos/videos with the "+" button next to the text box. Tag someone by typing their name to get their attention. Reply by holding down a message and choosing Reply.
6. **Know when to use Google Voice.** For quick calls, still use Google Voice (e.g., "I'm at the location and need to confirm the key code"). Keep it installed and signed in — it's used for voice calls only.

## Common Mistakes / Warnings
- Relying on Google Voice for messages (notifications are unreliable and messages get missed).
- Not enabling notifications inside Discord and in the phone's system settings — leading to missed messages again.
- Using a vague username that doesn't identify the cleaner.
- Uninstalling Google Voice — it's still needed for voice calls.
- Not checking Discord daily, especially before and after jobs.
- Panicking over a missed message instead of scrolling up to find it in the channel.
