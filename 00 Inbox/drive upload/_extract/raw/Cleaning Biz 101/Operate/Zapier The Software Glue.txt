Zapier is not a “system.”


It’s the glue between systems.



Every business without a developer (or even with a developer) uses Zapier as their glue when multiple softwares have to communicate.



Examples of what Zapier does for us:





New Lead Form Submitted → New Lead in Harvest CRM



New Booking in BookingKoala → Update Harvest CRM Pipeline + Send Discord Notification



Every Friday -> Send a Text to Cleaners to Update Their Schedule for Next Week



The goal is not to build a thousand zaps!!
You just remove the few manual steps that create the most errors or that you often forget, and let the glue make everything run smooth.
