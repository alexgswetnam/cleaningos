🧹 Team Communication Update: Switching to Discord
📣 Why Weʼre Making the Switch
Weʼre moving our main communication platform from Google Voice to Discord.
Hereʼs why:
Google Voice doesnʼt send message notifications reliably — we often miss
important messages.
Discord notifies instantly on all devices (phone, tablet, or computer).
Discord also lets us organize conversations better — we can have channels for
different topics (like job updates, supplies, scheduling, etc.).
We will still use Google Voice for phone calls, since itʼs faster and more
convenient for calling but you can also call us to our Discord accounts.
🪜 Step-by-Step: Setting Up Discord

Step 1: Install Discord
On your phone:
Go to the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store Android).
Search for “Discord”.
Tap Install or Get.
Step 2: Create or Log Into Your Account
Open the app.
Tap “Register” if you donʼt have an account, or “Log In” if you already do.
Use your work email or phone number to register.
Choose a username that clearly identifies you (e.g., Maria C. – Cleaner).
Step 3: Join Our Team Server
Once youʼre logged in, look for the “+” icon on the left side of the screen (to
add a server).
Tap “Join a Server”.
Enter the invite link weʼll provide (ask your supervisor if you donʼt have it yet).
Youʼll now see our team server appear in your sidebar.
Step 4: Turn On Notifications
To make sure you never miss messages again:
Tap the server name at the top.
Select Notifications

Do this for any important channels (like “Announcements” or “Daily Jobs”).
On your phone, also check your phoneʼs Settings

Step 6: When to Use Google Voice
If you need to call someone quickly, still use Google Voice.
Example: “Iʼm at the location and need to confirm the key code.”
Keep the app installed and signed in — weʼll still use it for voice calls only.
