This part of the business is your internal operating system.



Acquire gets leads.
Convert gets bookings.
Fulfill delivers the service.
Operate is what makes the business stable.



If Operate is weak, you’ll feel like:





everything depends on you



cleaners need you for every question



mistakes keep repeating



quality is inconsistent



you’re always putting out fires



The goal of Operate is simple:
the business runs the same way every time... even when you’re not paying attention.

Operate is built on:





communication



SOPs (how we do things)



quality control



reporting rhythms



When I got nose surgery in mid December, I didn't even look at my cleaning business for almost three weeks. I knew that my VA and cleaners were going to follow the right systems, and if they needed my help - they would ask.
