Most businesses rely on motivation or memory:





“I hope cleaners remember”



“I hope the VA knows”



“I hope they do it right”



Systems are meant so that they don't have to rely so much on a human, eliminating human errors and forgetfulness:





documented standards



clear communication channels



a repeatable workflow for problems



checklists with verifiable completion proof



Operate is not about having “more tools.”
We want to eliminate all the unnecessary fluff to make the work as easy and simple as it can be.



If you are doing something over and over, find ways to templatize or systemize it.



When we recognized that we were using the same response over and over for when someone asked about what is included in a standard clean vs deep clean, we started using the templates feature in Harvest CRM to automatically copy/paste the message for us.



The little things like that add up when you're on the go, or you are scaling and dealing with 10 bookings in one day.
