Most owners avoid SOPs because they think it means writing a novel.



It doesn’t.

A “minimum viable SOP library” is just the handful of documents that prevent 80% of problems.



If I could only keep a few SOPs, it would be:





New client intake + expectations



Cleaner hiring



Before/after photos + QA



What’s included vs not included (scope)



Reschedule / cancellation policy



Complaint / redo / refund process



Payment failure process



How to handle access issues (lockbox, pets, alarms)


They’re how you stop solving the same problem 50 times & easily scale without the stress.
