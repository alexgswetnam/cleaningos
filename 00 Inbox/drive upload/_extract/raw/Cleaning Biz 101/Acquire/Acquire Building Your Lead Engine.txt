This part of the business is your lead engine. 

The stuff that makes strangers become interested enough to raise their hand.

It’s about getting in front of the right people, building trust quickly, and giving them a clear next step (call, quote request, booking link, etc.).

If Acquire is weak, everything else feels stressful because you’re constantly trying to “find business” instead of simply working the system. 

The goal here is predictable lead flow: you know where leads come from, you know what a “good lead” looks like, and you have a clean path for turning attention into contact info.
