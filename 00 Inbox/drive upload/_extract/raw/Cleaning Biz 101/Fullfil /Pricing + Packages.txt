Most scheduling chaos starts with pricing chaos.



Your team should not be guessing with prices - creating a quote should be systemized.



The goal with pricing isn’t perfection.
The goal is consistency.



At a high level, you want:





a base price model



add-ons



a recurring discount structure (if you do it)



minimums (so small jobs don’t drain you)



Pricing systems protect your profit and your sanity.



It is IMPOSSIBLE for me to tell you what YOUR pricing should be - but here's how we do it:



We charge a flat rate. To get that flat rate, we reverse engineer our hourly rate. We want to charge $60/hr per cleaner.



So if I know a 3/2 1800 sqft monthly clean with a couple and one kid takes 3 hours on average, we will charge $179 + tax.



If a 3000 sqft deep clean takes you 10 hours on average and you want to charge $55/hr, then that would be a $550 clean. Some will take longer, some will take shorter. The result for the client is the same - they are satisfied with a clean home.



Pretty simple when you break things down this way. Every cleaner will clean at a different pace. To determine how long each of our cleaners take, we keep a spreadsheet and figure out their sqft/hr! Do this for a month and you'll figure out your average.



Eventually, we added a "condition of the home" pricing parameter. If someone's home is a dirtiness level of 2, it takes way less time than if it was a dirtiness level of 7.
