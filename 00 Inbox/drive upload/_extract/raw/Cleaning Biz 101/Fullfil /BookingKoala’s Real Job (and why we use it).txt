BookingKoala is our fulfillment source of truth.



That means: if a job is quoted or booked, BookingKoala is where we trust the scheduling/pricing details to live.



A lot of people try to do scheduling in their heads, in texts, or in random calendars… and that’s when the business starts breaking.



Our BookingKoala is responsible for:





pricing + packages (consistent quoting)



calendar availability + scheduling rules



booking confirmations



reminder texts/emails



payment charged notifications



recurring scheduling structure



cleaners knowing their pay + schedule through the BookingKoala app



In short: it prevents your business from being run by memory.
