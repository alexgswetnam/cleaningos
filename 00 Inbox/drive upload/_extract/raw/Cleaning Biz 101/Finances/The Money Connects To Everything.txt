Finances are not “separate” from the business.



They connect to everything:





Acquire tells you if marketing is profitable



Convert affects cost per booking



Fulfill affects refunds/redos and retention



Operate affects cleaner efficiency and quality



Money is just the scoreboard for the whole system.



If you track it, you can improve it.

If you ignore it, you’ll stay stressed no matter how busy you are.



If you don't know your EXACT numbers, you need to.
