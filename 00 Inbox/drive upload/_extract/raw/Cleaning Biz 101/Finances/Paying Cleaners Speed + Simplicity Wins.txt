Paying cleaners shouldn’t be a weekly nightmare. It was for us - until we kept it simple...



We use a bank that supports Zelle because it makes payouts fast and simple.



The goals with paying cleaners:





Pay accurately



Pay on time



Keep it easy to track



Reduce back-and-forth



When you use the Free Tracking Sheet in the Free Resources module, you will have a pay stub showing you how many hours your cleaners worked and how much to pay them at the end of every week!



We use PNC Bank, they raised our Zelle limit after a month of using them.
