Most bookings don’t happen on the first message!!



A lead might say:





“Let me talk to my spouse”



“I’ll get back to you”



“We’re still deciding”



“Maybe next week”



If you weren't able to get the booking with your ninja sales skills, that doesn’t mean they’re dead. It means they need follow-up.

A real Convert system includes:





a follow-up cadence (day 0, day 1, day 3, day 7…)



a way to separate hot vs cold leads



automation so this happens even when you’re busy



This is why people think marketing “doesn’t work.”

Marketing works.

You just didn't follow up, or don't have sales training.



If you followed up better, you’d close more with the same leads.
