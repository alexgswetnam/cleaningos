## The spine

### Units of production

| Engine | Produces |
|---|---|
| **Leads** | A booked client |
| **Labor** | A cleaner who completes a job to standard |
| **Logistics** | A completed, correct, paid job |
| **Leadership** | A decision made and a standard held |

Owner-independence appears **only** on the Season axis, so the 4×4 stays orthogonal.

### How graduation works

Every criterion is a **yes/no test**, never a threshold. The owner picks the window. **It has
to hold twice** — once could be luck, and "reliably" has to mean something.

**Metrics: we name the instrument, never the target.** The vault supports *measure cost per
booking*. Nothing in it supports *cost per booking should be $X*. Every target is `NEEDS ALEX`.

### Owner-dependence is measured four times

Harvest's own question is *"without depending on the owner as its primary fuel"* — so each
Engine graduates on it separately.

> **Observed (n=30):** of 14 who chose *"I bought myself a job,"* **6 never clean and 2 rarely
> do.**
> **Interpretation:** they describe owner-dependent production and decision-making, most
> likely Leads and Leadership — not Labor.
> **Caveat:** the options were written by Alex. Not organic customer language.

**Consequence:** the diagnostic asks about owner-dependence **per Engine**. Asking "do you
still clean?" once would misread most of that sample.

## Cell detail

### Leads — produces a booked client

**Survival.** Can it produce a booking at all? A stranger can reach you and get a response ·
at least one non-accidental booking traced to a source.
*Problems:* every booking feels like luck · no named channel · slow response · learning
marketing and selling at once.
*Metrics:* source of each booking · time to first response.
*Have:* [[Leads Problem Or Sales Problem]] · [[Ideal Customer Profile]] · [[Speed To Lead]] ·
[[Referral Program]] · [[Website Strategy]] · [[Owner Learns Marketing First]] ·
[[Sales Happen On The Phone]] · [[Price Delivery]] · [[Word Tracks]] · [[GBP Verification]]
*Sources:* `Leads 101 part 1` → What Way Should I Get Leads First · How did WE get started ·
Marketing Basics Survival Season Coaching · `Google Biz Profile Setup and Growth` (5)
**Decision 3 applied:** profitability is **not** required here. [[Month One Loses Money]]
says acquisition costs more than the first clean — requiring profit at Survival would fail
everyone doing it right.

**Stability.** Predictably, and you know the cost.
*Problems:* feast/famine · can't forecast · close rate unknown · leads go cold when busy.
*Metrics:* cost per booking · CPL/CPA · close rate by channel · one-time→recurring · speed to
lead. **Instruments supported. No target is.**
*Have:* [[Marketing Math]] · [[Conversion Tracking]] · [[Close Rate By Channel]] ·
[[Channel Prioritization]] · [[Pricing Methodology]] · [[One-Time To Recurring]] ·
[[Sales Pipeline Stages]] · [[Leads Don't Disappear]] · [[Reactivating Past Clients]] ·
4 objection pages · [[GBP Posting Cadence]] · [[Post-Clean Review Script]] ·
[[Website Technical SEO Basics]]
*Sources:* `Leads 101 Part 2` (22 lessons)
**Answered, Alex 2026-08-07 — two passes.** First: both — knowing the close rate isn't
enough, it also has to clear a minimum. Second, when asked what the minimum number is:
*"that number is based per channel and varying factors, the system should not try to know
or serve perfect numbers — point them to real life experience from operators and experts...
like me, their coach."*

**Net effect:** no fixed percentage gets hard-coded anywhere. "Clearing a minimum" is a real
requirement, but *what counts as cleared* is a judgment call for a human coach looking at
the specific channel and context — not a number the vault or an AI computes and checks
against. This actually lines back up with [[Close Rate By Channel]]'s existing teaching
("healthy means profitable, not a fixed percentage") rather than overriding it. Relevant to
how the $297 Strategist AI should behave here: point to coaching, don't compute a verdict.

**Scale.** Growth without more of you.
*Problems:* growth capped by your calendar · one-channel dependency · spend uncoupled from
capacity.
*Have:* [[Marketing Seasonality]] · [[PPC Search Not Display]] · [[PPC Landing Page Strategy]] ·
[[Client Concentration]] · [[AI CRM Auditing]] · [[Weekend Operations]]
*Missing:* **capacity-based marketing.** Named in the Business GPS grid; no page exists.
**Decision 9 applied:** [[Marketing Budget]]'s ~10% is **not** used as a signal here.

**Harvest.** — 2 pages.
*Have:* [[Client Concentration]] · [[Marketing Seasonality]] — neither is about delegated
marketing.
**Decision 5 applied:** the owner owns **the brand and the budget ceiling**. Not the
campaigns, not the channels. **Confirmed by Alex, 2026-08-07** — no changes to the split.
