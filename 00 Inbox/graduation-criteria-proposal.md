## Before the matrix: three things to approve first

Everything below depends on these. If any is wrong, the 16 cells are wrong.

### 1. Each Engine needs a unit of production

[[Four Seasons]] asks *"can this Engine reliably produce **its required result**?"* — and
never says what that result is. Without naming it, the four questions can't be answered.
Proposed:

| Engine | Produces |
|---|---|
| **Leads** | A booked client |
| **Labor** | A person who can deliver the promise without the owner |
| **Logistics** | A completed, correct, paid job |
| **Leadership** | A decision made and a standard held without the owner |

**This is the spine.** Approve it and the 16 cells follow mechanically.

### 2. Graduation is a test, not a number

You said don't invent thresholds. So every criterion below is written as **a test the owner
can run with a yes/no answer.** No revenue bands, no counts, no percentages.

*"You can predict next week's bookings and be right"* is observable and needs no threshold.
*"12 bookings a week"* would be invented. Only the first kind appears below.

**On metrics: we can name the instrument. We cannot name the target.** The vault supports
*measure cost per booking*. It does not support *cost per booking should be $X*. Every
metric below is an instrument; every target is `NEEDS ALEX`.

### 3. Owner-dependence is the Season axis, not the Leadership Engine

This is the structural point, and the customer research sharpened it.

Harvest's question — *"without depending on the owner as its primary fuel"* — means the 4×4
**already measures owner-dependence four separate times**, once per Engine. It is not one
Engine's topic.

> **Observed (30 responses):** 14 selected *"I bought myself a job."* Of those, **6 never
> clean and 2 rarely do.**
>
> **Interpretation (mine, not evidence):** they are describing owner-dependent *production,
> revenue and decision-making* — not owner-dependent cleaning. Most likely their Leads and
> Leadership Engines, not Labor.
>
> Caveat: response options were written by Alex, so this is not organic customer language.
> It is a signal about which of our own phrasings people select, and what it may mean.

**Product consequence:** the diagnostic must ask about owner-dependence **per Engine**. An
owner who never touches a mop can still be the entire Leads Engine. Asking "do you still
clean?" once would miss most of this sample.

**Messaging consequence:** "I bought myself a job" is the phrase that landed — and we've been
reading it wrong. It isn't about the mop. It's about being the only person who can produce
revenue.

## Cell detail

Format per cell: **Problems** · **Graduation** · **Metrics** · **Have** · **Sources** ·
**NEEDS ALEX**

### Leads

**Survival — can it produce a booked client at all?**
Observable: a stranger can reach you and get a response · at least one non-accidental
booking traced to a source · you know where the last one came from.
**Problems:** every booking feels like luck · no named channel · slow response · owner
learning marketing and selling simultaneously.
**Graduation:** name the source of recent bookings and deliberately trigger another.
**Metrics:** source of each booking · time from inquiry to first response. *Instruments
only.*
**Have:** [[Leads Problem Or Sales Problem]] · [[Ideal Customer Profile]] · [[Speed To Lead]] · [[Referral Program]] · [[Website Strategy]] · [[Owner Learns Marketing First]] · [[Sales Happen On The Phone]] · [[Price Delivery]] · [[Word Tracks]] · [[GBP Verification]]
**Sources:** `Leads 101 part 1` → What Way Should I Get Leads First · How did WE get started · Marketing Basics Survival Season Coaching · `Google Biz Profile Setup and Growth` (5)
**NEEDS ALEX:** does "profitable" belong here at all? [[Month One Loses Money]] says acquisition costs more than the first clean — so per-booking profit can't be judged in Survival. Suggest Survival = *can produce*, profit enters at Stability.

**Stability — predictably?**
Observable: you can forecast · cost per booking known · close rate known by channel ·
follow-up fires without memory.
**Problems:** feast/famine · can't forecast · close rate unknown · leads go cold when busy.
**Graduation:** forecast and hit it without changing anything **and** an unattended lead
still gets followed up.
**Metrics:** cost per booking · CPL/CPA · close rate by channel · one-time→recurring rate ·
speed to lead. **All instruments have vault support. No target does.**
**Have:** [[Marketing Math]] · [[Conversion Tracking]] · [[Close Rate By Channel]] · [[Channel Prioritization]] · [[Pricing Methodology]] · [[One-Time To Recurring]] · [[Sales Pipeline Stages]] · [[Leads Don't Disappear]] · [[Reactivating Past Clients]] · 4 objection pages · [[GBP Posting Cadence]] · [[Post-Clean Review Script]] · [[Website Technical SEO Basics]]
**Sources:** `Leads 101 Part 2` (22 lessons — close rate, response rate, objections, pipeline)
**NEEDS ALEX:** [[Close Rate By Channel]] says "healthy means profitable, not a fixed percentage." Does Stability require *knowing* the close rate, or *reaching* one?

**Scale — without proportional owner?**
**Problems:** growth capped by the owner's calendar · single-channel dependency · spend
uncoupled from capacity.
**Graduation:** someone else ran the sales conversation and close rate held · you moved
volume deliberately.
**Metrics:** close rate by person · CAC by channel · spend-vs-capacity.
**Have:** [[Marketing Seasonality]] · [[PPC Search Not Display]] · [[PPC Landing Page Strategy]] · [[Client Concentration]] · [[AI CRM Auditing]] · [[Weekend Operations]]
**Sources:** `Leads 101 part 1` → Marketing Basics Scale Season Coaching · Paid Marketing Methods Breakdown
**Missing:** **capacity-based marketing.** Named in the Business GPS grid; no page exists.
**NEEDS ALEX:** [[Marketing Budget]]'s ~10%-of-revenue is flagged unproven in the Claim Register. Usable as a graduation signal or not?

**Harvest — without you as fuel?** — **2 pages**
**Problems:** owner still the strategist · nobody else reads the numbers · brand risk on
delegation.
**Graduation:** you didn't look for a set period and volume *and quality* held.
**Have:** [[Client Concentration]] · [[Marketing Seasonality]] — neither is about delegated
marketing.
**Sources:** `Leads 101 part 1` → Marketing Basics Sustain and Sell Season Coaching · Should someone else do marketing for me
**NEEDS ALEX:** what does the owner still own at Leads Harvest? Budget? Brand? Nothing?
