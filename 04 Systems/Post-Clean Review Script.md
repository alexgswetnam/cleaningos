# Post-Clean Review Script

> [!abstract] In one line
> Send the review link while they're still on the phone, and confirm they got it — never "I'll send it after we hang up."

## Definition

A phone script for generating reviews immediately after a client's first clean: call to
check satisfaction, ask for a five-star review, and send the link live on the same call
rather than promising to send it later. Distinct from [[Reviews As Asset Not Identity]],
which covers the owner's emotional relationship to the star rating — this is the
mechanical process for generating reviews in volume.

## Why It Matters

The gap between "I'll send it later" and "here it is right now, did you get it" is the
difference between a review that happens and one that quietly never does. Once a client
hangs up, the moment of goodwill fades and the ask competes with the rest of their day.
Sending it live, while they're still engaged and have just said yes, converts far more of
those yeses into actual reviews.

## Symptoms

- Reviews growing slower than first-clean volume would suggest
- Asking for reviews by text or email after the fact with a low response rate
- Not having a standard script — asking inconsistently or not at all

## Common Mistakes

- Saying "I'll send it to you right when we get off the phone" — this is explicitly the
  mistake to avoid; the ask has to be completed on the call, not promised for after
- Not confirming the client actually received the link before ending the call
- Skipping the check-in call for first cleans and hoping for reviews organically

## Models

**The full call, in order:**

1. Call after the first clean: *"Hey, this is [name], one of the managers. Just want to
   check in — how did the clean go today?"*
2. If positive, reinforce it and name the cleaner specifically: *"I love to hear that. I'll
   make sure [cleaner name] knows. She gets a bonus for great reviews, so it means a
   lot."* — naming the cleaner and mentioning a bonus makes the ask personal rather than
   transactional.
3. Ask directly: *"Would you mind leaving us a five-star review? It takes 30 seconds."*
4. The moment they say yes, **send the review link immediately, while still on the call.**
5. Confirm receipt: *"Did you get it?"*
6. Thank them and assume the close: *"Thank you for doing it. It really helps us."*

**The rule that makes it work.** Do the ask and the send in the same call — never defer
either step. A client who's agreed and is holding the phone has no excuse not to follow
through in the next thirty seconds; a client who hangs up with a vague promise usually
just forgets.

**The broader version of the same call also confirms recurring and payment.** A second
source describes the same check-in call folding in two more objectives before the review
ask, in this order: confirm the next recurring clean is still scheduled (or offer to set
one up if there isn't one), then handle payment if it hasn't been collected yet — see
[[Payment Verification & Collection]] — and only then move to the review ask. If the
feedback wasn't positive, the call branches to a fix-it plan instead of the review ask:
*"Thanks for letting me know—that's exactly what I want to hear so we can fix it. Can you
tell me a little more about what was missed?"*

## Checklist

- [ ] Call every first-time client after their clean, not just problem cases
- [ ] Open with a genuine satisfaction check, not an immediate review ask
- [ ] Name the specific cleaner and mention a bonus/incentive when asking
- [ ] Send the review link live, on the same call, the moment they say yes
- [ ] Confirm receipt before ending the call
- [ ] Never say "I'll send it after we hang up"
- [ ] If run as the fuller check-in call: confirm the next recurring date, then handle
      any outstanding payment, before the review ask

## Templates

- "Hey, this is [name], one of the managers. Just want to check in — how did the clean go today?"
- "I love to hear that. I'll make sure [cleaner name] knows. She gets a bonus for great reviews, so it means a lot."
- "Would you mind leaving us a five-star review? It takes 30 seconds."
- "Did you get it? ... Thank you for doing it. It really helps us."
- "I've got your next clean set for [Date], does that still work for you?" (recurring
  confirmation)
- "I noticed we don't have a payment on file. How would you like to take care of today's
  clean?" (payment, if needed)

## FAQ

**Q:** Why not just text the review link after the call?
**A:** The source's explicit position is that live, in-call delivery converts far better
than a deferred text — the commitment and the action happen in the same moment, with no
gap for the client to forget or deprioritize it.
