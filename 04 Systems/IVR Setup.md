# IVR Setup

> [!abstract] In one line
> A one-key phone menu eliminates robocalls, because robots cannot press one.

## Definition

An Interactive Voice Response menu built in Harvest CRM. Callers hear a short greeting and press a key to connect.

## Why It Matters

Robocalls stop reaching you. Rick's greeting is one line — *"Hi, this is The Faithful Cleaners. Press 1 to connect."* Nothing else. The robocalls cannot press it. Alex: *"it just helps our sanity because the phone is not ringing constantly."*
