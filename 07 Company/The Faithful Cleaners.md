# The Faithful Cleaners

Alex and [[Elijah]]'s own cleaning business. Based in **Prosper, Texas**.

This is the proof behind CleaningOS — every claim in the Canon was tested here first.

## What's Confirmed

- **Founded:** February 2025 — first clients from that cohort were still recurring a year later
- **Name:** The Faithful Cleaners
- **Location:** Prosper, TX (Dallas area)
- **GBP name format:** *"The Faithful Cleaners House Cleaning Service Prosper"* — the
  door signage matches exactly. See [[Google Business Profile Naming]].
- **Season:** [[Four Seasons|Harvest]] — Alex describes it as running on VAs and systems,
  growing without aggressive marketing
- **Revenue goal:** end-of-year target was around $60–80K/month *(Alex wasn't certain of
  the figure on the call)*
- **Declined a $50K acquisition offer** in March 2025. See [[Business Valuation]].
- **Staffing:** hired three cleaners in March 2025 via sponsored Indeed post plus a local
  Facebook group post
- **VAs:** at least one named Elle. Managed per [[Managing A VA]].
- **Tooling:** [[Harvest CRM]], including an IVR built with an 11 Labs voice

## The First Six Months — confirmed by Alex 2026-08-05

| Month | Ad spend | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $3,000 | $8,000 |
| 2 | $3,000 | $8,000 |

Ad spend went on a credit card **while both founders were still in personal debt.** The
pullback to $500 was forced by three things: recouping the card, a cleaning-knowledge gap
that stopped the spend converting, and having to build systems and process from scratch
because they hadn't built in order. See [[Month One Loses Money]].

## Revenue At Roughly One Year

**~$27,500/month**, described as "zero to $27,500/month in about a year using VAs, CRMs, AI,
and automations."

Founded February 2025, so "about a year" puts this around **early 2026**.

> [!warning] Context matters for this number
> Stated by Alex to a broker while positioning himself as a credible buyer of another
> business. It's his own figure about his own business — but the setting was one where
> sounding capable was the point. Use it, attribute it, and don't present it as audited.
>
> Source: [[Another convo w the broker]]. That source's own recording date is unestablished.

**Against the March 2025 target.** The end-of-year goal on the March 2025 call was
$60–80K/month. Actual at ~12 months was $27,500. The gap is worth Alex's own comment before
anyone teaches from either number — a missed target isn't a failure story or a success story
until he says which.

**Against the declined $50K offer.** [[Business Valuation]] records Alex turning down $50K in
March 2025, reasoning *"would it be a lot nicer to wait six months, wait a year, and a
$200,000, $300,000 payday? Yeah."*

Running his own formula on $27,500/month — *at the 25% margin used illustratively elsewhere,
which is not a confirmed margin for this business* — gives roughly $82,500 annual profit and
a $165–180K valuation. That is **well above the $50K he declined and below the $200–300K he
named.** The decision to hold looks correct; the figure he hoped for hasn't arrived yet.

None of this is in a source. It's arithmetic on two numbers that are, flagged here because
the $50K decision is taught and this is the follow-up nobody has written down.

## Why It Matters

Alex teaches from an operating business, not from theory. The positioning in
[[Philosophy]] — *"still close enough to the actual work that the advice has to function
in a real company on a real Tuesday"* — depends on this being live.

## Acquisition Activity

Alex and [[Elijah]] are also **buyers**. With Courtney and Oliver they evaluated a
25-year-old commercial cleaning business through a broker — NDA signed, CIM requested, full
due diligence questioning. No deal closed in the source.

Their stated edge as buyers: *"We excel in growth. We excel in sales and marketing."* The
target type is an established book of business run without modern systems — *"a house that
looks like it could use a pretty good facelift."*

The full workflow is [[Buying A Cleaning Business]]. Note this is a **commercial/janitorial**
target, a different market from the residential business they operate.
