# The Faithful Cleaners

Alex and [[Elijah]]'s own cleaning business. Based in **Prosper, Texas**.

This is the proof behind CleaningOS — every claim in the Canon was tested here first.

## What's Confirmed

- **Name:** The Faithful Cleaners
- **Location:** Prosper, TX (Dallas area)
- **GBP name format:** *"The Faithful Cleaners House Cleaning Service Prosper"* — the
  door signage matches exactly. See [[Google Business Profile Naming]].
- **Season:** [[Four Seasons|Harvest]] — Alex describes it as running on VAs and systems,
  growing without aggressive marketing
- **Revenue goal:** end-of-year target was around $60–80K/month *(Alex wasn't certain of
  the figure on the call)*
- **Declined a $50K acquisition offer** in March 2025. See [[Business Valuation]].
- **Staffing:** hired three cleaners in March 2025 via sponsored Indeed post plus a local
  Facebook group post
- **VAs:** at least one named Elle. Managed per [[Managing A VA]].
- **Tooling:** [[Harvest CRM]], including an IVR built with an 11 Labs voice

## Why It Matters

Alex teaches from an operating business, not from theory. The positioning in
[[Philosophy]] — *"still close enough to the actual work that the advice has to function
in a real company on a real Tuesday"* — depends on this being live.
