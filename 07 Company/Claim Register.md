# Claim Register

**Every customer-facing claim, and whether it may be used.**

Internal. Never published, never rendered into an asset. Its only job is to make one
question answerable in five seconds: *can I say this?*

## The three statuses

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Traced to a source, permission cleared where a person is named. Usable. |
| **NEEDS VERIFICATION** | Plausible, probably true, not yet traced or not yet cleared. **Do not publish.** |
| **DO NOT USE** | Blocked. A legal, factual, or permission problem that a human must resolve. |

**Default for anything absent from this table is NEEDS VERIFICATION.** Silence is not
permission.

> [!danger] Never manufacture proof to fill a gap
> An empty Proof column is information — it says we teach something we can't yet
> demonstrate. A fabricated student win in this table eventually gets read aloud on a sales
> call. Constitution §IX.

## How to add a row

1. Find the claim in a customer-facing page or a draft.
2. Trace it to a source **at claim granularity** — the lesson, not the course.
3. If a person is named, record permission separately from verification. **They are not the
   same thing**, and conflating them is how a true story becomes a legal problem.
4. If you can't trace it, the status is `NEEDS VERIFICATION`. Never `VERIFIED` on the basis
   that it sounds right.
5. **Never cite a file in `09 Derived/` or an AI summary in `01 Sources/Course Videos/` as
   the source.** Those are interpretation. Go to the raw transcript.
