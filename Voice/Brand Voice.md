# Brand Voice

**How CleaningOS sounds to customers.** YouTube, VSLs, emails, Skool posts, lessons, sales
pages, ads, onboarding, community content.

Not how AI talks to Alex — that's [[AI Working Style]], and it is more blunt on purpose.

> [!info] This page and [[Alex Voice]] do different jobs
> **[[Alex Voice]]** holds the *sound*: verbatim anchors, sentence rhythm, the four modes
> (`teach` · `walk` · `straight` · `sell`), and the correction log. Read it for
> calibration — imitate the anchors, not a description of them.
>
> **This page** holds *brand personality, persuasion rules, terminology and teaching
> philosophy.* It doesn't restate the anchors.
>
> Writing customer-facing copy? Read both.

---

## Personality

CleaningOS sounds **clear · practical · confident · human · simple · experienced ·
direct · calm.**

Never like a corporate consultant, an AI, a fake guru, a motivational speaker, or someone
trying to sound rich.

## Writing style

Short sentences. Simple words. Natural spoken rhythm. Specific examples. Minimal jargon.

Explain complex business ideas so a first-time owner understands them.

**Make the customer feel understood before teaching or selling.**

| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| "This is probably why you're stuck." | "Our proprietary framework enables scalable operational optimization." |
| "Two cleans a day. Not four." | "Optimize scheduling density for throughput." |
| "You're not behind. You're early." | "Every entrepreneur's journey is unique." |

## Persuasion

Strong persuasion, no manipulation. Sell through **clarity · diagnosis · proof ·
specificity · useful teaching · examples · contrast · a clear next step.**

### Never invent proof

Not a testimonial. Not a student result. Not revenue, a screenshot, a client story, a
guarantee, or a number.

If proof would strengthen the draft and none is verified, leave a marked placeholder:

```
[INSERT VERIFIED STUDENT STORY ABOUT ______]
[INSERT SCREENSHOT SHOWING ______]
```

**Do not fill in the blank.** A fabricated win gets read aloud on a sales call.

Every factual claim, price, guarantee, statistic or named student needs a usable status in
`07 Company/Claim Register.md`. **`DO NOT USE` means not in any form**, including
paraphrased or softened.

## CleaningOS language

Use the real terms when they earn their place:

**CleaningOS** · **Four Engines** — Leads · Labor · Logistics · Leadership ·
**Four Seasons** — Survival · Stability · Scale · Harvest · **Business GPS** ·
**Roadmap** · **One Step**

**The Five Laws:**

1. Clarity Creates Momentum.
2. Stop Guessing.
3. Build In Order.
4. The Roadmap Already Exists.
5. One Step Wins.

**Do not force these into every piece.** Use them when they genuinely reinforce the idea.
A lesson that says "Law 3" without needing to is worse than one that never mentions it.

**Do not invent competing slogans or frameworks.** If an existing CleaningOS framework
already explains the idea, use it. Casual new frameworks are how one clear model becomes
five overlapping ones.

Hard rule: **"Harvest CRM" is always both words.** Bare *Harvest* means the Season. Bare
*Harvest* meaning the product is ambiguous out loud, which is where it does damage.

Owner = our student. Client = their customer. Cleaner = the person doing the work. Mixing
these produces sentences that are subtly nonsense.

## Teaching philosophy

CleaningOS does not exist to bury people in information.

```
Diagnose → Know what matters → Take the right action → Measure → Re-diagnose
```

**The customer should finish thinking: "I know what to do next."**

If a draft leaves them impressed but unsure what to do, it failed.
