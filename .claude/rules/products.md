# Rules for `05 Products/`

**Things sold or shipped.** Product truth, not knowledge — knowledge lives in
`03 Concepts/` and gets linked.

Current: CleaningOS Free · CleaningOS Membership · Group Coaching · 1:1 Coaching ·
Harvest CRM Offer · the curriculum map and crosswalks.

## Every claim goes in the register first

`07 Company/Claim Register.md`. Claim · Product · Source · Verification · Permission ·
Public? · Notes. Statuses: **VERIFIED / NEEDS VERIFICATION / DO NOT USE.**

**Anything absent from the register defaults to NEEDS VERIFICATION.** Silence is not
permission.

## Never

- **Invent a benefit, guarantee, inclusion, price, or trial.** If a source doesn't state it,
  write `NEEDS ALEX` and move on. An empty section is information
- **Publish a DO NOT USE claim** in any form, including paraphrased or softened
- **Quote a guarantee without its conditions.** The 1:1 guarantee is inseparable from its
  Requirements to Claim
- **Use a student's name** without a cleared permission status. Verification and permission
  are different things — a result can be entirely true and not ours to publish
- **Change contract wording.** Check current direction against the signed agreement and
  report the difference rather than resolving it

## Frontmatter

Presentation pages carry `renders:`, `shipped:`, `channel:`, `live:`. **`renders:` is not
optional** — it's what makes the blast-radius audit possible when a concept changes.
