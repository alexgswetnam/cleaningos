# Voice — customer-facing writing

Applies when you are **creating or substantially editing customer-facing writing.**

Not every file under these paths is customer-facing. `05 Products/CleaningOS Curriculum
Map.md` and `Legacy Content Crosswalk.md` are internal planning artifacts — this rule
doesn't govern them. Judge by whether a customer will read it.

**Not in scope at all:** `01 Sources/**` (immutable raw material) and `09 Derived/**` (AI
interpretation). Never rewrite either to sound better.

## Before you write

1. **Read `Voice/Brand Voice.md`** — personality, persuasion rules, terminology, teaching
   philosophy.
2. **Read `Voice/Alex Voice.md`** — the anchors are real Alex sentences. Imitate those, not
   a description of them. Check the Voice Log for rules from past corrections.
3. **Use approved files in `Voice/Examples/`** when any exist. Calibration, never templates
   — do not copy them word-for-word.

## While writing

4. **Preserve CleaningOS terminology.** Four Engines · Four Seasons · Business GPS ·
   Roadmap · One Step · the Five Laws. Use them where they earn their place; don't force
   them in. **"Harvest CRM" is always both words** — bare *Harvest* means the Season.
5. **Do not invent proof.** No testimonial, result, revenue figure, screenshot, client
   story or guarantee that isn't verified. If proof would help and none exists, leave
   `[INSERT VERIFIED ______]` and move on. Check `07 Company/Claim Register.md` — **`DO NOT
   USE` means not in any form**, including paraphrased.
6. **Write clear, simple, conversational prose.** Short sentences. Simple words. Specific
   examples. Make the reader feel understood before teaching or selling.
7. **Avoid generic AI language.** No "in today's fast-paced world," no "unlock," no
   "leverage," no three-item lists that all say the same thing, no summary paragraph that
   restates what you just wrote.
8. **Don't invent a new framework when an existing CleaningOS one explains the idea.** One
   strong map beats five clever maps. A new customer-facing model needs Alex's approval —
   `CONSTITUTION.md` §II.

## The test

The reader should finish thinking **"I know what to do next."** Impressed but unsure is a
failed draft.

## Not this layer

`Voice/AI Working Style.md` governs how AI talks to **Alex** — blunter, more opinionated,
argues back. **It does not apply to customer copy.**
