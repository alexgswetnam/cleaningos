# Draft

You are **rendering existing knowledge into a format**, not researching a topic.
Constitution §IV.

## 1. Load the voice

1. `02 Canon/Philosophy.md` — what we believe, how we teach
2. `Voice/Alex Voice.md` — **the anchors are the target.** Read the verbatim Alex samples
   before writing a word. Check the Voice Log for rules from past corrections
3. `02 Canon/Language.md` — "Harvest CRM" never shortens
4. `02 Canon/Five Laws.md` and `Four Seasons.md` — the frames

If a **belief or philosophy** you need isn't written, stop and ask. Do not improvise
philosophy — an invented belief in a script becomes one Alex has to defend on a call.

## 2. Read the concept, not the transcripts

Open the canonical page. Read its Related Concepts too — the best content usually comes
from the connection, not the concept alone.

**Do not search `01 Sources/` for material.** Pulling from transcripts is how the vault gets
bypassed and slowly becomes irrelevant.

## 3. Missing sections do not stop you

> **Drafting proceeds when the core teaching is verified.**

A concept lacking Examples, FAQ, Proof or any other optional section is **not** a blocker.
Write from what's there, and note what would have made it stronger.

This replaces the old rule that any missing section halted drafting. That rule stopped work
on pages that were perfectly adequate to teach from.

**What does stop you:** the core teaching itself being unverified, contested, or absent.

## 4. The gates

- **Contested** — `contested: true`, or a callout covering the claim you're about to teach
  → **stop and ask.** Do not pick a side. Do not write around it with vague phrasing
- **Superseded** — make sure you're using the current claim, not the one in the collapsed
  history
- **Claim register** — every price, guarantee, statistic, result or named student needs a
  usable status in `07 Company/Claim Register.md`. `DO NOT USE` means not in any form,
  including paraphrased

## 5. Facts get verified against raw sources

For **proof, exact quotations, results or numbers**: verify against the original material in
`01 Sources/`.

> **Never rely solely on an AI-generated summary for a factual claim.** Not `09 Derived/`,
> not the lesson `.md` files inside course folders. Those paraphrase. Go to the raw `.txt`.

Once verified, update the concept page or the claim register so nobody repeats the work.

## 6. Render

- **YouTube script** — hook, the mistake, the reframe, the one step, CTA
- **Email** — one idea, one story, one action
- **VSL / sales page** — enemy, promise, mechanism, proof, offer, objections
- **Course lesson** — where this sits on the roadmap, concept, checklist, template
- **Social post** — one claim, one proof, no CTA

Ask if the format is unclear rather than guessing.

## 7. Self-check

Does every claim trace to the concept page? Does it sound like the anchors — read one, then
read your draft, same person? Any Voice Log rule violated? Every "Harvest" that means the
product written as "Harvest CRM"? Owner vs Client correct throughout? Any claim taught that
sits under a Contested or Superseded block, or a DO NOT USE register row?

## 8. File it

Save to `05 Products/` or `06 Marketing/` with `renders:` `shipped:` `channel:` `live:`.

**`renders:` is not optional** — without it the asset becomes invisible to correction and
will still be teaching a retired claim a year from now.

Then note on the concept page where it's been presented, so the graph stays two-way.
