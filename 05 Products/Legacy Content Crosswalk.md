# Legacy Content Crosswalk

**Every piece of existing course material, mapped to Four Engines × Four Seasons.**

The goal is to find out how much of CleaningOS is *already recorded* before anything gets
rebuilt. **Skool is not reorganised by this document.** First understand what exists.

> [!warning] How to read the Engine and Season columns
> **Engine is reliable.** It comes from which course a lesson belongs to, which is a fact
> about the material, plus per-lesson overrides where a lesson clearly sits elsewhere.
>
> **Season is a proposal, not a finding.** It is inferred from lesson titles and subject
> matter. Where the title gives no signal it reads `?` rather than a guess. **Do not treat
> a Season here as decided** — that's a pass someone has to make by watching the lesson.
>
> **Outcome taught is derived from the title**, not from watching the video. Titles in this
> library are unusually descriptive, which makes this useful; it does not make it verified.

> [!info] What the .md files in these folders actually are
> The `.md` file for each lesson is an **AI-generated summary**, not a transcript — see
> `09 Derived/Derived Source Manifest.md`. The `.txt` beside it is the real record. This
> crosswalk is built from titles and structure, which is safe. **Anything quoted from a
> lesson must come from the `.txt`.**
