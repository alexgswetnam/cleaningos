# 1:1 Coaching

House Cleaning Skool 1:1 Coaching Package. Delivered by Alex and Elijah.

> [!abstract] In one line
> $6,000 over 12 months for weekly 1:1 time, a sourced and trained VA, marketing system
> setup, and a guarantee of +$10k/mo within 3 months.

## The Guarantee

**Add $10,000/month in revenue and build a system to maintain it while working under 10
hours per week — within 3 months. If not, we keep working for free until it happens.**

Revenue and systems guarantee. **Not** a promise of net profit, personal income,
valuation, exact margins, or specific ad metrics.

> "We bring the system, coaching, marketing setup, VA training, and pressure. You bring
> implementation, ad spend, and not ghosting the process."

## Investment

| | |
|---|---|
| Program | $6,000, via Klarna over 12 months |
| Harvest CRM | $147/month, discounted, ongoing while active |

Klarna is a separate financing provider; the payment arrangement doesn't change program
terms. **All payments final and non-refundable** — the remedy for dissatisfaction is the
guarantee, not a refund.

## What's Included

- Lifetime access to the highest tier of House Cleaning Skool
- All SOPs, templates, trainings, scripts, systems, resources
- **60+ minutes of 1:1 time weekly**, plus direct Discord access to Alex
- Marketing system setup and optimization — leads, booking, follow-up, tracking
- **VA sourcing plus an 8-week bootcamp** at no extra cost beyond paying the VA

Discord response expectation: 24 business hours, Mon–Fri.

## Requirements To Claim The Guarantee

The student must materially implement. At minimum:

- Willing and able to spend on advertising — *"$0 in ad spend is not a serious attempt at
  a paid marketing system"*
- Attend calls or reschedule with notice
- Implement assigned actions in a timely manner
- Provide access to lead, ad, booking, and revenue data
- Track revenue and lead sources consistently
- Maintain professional communication
- Not pause execution, shut down ads, fire the VA without replacement, or disappear

Not meeting these may pause, extend, or void the guarantee.

## Terms Worth Knowing

- **Non-solicit:** 12 months after last participation — no hiring or bypassing our
  trainers, VAs, or vendors
- **IP:** limited non-transferable license. No competing coaching program, course,
  community, VA training company, or CRM product for 2 years
- **Marketing permission:** broad by default — the program is designed to create public
  proof
- **Governing law:** Texas. Disputes go to binding arbitration
