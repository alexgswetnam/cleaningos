# CleaningOS AI

> [!abstract] In one line
> The AI is not one product — it's three access levels, one per tier, each with a different
> job. This page is the first place that job is written down. Answers open decision **#2c**
> ("A CleaningOS AI is a product component and has no page anywhere").

> [!warning] Working direction, not Canon
> Recorded from Alex direct, 2026-08-07. Internal names below are working names, not final
> branding. No prompts, agents, or implementation exist yet — this page is scope only.

## The three levels

| Tier | Working name | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Survival AI / Business GPS AI | **Help me understand where I am and get moving** |
| $47 — [[CleaningOS Membership]] | CleaningOS Implementation AI | **Help me implement the CleaningOS roadmap** |
| $297 — [[Group Coaching]] | CleaningOS Strategist AI / Full CleaningOS AI | **Help me apply CleaningOS to my specific business** |

Each level includes everything the level below it can do. The difference is **the job each
level performs and how personalized the reasoning gets** — not answer quality. Free is not
deliberately dumbed down; $297 is not deliberately the only "good" one.

## Free — Survival AI / Business GPS AI

**Job: diagnose + get moving.**

May: explain the Four Engines, explain the Four Seasons, explain Business GPS, help
interpret their Diagnostic, help clarify their next measurable goal, help them understand
Survival-level problems, help identify a sensible first action, help with basic Survival
implementation, point toward free resources, explain what Stability/Scale/Harvest mean at a
high level.

Must not: hand over full implementation systems for Stability, Scale, or Harvest.

## $47 — CleaningOS Implementation AI

**Job: implement the roadmap.** Everything Free does, plus: use the full core CleaningOS
curriculum, teach all four Seasons, help implement Survival/Stability/Scale/Harvest systems,
explain lessons, help use templates, help implement SOPs, troubleshoot normal implementation
problems, help create basic implementation assets, point to the correct lesson/system/
resource.

Flow: **Teach → Guide → Help implement → Point to the roadmap.**

Distinction from $297: this tier implements CleaningOS. It does not do full personalized
business strategy.

## $297 — CleaningOS Strategist AI / Full CleaningOS AI

**Job: apply CleaningOS to my specific business.** Everything Free and $47 do, plus: deeply
interpret the member's specific numbers, compare multiple Engines, challenge the member's
assumed bottleneck, reason through their next measurable goal, identify likely constraints,
prioritize competing actions, build personalized weekly plans, review implementation, prepare
the member for coaching calls, use advanced Vault material, reason through edge cases, help
make more complex business decisions.

Flow: **Diagnose → Challenge → Prioritize → Personalize → Prepare for coaching.**
