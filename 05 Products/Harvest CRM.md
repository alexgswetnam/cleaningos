# Harvest CRM

> [!danger] Naming rule — always both words
> **Never write "Harvest" alone when you mean the product.** Bare *Harvest* is the
> fourth [[Four Seasons|Season]]. Expand to "Harvest CRM" on every mention, including
> the second and third — no pronoun shortcuts.

## What It Is

The CRM used inside CleaningOS programs. Where the work created by healthy Engines
becomes visible and manageable.

> "The software is not the business."

## Pricing

**$147/month**, discounted rate for coaching students, for as long as it stays active.

Cancellable any time. From the 1:1 agreement:

> "Canceling the machine we are using to help make you money would be flat out stupid."

Canceling may limit our ability to support, track, optimize, or fulfill parts of a
program, and may affect guarantee eligibility where the CRM is materially needed.
