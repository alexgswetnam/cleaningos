# CleaningOS Free

> [!abstract] In one line
> Free tier. Core promise: **figure out what I should work on.** A real, useful result, not a
> teaser.

## What It Is

Working direction, Alex direct 2026-08-07. Current intended access:

- [[Business GPS]] Assessment
- Next measurable goal
- Season assessment across all four Engines
- Likely Engine to investigate first
- One Step / next action
- Basic Four Engines education
- Basic Four Seasons education
- Useful [[CleaningOS AI]] access, focused on diagnosis, orientation, and getting moving
- Survival-level guidance and basic implementation help

Free does **not** include the complete core implementation roadmap for Stability, Scale, and
Harvest — that's [[CleaningOS Membership]], $47.

## Free CleaningOS AI

**Job: diagnose + get moving.** Tier summary — full definition lives in [[CleaningOS AI]].

It may: explain CleaningOS, explain the Four Engines, explain the Four Seasons, run or help
interpret [[Business GPS]], help clarify the member's next measurable goal, explain
Diagnostic results, help investigate a likely constraint, help choose a One Step, help with
basic Survival-level implementation, point toward free resources, explain Stability, Scale,
and Harvest at a high level.

It should be genuinely useful — not intentionally made dumb. The distinction between Free,
$47, and $297 is scope and job, not answer quality.

## Business GPS vs. CleaningOS AI

- **Business GPS** — the ongoing navigation system that determines what to work on next.
- **Business GPS Assessment** — an initial or periodic diagnostic use of Business GPS.
- **CleaningOS AI** — the coach/interface that can run Business GPS, then help the member
  execute the resulting One Step.

Business GPS is not a competing product to CleaningOS AI — it's one of the core systems
CleaningOS AI can use.
