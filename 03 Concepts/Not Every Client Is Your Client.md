# Not Every Client Is Your Client

> [!abstract] In one line
> Twenty clients at full price beats fifty at a discount — and the discounted fifty will cost you more than money.

## Definition

Some price gaps aren't objections to overcome. They're evidence the prospect belongs to a
different market. Letting them go is the correct move.

## Why It Matters

> "It's better to have no client than a very bad experience with a client."

When someone's last cleaner charged $90 and you quote $249, that is not a negotiation —
it's a different league. Chasing them costs you the rate, and then costs you again in
complaints, bad reviews, and refund requests.

**The arithmetic, from the workshop:** a Fort Worth owner averaged **$110 per clean**
because she discounted constantly. Three times the bookings, one third the revenue. More
cleaners to manage, more headaches, less profit.

> "Is it better to have 20 clients paying full price to make $10K, or 50 clients to make
> $10K, or 100 clients?"

## Symptoms

- Discounting to close
- Your average ticket is drifting down
- Your worst clients are the ones you gave the best price to
- More bookings, flat profit

## Common Mistakes

- Treating every price gap as an objection to handle
- Discounting to hit a booking count instead of a revenue number
- Measuring the business in clients rather than profit per clean

## Models

**Push back instead of defending.** When the gap is large:

> "That's a great price. Why are you even looking around?"

**The Craigslist redirect** — honest, and it reframes cheap as a choice with consequences:

> "There's people on Craigslist who probably won't pass a background check. There's
> cheaper options. But you're going to pay for a cheaper option."

**If budget is real**, don't cut the rate — cut the scope. See [[Drop Scope Not Price]].

## Checklist

- [ ] Ask what they were paying before
- [ ] If the gap is large, ask why they're looking at all
- [ ] Don't defend your price — dig into what failed last time
- [ ] Offer reduced scope, never a reduced rate
- [ ] Let them go if they're a different league
