# Client Expectation Setting

> [!abstract] In one line
> A message sent 24–48 hours before the first clean, covering prep, scope, limitations,
> payment, and cancellation policy, prevents most complaints before they can happen.

## Definition

A written message — sent after booking is confirmed, 24–48 hours before the
appointment — that tells a new client what to prepare, exactly what's included and
excluded, business limitations, payment timing, the cancellation policy, and what
quality assurance looks like. One of the eight essential SOPs named in [[SOPs]]'s
"minimum viable SOP library" ("new client intake and expectations"), and the first
concrete artifact filling that gap.

## Why It Matters

Not setting expectations early is one of the most common new-owner mistakes, and it's
directly upstream of complaints, refund requests, and bad reviews. A well-built
expectation message is described as one of the highest-ROI systems in the business —
capable of reducing issues by 70–80% on its own.

## Symptoms

- Clients say "I thought this was included" after the clean
- Refund or complaint volume feels disproportionate to actual service quality
- Clients are surprised by scope limitations, payment timing, or the cancellation policy
- No standard message goes out between booking and the first clean

## Common Mistakes

- Setting expectations reactively, after a complaint, instead of before the job
- Being vague about scope ("we clean the house") instead of explicit about what's
  included and excluded
- Sounding aggressive or legalistic rather than professional and warm
- Skipping the message for repeat/recurring clients where expectations still drift

## Models

**Six things every expectation message covers:**

1. **Preparation instructions** — clutter removal, add-on prep (e.g. apply oven
   cleaner beforehand), access.
2. **Scope of work** — explicitly what's included and what's not, to head off
   "I thought this was included."
3. **Limitations** — no heavy lifting, no ladder work, no blocked/inaccessible areas.
4. **Payment expectations** — when and how payment is processed.
5. **Cancellation policy** — required notice and fees, stated upfront. See
   [[Reschedule Not Cancel]] for the full policy and retention script.
6. **Quality assurance process** — a walkthrough happens, and re-cleans happen on the
   spot if something's missed.

**Timing:** send after the booking is confirmed, 24–48 hours before the appointment —
early enough to actually change client behavior (decluttering, prepping add-ons), late
enough to stay top of mind.

## Checklist

- [ ] Message sent 24–48 hours before every first-time appointment
- [ ] Preparation instructions included (clutter, add-on prep, access)
- [ ] Scope explicitly stated: included and excluded
- [ ] Limitations stated: no heavy lifting/ladders/blocked areas
- [ ] Payment timing and method explained
- [ ] Cancellation policy stated with required notice
- [ ] Walkthrough and on-the-spot re-clean guarantee mentioned
- [ ] Template customized with company name and checklist link

## Templates

**Client Expectation Email (subject: "Your Upcoming Cleaning – What to Expect"):**
> "Hi [Client Name], Welcome to [Your Company Name]! We're excited to take care of your
> home. To ensure everything goes smoothly and you get the best results, here are a few
> important details to review before your appointment: [prep instructions] · [what's
> included, with checklist link] · [what's not included] · [payment timing] ·
> [satisfaction guarantee / walkthrough] · [cancellation policy]. If you have any
> questions before your appointment, feel free to reach out anytime! Best regards,
> [Your Company Name]"
