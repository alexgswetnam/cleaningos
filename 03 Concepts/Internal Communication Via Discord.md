# Internal Communication Via Discord

> [!abstract] In one line
> One ops room for cleaner communication, reporting, and escalation — not texts, calls, and random DMs.

## Definition

Using Discord as the single internal communication platform for cleaners: one place for
day-to-day communication, one place for daily reporting, one place for issues and
escalations. Distinct from [[Managing A VA]]'s use of a Discord voice channel as "the
office" for VA presence — this is the cleaner-facing team-communication system, with its
own onboarding SOP.

## Why It Matters

When communication is scattered across texts, calls, and random DMs, visibility,
accountability, and consistency all leak away — nobody has a single place to check, and
nothing is searchable after the fact. A single "ops room" fixes the structural problem,
not just the symptom.

## Symptoms

- Cleaner communication spread across texts, calls, and multiple apps
- Missed messages because notifications weren't reliable on the previous platform
- No searchable history of past instructions or issues

## Common Mistakes

- Running communication through a channel with unreliable notifications (the specific
  reason this business moved off Google Voice)
- Not having a written onboarding SOP for a new communication platform
- Mixing every topic into one channel instead of organizing by purpose (job updates,
  supplies, scheduling, etc.)

## Models

**Why Discord over Google Voice.** Google Voice didn't reliably send message
notifications — important messages were being missed. Discord notifies instantly across
phone, tablet, and computer, and supports organizing conversations into channels by topic.
Google Voice is kept specifically for phone calls, since it's faster and more convenient
for that one purpose — the switch isn't total, it's purpose-specific.

**The onboarding SOP, step by step:** install the app → register or log in with a work
email/phone, using a clearly identifying username (e.g. "Maria C. – Cleaner") → join the
team server via an invite link → turn on notifications for all messages, both in-app and
at the phone's OS level, especially for Announcements and Daily Jobs channels → learn the
basics (send a message in a channel, send photos/videos via the "+" button, tag someone
with @Name, reply by holding a message) → keep Google Voice installed for calls only.

**Daily habit, not a one-time setup.** Check Discord daily, especially before and after
jobs. Missed messages stay visible by scrolling up — nothing is lost the way a text
thread can bury something.

**Reporting rolls up through the same system.** [[Zapier]] connects BookingKoala and
[[Harvest CRM]] data into the ops room so daily numbers — leads today, how cleans went,
new bookings — are visible without manually checking multiple tools.

## Checklist

- [ ] Move all cleaner communication to one platform with reliable notifications
- [ ] Write and hand every new cleaner the same onboarding SOP
- [ ] Set notifications to "All Messages" for at least Announcements and Daily Jobs
- [ ] Organize channels by topic rather than one undifferentiated feed
- [ ] Pipe key daily numbers (leads, cleans, bookings) into the same space via automation

## Templates

- Username convention: "[First name] [Last initial]. – Cleaner"
- "Keep both apps installed on your phone. Check Discord daily — especially before and
  after jobs."
