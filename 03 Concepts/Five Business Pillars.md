# Five Business Pillars

> [!abstract] In one line
> Acquire, Convert, Fulfill, Finances, Operate — the beginner's map, used before a founder is ready for the Four Engines diagnostic.

## Definition

A full five-part course framework — **Cleaning Biz 101**, not just an ad-hoc beginner
simplification — structured as **Acquire** (get leads), **Convert** (turn leads into
bookings), **Fulfill** (deliver the service), **Finances** (know the money), **Operate**
(the systems and people that make everything run smoothly). Confirmed by the course's own
per-pillar opening lessons: Acquire is defined as *"your lead engine — the stuff that
makes strangers become interested enough to raise their hand."* Convert: *"Acquire gets
people to raise their hand. Convert is what makes sure those raised hands actually turn
into booked jobs."* Fulfill: *"Acquire gets leads. Convert gets bookings. Fulfill is what
turns bookings into a smooth experience, happy clients, and clean operations."*

## Why It Matters

A brand-new owner who "sees the whole elephant" freezes rather than acting. A five-pillar
generic-business framework, taught before the cleaning-specific [[Four Engines]]
diagnostic, gives them a starting vocabulary and a concrete first assignment instead of an
overwhelming blank page.

## Symptoms

- A brand-new owner who doesn't know where to start and is paralyzed by the whole business
- No baseline vocabulary for talking about the business (e.g. not knowing what a CRM is)
- Trying to learn and fix everything simultaneously instead of one piece at a time

## Common Mistakes

- Trying to learn everything at once instead of going pillar by pillar
- Letting overwhelm sit unaddressed — Alex's read: it doesn't resolve itself, it can sit
  for years (Courtney's business was static for four years before she decided to grow)
- Treating this framework and [[Four Engines]] as interchangeable — they're related but
  not identical, and nothing in the source explains the exact mapping

## Models

**The five pillars, defined:**

1. **Acquire** — get leads, get people interested.
2. **Convert** — turn leads into bookings/sales.
3. **Fulfill** — deliver the service.
4. **Finances** — know the money: what's coming in, what's going out, what makes sense.
5. **Operate** — the glue: systems, SOPs, people, everything running smoothly without
   the owner personally holding it together.

**The Buffalo Wild Wings analogy**, used to make the pillars concrete for a non-cleaning
example: a sign out front and events (acquire), a menu designed to look great and push
desserts (convert), good food and atmosphere (fulfill), someone running the numbers in
the back (finances), and a GM, AGM, staff, and systems making it all run (operate).

**Eat the elephant one bite at a time.** Take one pillar, learn it, post what you learned
in the community (with journal notes), move to the next. The instruction is explicitly
paced — not "read everything this week."
