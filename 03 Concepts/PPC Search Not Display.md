# PPC Search Not Display

> [!abstract] In one line
> Google's setup wizard will put you on the display network, and it costs 4–6x per lead.

## Definition

Cleaning PPC belongs on **search** — people actively typing that they need a cleaner.
The **content display network** puts banner ads on random websites in front of people
who aren't looking for anything. Same budget, very different result.

## Why It Matters

Real numbers from the 2025-03-25 call:

| | Cost per lead |
|---|---|
| Properly built search campaign | ~$40 |
| Content display network | $200+ |

Alex tested it deliberately for a week: *"it went from being like $40 to upwards of $200
for just a lead."*

Rick spent **$1,700** and got 6–7 leads, roughly $1,000 in sales. *"That's not — it
wasn't worth it."*

## Symptoms

- Ad spend jumped and bookings didn't
- The setup asked you for videos and photos ← **this is the tell**
- Leads are low-intent or don't convert
- You can't attribute spend → leads → bookings

## Common Mistakes

- **Following Google's own setup recommendations.** Rick: *"All I did was get on there,
  start a campaign, go through its recommendations. It wanted three videos and a couple
  dozen pictures."* That's display, not search.
- **Using ChatGPT to build the campaign** without understanding campaign types. Rick did
  both. Neither produced a search campaign.
- **Taking calls from Google reps.** See below — this one has burned everyone on the call.
- Concluding "PPC doesn't work." Wrong conclusion. Alex: *"I don't look at it as PPC
  doesn't work. I look at it as the setup and the way that it was structured didn't work
  for you guys in your area with your offer."*

## Models

**The tell:** if the setup is asking for videos and images, you're building a display
campaign. Search ads are text.

**Google reps — hang up.**

> "If you guys ever get a call from Google and they tell you, hey, we have recommendations
> for your ad account, just hang up. It will ruin your ad account. I'm not kidding you."

Alex gets five a day. The incentive problem, in his words: *"Are they incentivized for you
to get good results, or are they incentivized for you to spend as much money as they can
get you to spend?"*

Melissa followed a verified Google rep's advice: *"I spent so much money. It's shameful."*
Alex: *"She blew up your account, too."* — *"Yes. Yes, she did."*

**Setups aren't portable.** Alex and Elijah can hand over their exact campaign and watch
it fail in a different market. Jack pays a different provider, has a completely different
structure, and gets ~$53 CAD per lead. *"If one way doesn't work, try another way."*

**A second setting that does the same damage: search network partners.**

From the 2025-02-03 call — Alex traced a high no-contact rate back to Google Ads' *search
network partners* option, which was generating unqualified leads that never answered the
phone. He turned it off. Track no-contact rate per channel and it shows up. See
[[Thumbtack]] for the metric.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm campaign type is **Search**, not Display / Performance Max
- [ ] Turn **off** search network partners
- [ ] If it asked for videos or images, rebuild it
- [ ] Never accept ad-account recommendations from a Google rep
- [ ] Track spend → leads → bookings so you can attribute
- [ ] Test one week on, one week off to isolate the effect (Rick's method)
