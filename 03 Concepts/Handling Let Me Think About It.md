# Handling Let Me Think About It

> [!abstract] In one line
> Don't accept the delay — find out what they're actually thinking about.

## Definition

The response to "I need to think about it" is a single question that surfaces the real
objection hiding behind the delay.

## Why It Matters

"Let me think about it" is not information. It's a polite exit. Every owner on the
2025-03-25 call named it as a deal they lose — and the reason they lose it is that they
accept it and move to follow-up, where the prospect goes quiet or books elsewhere.

> Melissa: *"when you try to follow up, they either go to somebody else or they just
> ignore you and you can't get anywhere."*

## Symptoms

- You say "sure, no problem, I'll follow up" and never hear back
- Your follow-up sequence is carrying deals your call should have closed
- You don't know why any given deal died

## Common Mistakes

- Accepting the delay and moving to follow-up
- Asking "what do you need to think about?" in a way that sounds defensive
- Dropping price before you know whether price is the problem

## Models

**The line:**

> "Sure, no worries. There's no pressure. What's your main concern you need to think about?"

**Then, if it turns out to be money — the money-aside separator:**

> "Money aside, do you feel like this could be the answer for you?"

This splits the fit objection from the price objection. If they say yes to fit, price is
the only thing left to solve. Alex uses this in another business on a $1,600/month offer,
the moment any resistance appears after the price drop.

## Checklist

- [ ] They say "I need to think about it"
- [ ] "Sure, no worries. What's your main concern you need to think about?"
- [ ] Listen — adjust tone to their personality, not the words
- [ ] If money: "Money aside, do you feel like this could be the answer for you?"
- [ ] If fit is confirmed, solve price
- [ ] Move to [[The Soft Close]]

## Templates

- "Sure, no worries. What's your main concern you need to think about?"
- "Absolutely, no worries. Of course you want to think about it. I don't want you to feel any kind of pressure at all. I'm just curious what's your main concern you need to think about?"
- "Money aside, do you feel like this could be the answer for you?"
