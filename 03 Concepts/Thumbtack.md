# Thumbtack

> [!abstract] In one line
> Profitable if you filter hard and call fast — expensive if you let it send you anything.

## Definition

Lead marketplace. Second-most-profitable channel in Alex's [[Channel Prioritization]]
ranking, largely because of the free-leads method.

## Why It Matters

Unfiltered, it will happily sell you one-bed one-bath one-time jobs at full price. Filtered
to recurring work with the price slider worked up from the bottom, it performs.

Alex's Q4: 90 leads, **51 of them free**, $15.64 cost per lead, 26% close, $58 CPA.

## Symptoms

- Spend spiking on leads that don't fit your business
- Leads that never answer
- Low close rate specifically from this channel

## Common Mistakes

- Not filtering out unprofitable job types. One member spent **$220 in a day on four bad
  leads** — 1-bed/1-baths and one-times.
- Starting the lead price slider at the top instead of working up from the bottom
- Not using the free-leads cycling method
- **Only texting.** Call, and call more than once.

## Models

**The follow-up sequence:**

1. **Message inside Thumbtack immediately** — they measure your response time
2. **Call.** Twice. Three times if it looks like a serious job (not a 1-bed/1-bath)
3. **Leave a voicemail**
4. **Text:** *"Hey, I just tried you. You just reached out. When's a better time to call?"*
5. Hand ongoing follow-up to the CRM

Alex on calling three times: *"I have no shame... you reached out to me and said you want
cleaning services."*

**If they push back on the calls:**

> "Am I bothering you? You just reached out to us and I wanted to get ahold of you."

And if they stay hostile — they probably weren't going to be a good client.

**No-contact rate is a metric.** Q4: 6 of 90 Thumbtack leads never responded; 5 of 36 PPC
leads never responded. A high number means something upstream is wrong — it's how Alex
found the Google Ads *search network partners* setting was generating junk. See
[[PPC Search Not Display]].

## Checklist

- [ ] Filter to recurring work
- [ ] Price slider from the bottom up
- [ ] Free-leads method enabled
- [ ] Message in-platform the moment a lead lands
- [ ] Call twice, three times if serious
- [ ] Voicemail, then text
- [ ] CRM takes over
- [ ] Track no-contact rate
