# What The Money Makes Possible

> [!abstract] In one line
> A revenue target that hasn't been converted into what it buys stops motivating anyone,
> including the Owner who set it.

## Definition

Revenue is a mechanism, not a destination. The practice is to take any number the Owner
names — users, clients, top line, one VA a quarter — and push it until it says what
becomes available, for the Owner, for the team, and for the Client.

This is the working form of the Mission in [[Philosophy]]: *money matters because it
creates options, but the final product is not money.*

## Why It Matters

An abstract number is easy to abandon. "Get to $25k" survives exactly as long as
motivation does. "$25k is the month I stop cleaning on Saturdays" survives longer, because
the Owner can feel the cost of missing it.

It also changes who the goal is for. A number stated only as the Owner's income is a goal
one person carries. The same number stated as what it funds for the team and the Clients
is a goal other people will help carry.

## Symptoms

- The Owner can state a revenue target but not what changes when they hit it
- Goals get quietly replaced each quarter without anyone noticing they were missed
- The team knows the target and is indifferent to it
- The Owner has hit a number they once wanted and feels nothing

## Common Mistakes

- **Stopping at the number.** "100 users this quarter" is specific and still says nothing
  about why anyone should care.
- **Naming only the Owner's upside.** True, and it gives the team no reason to pull.
- **Treating this as a substitute for the number.** It is the second half. The first half
  is still a specific figure with a date on it — see [[KPI Tracking Sheet]] and
  [[Marketing Math]].

That is the [[Owner Role Evolution]] mechanism stated from the inside: the hire is what lets him act
as the next role up.

## Models

**The cascade.** Run any target through four questions:

| Ask | Produces |
|---|---|
| What does this number buy **me**? | Time, options, a specific thing that changes |
| What does it buy my **team**? | Pay, hours, security, what their families get |
| What does it buy my **Clients**? | The outcome in [[Cleaning Is Holy Work]] |
| What becomes possible **after** that? | The reason the number was worth setting |

Stop when the answer is something a person outside the business would recognise as good.

## Checklist

- [ ] Write your current revenue target as a figure with a date
- [ ] Write the one thing in your week that changes when you hit it
- [ ] Write what it changes for one named person on your team
- [ ] Put both sentences where you'll see them — next to the number, not instead of it

## FAQ

**Q:** Isn't this just motivation?
**A:** Motivation is the effect. The mechanism is that a goal with a named consequence is
harder to quietly drop than a goal without one.

**Q:** Do I share this with my team?
**A:** The part that concerns them, yes. That's the half most Owners leave out.
