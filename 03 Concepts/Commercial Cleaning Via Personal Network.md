# Commercial Cleaning Via Personal Network

> [!abstract] In one line
> Commercial accounts came from someone inside the industry socializing the pitch, not from SEO.

## Definition

Landing commercial cleaning accounts (restaurants, bars) through a partner or connection
who already has credibility and relationships inside that industry, rather than through
paid or organic search marketing.

## Why It Matters

Residential lead-gen (GBP, PPC, LSA — see [[Channel Prioritization]]) doesn't translate
directly to commercial accounts. Jack's route into Toronto restaurant/bar cleaning came
entirely through his business partner's personal network in that industry, not a channel
this vault has otherwise documented.

## Symptoms

- Residential channels aren't producing commercial leads
- You (or a partner) have real credibility in a specific industry you haven't leveraged
  for cleaning accounts

## Models

**Socialize the pitch through someone with standing in that world.** Jack's partner is a
bartender; he closed a second restaurant account by pitching general managers directly,
using his own credibility in that industry:

> "I've worked in bars since I was 18. I know what the pain points are when you come in
> and open a restaurant and see something that's not done. Our company fixes that for
> your opening staff."

**The niche:** higher-end restaurants that pay for nightly cleaning rather than having
closing staff do it.

**Scaling it with cold email.** Alex recommended instantly.ai to extend the same idea past
personal network — it finds your ideal prospect, warms up an email domain, and runs a
drip campaign to bars/restaurants in the area. Roughly $50 to send a batch of outreach
emails; best case, new accounts.

## Checklist

- [ ] Identify anyone in your network with real standing inside a commercial vertical
      (hospitality, medical offices, etc.)
- [ ] Have them pitch using their own credibility, not a generic sales script
- [ ] Once the niche is proven, consider cold email (e.g. instantly.ai) to scale past
      personal contacts
