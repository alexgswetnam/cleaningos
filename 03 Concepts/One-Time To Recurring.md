# One-Time To Recurring

> [!abstract] In one line
> The percentage of one-time cleans that become recurring clients — where the profit actually lives.

## Definition

Conversion rate from a single booking into an ongoing schedule.

## Why It Matters

[[Client Lifetime Value]] depends entirely on it. A one-time client is worth one clean; a recurring client is worth twelve. Doubling this number changes the whole business without adding a single lead.

## Common Mistakes

- Not tracking it at all
- Treating a booking as the finish line rather than the start

## Models

One member: **70% conversion in December, 16% in January.** Same business, same offer, wildly different month. Alex's point: if January's number doubled to 30%, the following month looks completely different — and that is far cheaper than buying more leads.
