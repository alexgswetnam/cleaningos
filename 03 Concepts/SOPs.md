# SOPs

> [!abstract] In one line
> When this happens, we do this — written down after you've done it three times, not before.

## Definition

Standard Operating Procedure. A repeatable, documented answer to a recurring situation.
Checklist, step-by-step, or video.

## Why It Matters

> "SOPs = replacing yourself. Every SOP you create is a piece of yourself that someone
> else can now do."

They're what makes [[Four Roles|Owner]] possible, and they're what a buyer is actually
purchasing. See [[Business Valuation]].

## The Timing Rule

**You don't need 50 SOPs in Survival or Stability.**

> "Don't go build a library of SOPs when your bottleneck is leads."

SOPs become essential at **Scale** (a VA needs to follow them) and at **Harvest** (a buyer
needs to run the business without you). Building them earlier is the classic wrong-Engine
mistake — see [[Business GPS]].

## Symptoms

- A VA can't take a task because it only exists in your head
- The same situation gets handled differently every time
- You can't take a week off

## Common Mistakes

- **Building SOPs when your constraint is Leads.** The most common misallocation in the
  whole method.
- **Inventing them in advance.** Don't ChatGPT a lockout SOP on day one.
  > "Wait until you've had three lockouts, figure out what works, then document it."
- Writing documents nobody opens instead of checklists someone follows

## Models

**Start with the daily checklist.** What has to happen every day:

- [ ] Cleans are assigned
- [ ] First-time cleans from yesterday have been called
- [ ] Leads have been called and texted twice
- [ ] Reviews checked
- [ ] Tracking sheet updated — see [[KPI Tracking Sheet]]

**Then build from what you encounter.**

> "You're already doing the process. An SOP is just writing it down."

**The hiring SOP** — the worked example, kept in Notion:

Indeed post → screen applicants (video of what to look for, plus accept/message/reject
criteria) → schedule interview (three time slots, 24–72 hours out) → interview guide
(warmth, communication, professionalism, reliability, residential know-how, red flags) →
**same-day onboarding**: subcontractor agreement, BookingKoala provider account, cleaner
handbook covering client experience, photo guide, scheduling, supplies list, home
condition guide.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm Logistics is actually your constraint before building any
- [ ] Write the daily checklist first
- [ ] Log situations as they happen; document after the third occurrence
- [ ] Prefer checklists to prose
- [ ] Test it by handing it to someone else
