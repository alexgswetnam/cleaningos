# Cleaning Is Holy Work

> [!abstract] In one line
> You are not selling cleaning. You are selling a home someone can walk into and breathe.

## Definition

A reframe of what the service actually delivers. The transaction looks like labour sold by
the hour or the square foot. What the Client actually buys is the mental and emotional
capacity that a clean house gives back to them.

This matters to the Owner before it matters to the Client. An Owner who believes they run
a menial business sells like it, recruits like it, and quits like it.

## Why It Matters

Three things break without it.

**Sales.** An Owner who thinks they sell hours competes on hours, which means competing on
price. See [[Not Every Client Is Your Client]] and [[Drop Scope Not Price]].

**Labor.** Cleaners are asked to care about a job the Owner has privately decided is
beneath caring about. That gap is visible to them.

**Survival.** In the Season where the Owner is doing the cleaning themselves, the question
that ends businesses is not "is this profitable" — it's "is this worth my life." A reason
to keep going is load-bearing infrastructure, not decoration.

## Symptoms

- The Owner hedges when asked what they do for a living
- Marketing describes inputs — hours, rooms, checklists — and never an outcome
- Every sales conversation lands on price because nothing else has been offered
- Cleaners are managed as labour units and turn over accordingly
- The Owner is embarrassed to raise prices because "it's just cleaning"

## Common Mistakes

- **Selling the input.** Square footage, hours, and a checklist of tasks. Accurate, and it
  gives the Client nothing to value except the number at the bottom.
- **Reaching for the reframe only in marketing.** If the Owner doesn't believe it, it reads
  as copy. The belief is the product; the sentence is the presentation.
- **Confusing this with inspiration.** It does not replace [[Marketing Math]] or a working
  [[Leads Engine]]. A meaningful business that can't acquire clients profitably still fails.

## Models

The chain the Owner should be able to state without hesitating:

```
clean house
   → the Client stops carrying it
      → mental and emotional capacity returns
         → they are better with the people around them
```

The service is the first box. **The thing being bought is the third.**

## Checklist

- [ ] Write the one sentence you would say at a party when asked what you do — outcome, not input
- [ ] Read your current ad copy. Underline every line describing an input. Rewrite one as an outcome
- [ ] Say the outcome version out loud to your next three prospects and note where the conversation goes
- [ ] Tell your cleaners what the Client actually gets. Once, plainly, not as a speech

## FAQ

**Q:** Isn't this just a marketing angle?
**A:** It's a belief that produces a marketing angle. Written by someone who doesn't hold
it, it reads as copy — which is why it sits in Concepts and not in Marketing.

**Q:** Does this mean I charge more?
**A:** It means you have something to say other than the price. Whether that supports a
higher price is a [[Price Objection]] question.
