# Hiring SOP

> [!abstract] In one line
> Qualify, interview on a rubric, paper the hire before assigning work, then pair the
> first job with an experienced cleaner and evaluate before going solo.

## Definition

The screening-through-onboarding process once an applicant exists: qualify against
simple criteria, run a scored interview, decide, complete paperwork before any job
assignment, onboard with accounts and guides, pair the first job with an experienced
cleaner, and evaluate before scheduling independently. Distinct from [[Hiring Channels]]
(where applicants come from) and [[Automated Hiring Pipeline]] (the CRM automation that
fires emails/tasks as an applicant moves stages) — this is the actual qualification
rubric and onboarding sequence that runs inside those stages. Resolves the gap [[SOPs]]
names directly: *"Hiring SOP lives in Notion — not yet in the vault."*

## Why It Matters

Unstructured hiring produces hit-or-miss cleaners, messy onboarding, and mistakes on
jobs — all of which compound as a business scales past what one owner can personally
vet. A documented rubric and paperwork gate is what lets an owner or a VA run hiring
consistently instead of relying on gut feel.

## Symptoms

- Cleaners get assigned jobs before paperwork (agreement, W-9, background check) clears
- Interview quality varies by who's doing the interviewing
- No feedback loop after a new cleaner's first job
- Hiring decisions can't be explained or defended after the fact

## Common Mistakes

- Assigning jobs before paperwork clears
- Skipping the rubric and relying on gut feel
- No feedback loop after the first job — sending a new cleaner solo without evaluation

## Models

**Flow:** find applicants (job boards — Indeed, Nextdoor, Facebook groups; see
[[Hiring Channels]] for the fuller channel list and cold-calling method) → screen resumes
→ interview → decision → paperwork → onboarding → first-job pairing → evaluation.

**Screening criteria:** accept 6+ months residential cleaning experience, nearby
location, a complete resume, and a friendly tone; message if experience is unclear;
reject for no relevant experience, missing transport/contact info, or a hostile tone.

**Interview:** offer three time slots within 24–72 hours, via video or phone. Score 1–5
on a rubric: warmth/friendliness, communication clarity, professionalism (appearance,
punctuality), reliability (transportation, schedule honesty), residential cleaning
knowledge. Watch for red flags: lateness, aloofness, arguing, blaming previous clients.

**Decision → paperwork:** hire, decline, or waitlist. If hired, send the subcontractor
agreement, collect a W-9, run a background check — wait for everything to clear before
assigning any job. See [[Subcontractor Vs W-2]] for the classification this paperwork
has to match.

**Onboarding:** create system accounts (booking app, communication platform — see
[[Internal Communication Via Discord]]), and provide the [[Cleaner Handbook]].

**First job and evaluation:** pair the new cleaner with an experienced one, communicate
expectations to both, observe the first job. Call both the new cleaner and the paired
cleaner afterward for feedback before deciding on independent scheduling or added
support. See [[Testing A New Cleaner]] for a live version of this pairing step.

## Checklist

- [ ] Applicant screened against experience/location/tone criteria
- [ ] Interview scheduled within 24–72 hours, three slots offered
- [ ] Interview scored on the 5-point rubric across all five criteria
- [ ] Hire/decline/waitlist decision made and communicated
- [ ] Subcontractor agreement, W-9, and background check cleared *before* any job
      assignment
- [ ] System accounts created; handbook and guides provided
- [ ] First job paired with an experienced cleaner
- [ ] Feedback collected from both cleaners post-job before independent scheduling
