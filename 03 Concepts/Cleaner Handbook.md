# Cleaner Handbook

> [!abstract] In one line
> A written onboarding packet that sets standards from day one — client-experience
> protocol, photo standards, and the supply list, given to every cleaner before their
> first job.

## Definition

An onboarding document given to new cleaners covering how the company operates, what's
expected of them, and how the work should be done. Named by two members on the
2026-02-20 call before any actual content existed in the vault; a third source now
supplies the actual contents that call could only gesture at — the on-site client
experience protocol, before/after photo standards, and the preferred supply list. Distinct
from [[Cleaner Availability System]] (setting weekly availability) and
[[Cleaner Pay Structure]] (compensation) — this is specifically what a cleaner does on
site, and what they document while there.

## Why It Matters

A cleaner who wasn't handed a handbook improvises client interaction, photo
documentation, and even which supplies to bring — and every improvisation is a place
quality and consistency can drift between cleaners. The handbook is what lets a new hire
represent the company correctly on day one, not just eventually.

## Symptoms

- New cleaners unsure how to open a walkthrough or handle a client request on site
- Photo documentation inconsistent across cleaners — some skip it, angles don't match
- Cleaners showing up without the right supplies, guessing what's needed
- Client complaints that trace back to a cleaner not knowing a stated boundary (no client
  supplies, no personal calls on site, etc.)

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming client-facing conduct is common sense instead of writing it down
- Treating photos as optional instead of a standard part of every first-time clean
- Not giving cleaners a supply checklist, so kit quality varies person to person

## Models

**On-site flow, start to finish:**

1. **Before arrival** — double-check the schedule, notify the team of any chance of
   being late, mark "on the way" in [[BookingKoala]], read the client notes (pets,
   preferences), call the team about access issues (parking, gate codes) before arriving.
2. **Arrival** — bring all supplies to the door, knock then ring if no answer within 30
   seconds, call the team if there's no response after 3 minutes.
3. **Walkthrough** — greet the client warmly and offer to walk the home together; ask
   about preferences and areas to avoid; build rapport with a genuine compliment; for
   larger issues, set expectations on the spot and text the team a photo before starting.
4. **Photos** — 2–3 wide "before" shots per room once cleaning begins (not during the
   walkthrough), focused on visibly dirty areas; "after" shots at the final walkthrough,
   matching before angles as closely as possible, surfaces dry/wiped/staged. Photograph
   anything damaged or impossible to clean. Natural light, landscape, steady, camera
   stepped back to fit the room, no personal clutter in frame. Upload everything to
   BookingKoala job media.
5. **During the clean** — work efficiently and quietly, no loud music or speakerphone,
   respect pets and anyone working from home, follow BookingKoala notes when unsure.
6. **Final walkthrough & goodbye** — walk the finished areas with the client if they're
   home, invite feedback ("I'd love to touch up anything you notice — it's no problem at
   all!"), mention a review bonus if applicable; if the client isn't home, text the team
   the clean is complete and send after photos.

**The do-not-do list**, stated plainly so it isn't left to judgment: don't ask how long
you're supposed to stay (check BookingKoala or call); don't use client supplies unless
explicitly allowed; no eating, drinking (besides water), smoking, or vaping on site; no
guests or unapproved team members; no personal calls or loud phone conversations; don't
leave early without confirming the clean is complete and client-approved.

**The supply kit.** General: color-coded microfiber cloths, dusting tools, all-purpose /
glass / disinfectant / furniture cleaners, vacuum with attachments, washable-head mop,
broom and dustpan, bucket, step stool, trash bags, laundry basket. Kitchen: degreaser,
stainless steel and countertop cleaners, dish soap, non-scratch sponges, detailing
brushes, oven and fridge cleaners, polishing cloths. Bathroom: tile/grout cleaner, toilet
cleaner plus dedicated brush, shower and mildew cleaner, scrub brushes, rubber gloves.
Floors: vacuum, hardwood/tile cleaners, mop solution. Optional: handheld vacuum, supply
caddy, spray bottles, mask, knee pads, sticky roller for blinds/fabric.

## Checklist

- [ ] Schedule double-checked and BookingKoala marked "on the way" before arrival
- [ ] Client notes read for pets/preferences before knocking
- [ ] Supplies at the door before starting; knock, then ring after 30 seconds
- [ ] Walkthrough offered; preferences and no-go areas confirmed
- [ ] Before photos taken once cleaning begins (2–3 wide shots/room); after photos at
      final walkthrough, matching angles
- [ ] Do-not-do list followed: no client supplies without permission, no food/drink/
      smoking/vaping, no guests, no personal calls
- [ ] Final walkthrough done; feedback invited if client is home, text + after-photos
      sent if not
- [ ] All photos uploaded to BookingKoala job media
