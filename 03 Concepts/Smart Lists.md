# Smart Lists

> [!abstract] In one line
> A saved filter that keeps refilling itself as contacts start matching it.

## Definition

Dynamic contact lists in Harvest CRM. You define filters; membership updates continuously as contacts meet or stop meeting them.

## Why It Matters

A static export is stale the day you make it. A smart list is always current, so campaigns aimed at a segment keep finding new people in that segment without you rebuilding anything.

## Student Examples

**Alex's example filter:** cold tag + long-term follow-up stage + contact created at least three months ago. That list stays populated on its own.

**A second worked example, from [[Reactivating Past Clients]]'s SMS reactivation system:**
three segments defined as ongoing filters rather than one-time exports — clients eligible
for a last-minute-opening fill (haven't booked recently, open slot within 72 hours),
"warm" lapsed clients (booked 2–6 months ago, no outreach in 30 days), and "full"
reactivation candidates (booked 6+ months ago, not opted out). Each stays current the
same way Alex's cold-tag list does — membership updates as contacts age into or out of
each window, without manual rebuilding.
