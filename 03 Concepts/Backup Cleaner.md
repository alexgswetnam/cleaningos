# Backup Cleaner

> [!abstract] In one line
> Even with no growth plans, one cleaner leaving should not be able to take you down.

## Definition

At least one more cleaner than the schedule strictly requires, working alongside the team, so a departure or a sick day is absorbed rather than catastrophic.

## Why It Matters

Alex, to Courtney: *"if one of your girls just had to go — something happened, she had to move — you're in big trouble. Even if you weren't planning to scale, you still would want to have at least one more kind of backup working alongside people in case someone left or two people left."* It also underwrites every high-pressure job — see [[Airbnb Clients]].
