# Referral Program

> [!abstract] In one line
> Before you can pay for leads, call the clients you already have and ask directly — it's free and it leverages trust you've already earned.

## Definition

Calling existing clients to check in and explicitly ask for referrals, with a small
mutual discount attached, as a lead source that requires no ad spend and no new channel
setup.

## Why It Matters

It's the fastest available growth lever for an owner who can't or hasn't started paid
marketing yet — the trust is already built, the only missing step is asking directly
instead of hoping referrals happen on their own.

## Symptoms

- No paid marketing running yet, and no organic system either
- Happy clients who've never been asked for a referral
- Growth stalled despite good client relationships

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming happy clients will refer people on their own without being asked
- Treating this as beneath a "real" marketing strategy instead of using it alongside one

## Models

**Call, check in, ask directly — with an incentive attached:**

> "I'm trying to take on more clients. I'm doing a referral thing — if you refer someone,
> you both get $30–$40 off your next clean."

Simple, immediate, and it costs nothing until it produces a booking.

## Checklist

- [ ] Call existing clients to check in (not just to ask for a referral cold)
- [ ] Ask directly — don't wait for it to happen organically
- [ ] Offer a mutual discount ($30–40 range) so both sides have a reason to act
- [ ] Use this alongside paid channels, not instead of them once you're ready to add one
