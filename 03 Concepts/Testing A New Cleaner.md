# Testing A New Cleaner

> [!abstract] In one line
> Partner them with your best cleaner for two or three cleans, then ask your best cleaner.

## Definition

Before a new cleaner touches a paying client from marketing, they run two or three cleans partnered with an existing cleaner. Your best cleaners give the verdict.

## Why It Matters

It is the answer to the fear that blocks hiring. You are not gambling your reviews on someone unproven — you are testing them behind your best people first. See [[Reviews As Asset Not Identity]].
