# The Walkthrough

> [!abstract] In one line
> A missed room almost always means nobody walked the house before leaving.

## Definition

Cleaners check every room together before they leave. Ideally the homeowner walks it too.

## Why It Matters

It is the single cheapest prevention in the whole business. It costs three minutes and it
removes most of the input to [[Quality Complaints]] — the complaint, the return trip, the
discount, and the pay adjustment that follows.

> "A missed room almost always means no walkthrough happened."

## The Practice

Before leaving, the cleaners go room to room together:

> "Did you do that room? Did you do that room?"

Then, where possible, the homeowner does their own pass. A client who has walked the house
and said it looks good is far less likely to complain afterwards — and if they spot
something, it gets fixed in the moment instead of becoming a phone call.

## Symptoms

- Complaints about entire rooms, not details
- Return trips
- Cleaners surprised by the complaint

## Common Mistakes

- Treating it as optional when running late — which is exactly when it's needed
- One cleaner walking alone
- Skipping the homeowner pass because it feels awkward

## Checklist

- [ ] Both cleaners walk every room together before packing up
- [ ] Say the rooms out loud
- [ ] Offer the homeowner a walkthrough if they're there
- [ ] Fix anything found before leaving
