# Call Breakdowns

> [!abstract] In one line
> Post the call you lost, not the one you won.

## Definition

A coaching mechanism: a student submits a recorded sales call they struggled with or lost,
and Alex and Elijah break it down with specific feedback on what could have gone
differently.

## Why It Matters

It's the only reliable way to fix a sales problem, and a sales problem is the harder half
of [[Leads Problem Or Sales Problem]] — the half that can't be copied from someone else's
account.

Reviewing your own lost call surfaces things no amount of listening to good calls will.
Jack's own diagnosis after re-listening: *"I forgot to go into why she's looking to
switch. Listening back to my call, I could have really explored that a lot more."*

## The Ask

> "Pick a call you struggled with or lost. Post it in the chat. We'll break it down."

The instruction is specifically the **lost** call. Reviewing wins is comfortable and
teaches less.

## Common Mistakes

- Submitting your best call
- Not recording calls at all
- Taking the feedback as a verdict rather than a rep

## Student Examples

**Rick and Melissa** — had a call broken down previously. Melissa was *"stoked"* about the
feedback.

**Jack** — posted a call covering the shopping-around objection.

## Checklist

- [ ] Record calls
- [ ] Pick one you lost
- [ ] Post it
- [ ] Apply one change on the next call, not five
