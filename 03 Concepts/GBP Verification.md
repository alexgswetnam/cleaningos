# GBP Verification

> [!abstract] In one line
> If Google denies a pre-recorded verification video, request a live video call — it works.

## Definition

The process of verifying a Google Business Profile (GBP) so it can rank and show reviews.
Distinct from [[Google Business Profile Naming]], which covers how to *name* the profile —
this page covers how to get it *verified* in the first place, including what to do when
Google denies the standard pre-recorded verification attempt.

## Why It Matters

Verification is a gate: an unverified GBP won't fully rank or display, no matter how good
the naming or the review count. Courtney's profile was denied on the standard
pre-recorded video method; requesting a live video call instead got her approved within
24 hours.

## Symptoms

- GBP verification denied after submitting a pre-recorded video
- Profile isn't showing in local search despite reviews and activity

## Common Mistakes

- Giving up after one denial instead of escalating to a live call
- Assuming a denial means something is wrong with the business itself

## Models

**If pre-recorded verification is denied, request a live video call with Google.** This
is a distinct, escalated path — not a resubmission of the same recorded video. Courtney's
profile was denied on the pre-recorded method, requested the live call instead, and was
approved within 24 hours.

**What the live video call actually asks for.** Before the call, the business name and
suite number need to be physically on the office door, matching the GBP name exactly. On
the call, Google asks you to show three things in sequence: the office door with the
business name visible, that you have access to the space (open the door), and the
interior of the office itself. Google screenshots each step. The whole call runs about
five minutes.

**A virtual office or coworking space is the standard address source, and it has to be
the right kind.** A P.O. box will get the profile suspended — it has to be a real address
with a physical presence you can show on camera. Call ahead and confirm the space
explicitly allows GBP verification before signing up for a virtual mail/business address
plan (roughly $70/month). See [[Google Business Profile Naming]] for why the address
itself, not just the verification method, is one of the two highest-leverage GBP moves.

## Checklist

- [ ] Submit standard pre-recorded video verification first
- [ ] If denied, request a live video call verification instead of resubmitting
- [ ] Expect approval within roughly 24 hours of the live call
- [ ] Put the business name + suite number on the office door before the call, matching the GBP name exactly
- [ ] Confirm the address source is a real virtual/coworking office, not a P.O. box
- [ ] Call ahead to confirm the space allows GBP verification video calls
- [ ] On the call: show the door, show access, show the interior
