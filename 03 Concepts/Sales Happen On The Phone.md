# Sales Happen On The Phone

> [!abstract] In one line
> The call is a conversation where booking is the obvious next step — not a script you perform.

## Definition

The frame for a sales call: rapport first, understand the real need, guide naturally to
the booking. Phone calls with genuine human connection close far better than text, forms,
or instant quotes.

## Why It Matters

Jack closed **7 of his last 8 calls** with this approach. Not a rigid script —
conversational and relationship-driven.

The elements:

- **Build rapport quickly** — ask about them, their home, their situation
- **Understand what they actually need**, not just what they asked for
- **Guide naturally toward booking** so it feels like the obvious next step
- **Be genuinely enthusiastic about helping**

> He's not "selling." He's having a conversation where booking is the natural outcome.

## How This Fits With Word Tracks

These look contradictory. They aren't, and the distinction matters:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The call** | Conversational. No script. [[Sales Happen On The Phone]] |
| **The pressure points** | Exact lines, known cold. [[Word Tracks]] |

Alex told Courtney she engages well and *still* needs word tracks. Both were true. The
conversation is the frame; the word track is what you reach for when an objection lands,
precisely so you can stay conversational instead of scrambling.

> A word track exists so you can keep listening. It's not the opposite of a conversation —
> it's what protects one.

## Symptoms

- Quoting by text or form instead of getting them on the phone
- Calls that feel like an interrogation of requirements
- High lead volume, low close rate — see [[Leads Problem Or Sales Problem]]

## Common Mistakes

- Instant-quoting instead of calling
- Running a rigid script end to end
- Talking more than they do

## Checklist

- [ ] Get them on the phone
- [ ] Rapport before requirements
- [ ] Ask why they're looking — see [[Price Objection]]
- [ ] Listen for the real need
- [ ] Reach for a [[Word Tracks|word track]] only when an objection lands
- [ ] Close with [[The Soft Close]]
