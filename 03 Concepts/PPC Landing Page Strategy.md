# PPC Landing Page Strategy

> [!abstract] In one line
> Send paid traffic to your real website, not a squeeze page — the SEO benefit compounds.

## Definition

Where Google Ads traffic lands, and why it isn't a dedicated landing page.

## Why It Matters

Two reasons. The site already converts. And the paid traffic feeds organic ranking — Google sees a legitimate, well-trafficked site, ranks it higher, and organic leads (which are free and close best) increase. A squeeze page on a separate domain gives none of that back.

## Common Mistakes

- Building a squeeze page on a separate domain
- Optimizing the ad and ignoring the page it lands on

## Models

> "Every single one of our PPC ones are going to our site. Our site is ranking higher and benefiting from that rather than sending them to a separate domain."

The loop: paid traffic → SEO authority → more organic → lower average cost per lead → see [[Channel Prioritization]].

Visitors often spend 10–30 minutes on the site before calling, confirmed through session recordings. By the time they call, they've closed themselves.
