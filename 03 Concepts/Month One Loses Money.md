# Month One Loses Money

> [!abstract] In one line
> Acquiring a client costs more than the first clean pays — that's the normal pattern, not a broken funnel.

## Definition

New-client acquisition is usually unprofitable in the month it happens. The profit shows
up over the following months as those clients recur.

## Why It Matters

This is the moment most owners quit on marketing. They spend, they see the first month's
P&L, they panic and turn it off — right before the recurring revenue arrives.

## Models

**Alex's own first four months, verbatim:**

> "Our first month we spent $4,000 in marketing and we brought in like eight grand in
> business. And just like you said, we lost money because we had to pay cleaners."

> "But then month three, we spent no dollars on marketing and we had like $4,000 in
> bookings. Then the next month we broke even for the business and we were profitable."

| Month | Marketing | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $4,000 | ~$6–8K revenue *(contested, see below)*, lost money after cleaners |
| 2 | $3,000 | still lost money |
| 3 | $0 | ~$4,000 in bookings, all recurring |
| 4 | — | breakeven, then profitable |

**Month three is the point.** Zero marketing, four thousand dollars in bookings. That money
was bought in months one and two.

## Symptoms

- First month of spend looks like a disaster
- You want to turn marketing off
- You're judging a channel on its first 30 days

## Common Mistakes

- Killing a channel before recurring revenue arrives
- **Pocketing early profit instead of reinvesting.** Alex, on the first four to five
  months: *"every dollar we could put to marketing, we put to marketing."* And:
  > "I'm not looking to pocket $400 a month and go, look at my profit. I want to put that
  > right back into marketing so that three months from now it's a much bigger amount of money."
- Measuring acquisition against one clean instead of [[Client Lifetime Value]]

## Checklist

- [ ] Expect month one to lose money
- [ ] Track which month's spend produced which month's recurring revenue
- [ ] Judge a channel at 3+ months, not 30 days
- [ ] Reinvest early profit during the growth phase

## FAQ

**Q:** How do I know it's the normal pattern and not a bad channel?
**A:** [[Marketing Math]]. If CPA is sane and clients are recurring, it's the pattern. If
CPA is absurd or nobody recurs, it's the channel.
