# Review Response Scripts

> [!abstract] In one line
> Reply to every review within 12 hours — the reply is public, and it's read by the next
> hundred people who haven't booked yet, not just the reviewer.

## Definition

The mechanical scripts for replying to Google reviews — positive and negative — as
distinct from [[Reviews As Asset Not Identity]] (the owner's emotional relationship to
the star rating, not what to type) and [[Post-Clean Review Script]] (the phone call that
generates the review in the first place). This is what to write once a review exists.

## Why It Matters

Every review response is public. A calm, consistent negative-review reply demonstrates
professionalism to everyone who reads it later, not just the unhappy client — and a
resolved-looking thread can convert as well as a five-star review. A warm positive-review
reply costs nothing and reinforces the brand's mission on every visit to the profile.

## Symptoms

- Negative reviews sit unanswered for days
- Responses vary in tone depending on who's angry that day
- Positive reviews get no reply at all, wasting a free piece of public brand copy
- A negative review gets an emotional, defensive response instead of a template

## Common Mistakes

- Responding defensively or arguing with the reviewer
- Accusing an unverified reviewer of lying, even when they likely have the wrong business
- Sharing private client details in a public reply
- Offering a public discount or using emojis in a serious complaint response
- Letting a review sit more than 12 hours before any reply

## Models

**Daily monitoring, then branch by review type.** Check Google Business Profile reviews
at least once a day. For anything 1–3 stars, first categorize the reviewer:

- **Unverified** — name/email/phone doesn't match any booking on file. Respond politely,
  don't accuse them of lying, ask them to reach out privately, and document it internally.
- **Verified** — a real client with a real booking. Pull the booking details (cleaner,
  service type, notes/photos) before choosing a response template.

**Tone rules, all responses:** professional, calm, apologetic where warranted, never
defensive, no emojis, no public discounts, never share private booking details publicly.

**Escalate instead of replying solo when:** the claim involves theft, damage, or a safety
issue; the client is requesting a refund beyond your approval level; the review is
attracting outside attention; or there's a legal threat in the text.

## Checklist

- [ ] Check GBP reviews at least once daily
- [ ] Flag any 1–3 star review for same-day response
- [ ] Categorize: verified client or unverified
- [ ] Pull booking details before responding to a verified complaint
- [ ] Use the matching template — apology+resolution, already-resolved, or
      incomplete-details
- [ ] Never argue, never share private details, no public discounts
- [ ] Escalate theft/safety/legal/viral cases to leadership instead of replying solo
- [ ] Log every response internally: date, reviewer, category, complaint summary, action,
      resolution
- [ ] For positive reviews: thank the client by name, name their city, restate the
      mission/guarantee, close warmly

## Templates

**Negative — unverified reviewer:**
> "Hi there! We take all feedback seriously, but we're unable to find your name or
> booking details in our system. We want to make sure this concern is addressed
> correctly. Please reach out to us at [PHONE NUMBER] so we can help. Thank you, and
> we're happy to look into this right away."

**Negative — verified, standard apology + resolution:**
> "Hi [Name], thank you for your feedback. We're very sorry to hear about your
> experience — this is not the level of service we aim to provide. We have reached out
> privately to learn more and make this right. We appreciate the opportunity to
> improve."

**Negative — verified, already resolved:**
> "Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. We sincerely apologize for the issues
> you experienced. We're glad we were able to speak with you and resolve this together.
> Thank you for giving us the chance to make it right."

**Positive:**
> "Hey! Thank you for the five-star review and for trusting us as your maid service in
> [CITY]! We're thrilled to hear that our home cleaning service made a difference for
> you. Our mission is to provide [reliable, detail-oriented] house cleaning services in
> [CITY/COUNTY], with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. We're glad you chose us as your
> house cleaners :) Blessings and thanks again, The Team"

## FAQ

**Q:** What if a review contains profanity, spam, or is clearly not from a real client?
**A:** Flag it for removal only if it meets Google's actual removal criteria — not a real
client, profanity/hate speech, or irrelevant/spam content. Otherwise, respond; don't
just report and wait.
