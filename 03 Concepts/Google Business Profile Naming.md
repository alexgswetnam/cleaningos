# Google Business Profile Naming

> [!abstract] In one line
> Put the service and city in the GBP name — and make your signage match it exactly.

## Definition

Naming convention for the Google Business Profile: brand, divider, service, city.

## Why It Matters

It is what the profile ranks for. The format is `Business Name | House Cleaning Service [City]` — choose the city you most want clients from.

## Common Mistakes

- Editing the name during video verification. You cannot change it before the check — only after. On a live call the reviewer can edit it with you in real time.
- Signage that does not match the profile name
