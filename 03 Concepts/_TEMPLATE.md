## Template Rules

**Keep every heading, even when empty.** Write `*None yet.*` underneath rather than
deleting the section.

This is deliberate. An empty `Student Examples` section on a Canonical page is
information: it says you're teaching something you have no proof for. An empty `FAQ`
says you haven't listened to enough sales calls about it. Deleted sections hide those
gaps; empty ones surface them, and `/lint-vault` counts them to tell you what to work on.

The template is a diagnostic instrument, not a form to fill in.
