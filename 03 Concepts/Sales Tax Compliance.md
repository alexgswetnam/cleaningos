# Sales Tax Compliance

> [!abstract] In one line
> Check your state/province's sales tax rules now — forgiveness programs exist, but only if you go find them.

## Definition

The owner's obligation to collect and remit sales tax, and the discovery that this is
easy to miss entirely — not a marketing or operations concept, but a real financial
exposure that surfaced independently for two different members. Not owned by any single
[[Four Engines|Engine]]: [[Four Engines]] treats finance as *"the health of all four, not
a separate production Engine,"* and this is exactly that kind of cross-cutting item.

## Why It Matters

Sales tax obligations don't announce themselves. Two members independently discovered
they owed back sales tax they hadn't known about — one for six months of not collecting
at all, then collecting without remitting; another dating back to 2022. Neither had been
told to check this by any course or coach, which is itself the point: it's easy for a
purely marketing/operations-focused education to leave a real compliance gap unaddressed.

## Symptoms

- Never having explicitly checked your state or province's sales tax requirements for a
  cleaning service business
- Collecting sales tax but not confirming it's actually been remitted monthly
- Assuming a paid course or coach would have flagged this if it mattered

## Common Mistakes

- Not checking sales tax obligations at all when starting the business
- Collecting tax from clients without remitting it — this is worse than never collecting,
  since the money was taken and not passed on
- Assuming back taxes automatically come with penalties, without checking whether a
  forgiveness program exists first

## Models

**Two real cases, both resolved without penalty.** One member's business didn't collect
sales tax for the first six months, then collected but didn't remit it monthly; a
forgiveness program allowed repayment of what was actually collected, with no interest or
fees. Another member owed back to 2022, again with no penalties, but still had to repay
what was owed.

> "I spent six grand on a course, and nobody told me to go do sales tax. That's
> ridiculous."

**The action, not the anxiety.** The lesson isn't "panic about compliance" — it's "check
now, and look for a forgiveness program before assuming the worst." Both real cases in the
source resolved with repayment only, no punitive cost, because the members went and asked.

## Checklist

- [ ] Check your state/province's sales tax requirements for cleaning services specifically
- [ ] Confirm collected tax is actually being remitted, not just collected
- [ ] If behind, search for a forgiveness/amnesty program before assuming penalties apply
- [ ] Don't treat "nobody told me" as a reason to keep not checking
