# BookingKoala

> [!abstract] In one line
> The booking and provider-management software in the stack — mentioned in two sources, explained in neither.

## Definition

Booking software used alongside [[Harvest CRM]]. Clients book through it; cleaners get a
**provider account** in it during onboarding.

> [!question] Stub — the open question is why there are two tools
> Both sources mention BookingKoala in passing. Neither explains what it does that
> [[Harvest CRM]] doesn't, which is the first thing a student will ask when told to pay for
> both. That question is the reason this page exists.

## Why It Matters

*Not yet documented.* What's known:

- A cleaner's **provider account** is created during same-day onboarding, per the hiring
  SOP — see [[SOPs]]
- A **new booking** is the trigger for the pipeline-update zap — see [[Zapier]]

So it sits at the intersection of Labor and Logistics: it's where cleaners exist as
schedulable resources and where client bookings originate.
