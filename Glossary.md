# Glossary

Terms with their own page link there rather than being defined twice. This page is an
index, not a second home for definitions.

> [!info] This page is deliberately outside the Canon
> Moved out of `02 Canon/` on 2026-08-06 on Alex's instruction, following the
> [[Alex Voice]] precedent.
>
> **It isn't governing.** Canon is locked because changing it silently changes everything
> downstream — true of [[Five Laws]], [[Philosophy]], [[Four Seasons]], which are beliefs
> and taxonomy. This page is an *index*. Every real definition lives on a concept page;
> an entry here is a pointer to one. Adding a pointer can't change what CleaningOS
> believes.
>
> **It has to move at the speed of ingestion, and Canon can't.** Every ingest run
> introduces terms. Under the lock, `/ingest` Step 6 ordered agents to do something §II
> forbade, so in practice the Glossary just fell behind — one run on 2026-08-06 produced
> 19 new concepts and zero glossary entries.
>
> Unlike [[Alex Voice]] this file is **not portable** — it's CleaningOS vocabulary, not
> Alex's. That's why it sits at the vault root with the other reference documents rather
> than in its own unnumbered folder.

> [!abstract] Write policy
> | What | Who may write | Rule |
> |---|---|---|
> | **A term whose concept page already exists** | Agents, freely | Append a row. The definition must be a one-line *summary* of the concept page, with a `[[link]]` to it. |
> | **A term with no concept page** | Nobody — yet | If it needs defining, it's a concept. Create the page first, then add the pointer. This is Rule 1 doing its job. |
> | **Changing an existing definition** | Agents may propose | If a row contradicts its concept page, the page wins. Fix the page, then the row. |
> | **Removing a term, restructuring sections** | Alex | |
>
> **The one hard rule: never let a row become the only place a term is defined.** That's
> how Rule 2 dies quietly — a definition drifting on an index nobody thinks of as content.
> Nothing here needs a Canon proposal any more.
